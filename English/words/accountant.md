# accountant
?
# Meaning:
- A person whose job is to keep, check, or inspect financial records and accounts.
- Someone trained to prepare and analyze financial information for individuals or companies.
# Usage:
- Used in professional, business, and financial contexts.
- Can refer to someone working in a company, for clients, or independently.
# Example:
- _She hired an accountant to help with her taxes._
- _The accountant found several errors in the company's books._
