# hypocrisy
?
# Meaning:
- The practice of claiming to have moral standards or beliefs to which one's own behavior does not conform.
- Pretending to be something one is not, or saying one thing and doing another.
# Usage:
- Used to describe a person's actions or words that contradict their stated values or beliefs.
- Often used in moral, political, or social criticism.
# Example:
- _His hypocrisy was obvious: he preached about honesty but lied constantly._
- _She accused him of hypocrisy for criticizing her spending while he bought luxury cars._
# Russian:
- Лицемерие, ханжество, двуличие.
- Происходит от греческого hypokrisis (актёрство); означает притворство, несоответствие слов и дел.
