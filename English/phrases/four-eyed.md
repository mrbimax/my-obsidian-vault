# four-eyed
?
# Meaning:
- Informal, often derogatory slang meaning “wearing glasses.”
- Literally “having four eyes,” but used to mock someone whose glasses look like two extra eyes.
# Usage:
- Used as an insult or teasing, especially by children or bullies; can be offensive.
- Also used literally, but that sense is rare in everyday speech.
# Example:
- _The kids at school called him four-eyed because of his thick glasses._
- _She got tired of being called four-eyed and started wearing contacts._
# Russian:
- Очкастый, четырёхглазый (оскорбительно).
- Буквально: «с четырьмя глазами»; намёк на то, что очки выглядят как ещё одна пара глаз.
