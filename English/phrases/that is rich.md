# that is rich
?
# Meaning:
- A sarcastic reply meaning that what someone just said is hypocritical, absurd, or hard to believe.
- It often implies “you are in no position to say that.”
# Usage:
- Informal and mocking; used as a reaction to criticism or a statement that seems hypocritical.
- Tone can range from amused disbelief to sharp sarcasm.
# Example:
- _You're calling me lazy? That is rich, considering you haven't done anything all day._
- _He says I waste money? That is rich, coming from someone who just bought a third car._
# Russian:
- Ну это сильно! / Хорошенькое дело! / Это называется с больной головы на здоровую.
- Буквально: «это богато»; здесь rich означает не «богатый», а «уморительно нелепый, лицемерный».
