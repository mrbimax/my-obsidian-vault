# accountant feller
?
# Meaning:
- Informal or dialectal way of saying “an accountant guy” or “a man who is an accountant.”
- “Feller” is a colloquial variant of “fellow,” meaning a man or guy.
# Usage:
- Informal, regional, or old-fashioned. Common in rural or working-class speech; can sound rustic or humorous in modern use.
- Not standard in formal writing.
# Example:
- _My uncle's an accountant feller — does all the books for the local farms._
- _She married some accountant feller from up north._
# Russian:
- Мужик-бухгалтер / бухгалтер, мужик.
- Feller = просторечное «мужик, парень»; accountant feller = «бухгалтер-мужик» или «мужик, который работает бухгалтером».
