# to bump someone
?
# Meaning:
- To hit or knock against someone, usually accidentally but sometimes deliberately.
- To push or jostle someone in a crowd.
# Usage:
- Common in everyday speech for accidental physical contact.
- Often used with into (bump into someone) meaning to meet by chance, but without into it means to physically hit.
# Example:
- _I accidentally bumped someone in the crowded subway and apologized._
- _He bumped me with his shoulder as he walked past._
