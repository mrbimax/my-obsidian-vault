# to bump
?
# Meaning:
- To hit or knock against someone or something, often lightly or accidentally.
- To move with a jolt or bounce.
# Usage:
- Common in everyday speech for accidental contact or impact.
- Can be used literally (bump your head) or for a rough ride (the car bumped along the road).
# Example:
- _I bumped my head on the low doorway._
- _The suitcase bumped against the stairs as he dragged it down._
