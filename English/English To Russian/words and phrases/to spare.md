# to spare
?
# Transcription:
- /tə speər/ (BrE), /tə sper/ (AmE)
# Meaning:
- To give something, especially time, money, or effort, that you can afford to give.
- To refrain from harming, punishing, or upsetting someone; to save someone from something unpleasant.
- To save or free up something so it can be used for another purpose.
# Usage:
- Common patterns: spare time/money, spare a moment, spare someone's life, spare someone the trouble/details.
- Often used in questions and negatives: Can you spare a minute? I can't spare any cash right now.
# Example:
- _Could you spare a few minutes to look at this?_
- _The king spared the prisoner's life._
- _She spared him the embarrassment of explaining._
# Russian:
- Уделить, выделить (время, деньги); пощадить, избавить (от чего-либо); сэкономить, высвободить.
- В зависимости от контекста: _spare a moment_ — уделить минутку; _spare someone's life_ — пощадить чью-то жизнь; _spare someone the details_ — избавить кого-то от подробностей.
