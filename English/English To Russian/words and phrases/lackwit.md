# lackwit
?
# Transcription:
- /ˈlækwɪt/
# Meaning:
- A foolish or stupid person; someone lacking intelligence or good sense.
- A mild insult, often used in archaic, literary, or humorous contexts.
# Usage:
- Archaic/literary and mildly derogatory. Used as a noun, often as an insult. Can be used humorously or affectionately in modern writing, but it is not common in everyday English.
# Example:
- _You lackwit! You've spilled ink all over the manuscript._
- _The character is portrayed as a bumbling lackwit._
# Russian:
- Глупец, дурак, недоумок; бестолочь.
- Устаревающее/литературное, мягко-пренебрежительное. Буквально: «недостаток ума» (lack + wit).
