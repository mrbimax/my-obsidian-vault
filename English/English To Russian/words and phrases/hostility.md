# hostility
?
# Transcription:
- /hɒˈstɪləti/ (BrE), /hɑːˈstɪləti/ (AmE)
# Meaning:
- Unfriendly or aggressive feelings or behavior toward someone or something.
- Strong opposition or resistance; a state of open conflict.
# Usage:
- Common in formal and neutral contexts. Often used in plural "hostilities" to mean acts of war or fighting: the outbreak of hostilities, cease hostilities.
- Can describe personal attitudes or relations between groups, countries, or organizations.
# Example:
- _There was open hostility between the two departments._
- _The ceasefire ended months of hostilities._
# Russian:
- Враждебность, неприязнь; вражда, конфликт.
- Во множественном числе _hostilities_ — военные действия, боевые действия. _Open hostility_ — открытая враждебность.
