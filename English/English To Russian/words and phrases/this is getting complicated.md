# this is getting complicated
?
# Transcription:
- /ðɪs ɪz ˈɡetɪŋ ˈkɒmplɪkeɪtɪd/
# Meaning:
- This situation is becoming more difficult, confusing, or complex.
- Used to express that something is no longer simple and is getting harder to handle.
# Usage:
- Neutral to informal, common in conversation. Often said when a problem, plan, or discussion starts to involve many details or problems.
# Example:
- _First the car broke down, then it started raining — this is getting complicated._
- _We need to involve three more departments? This is getting complicated._
# Russian:
- Это становится сложно; всё усложняется.
- Выражает озабоченность или раздражение тем, что ситуация становится труднее.
