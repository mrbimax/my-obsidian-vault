# corroboration
?
# Transcription:
- BrE /kəˌrɒbəˈreɪʃn/; AmE /kəˌrɑːbəˈreɪʃn/
# Meaning:
- Evidence or information that supports a statement, theory, or claim.
- The act of confirming or strengthening something with additional proof.
# Usage:
- Formal and common in legal, academic, journalistic, and scientific contexts. Often followed by "of" or "for": corroboration of a story, corroboration for a claim. Common phrases: provide corroboration, seek corroboration, in corroboration of.
# Example:
- _The witness provided corroboration for his account of the events._
- _The police needed corroboration before charging the suspect._
- _There is no independent corroboration of her claims._
# Russian:
- Подтверждение, подкрепление (доказательствами); corroboration.
- О дополнительных доказательствах или сведениях, которые подтверждают чьи-либо слова, версию или теорию. Обычно формально.
