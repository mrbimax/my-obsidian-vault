# insulation
?
# Transcription:
- /ˌɪnsjuˈleɪʃn/ (BrE), /ˌɪnsəˈleɪʃn/ (AmE)
# Meaning:
- The act of protecting something by surrounding it with material that reduces the transfer of heat, sound, or electricity.
- Material used for this purpose, such as foam, fiberglass, or rubber.
# Usage:
- Common in construction, engineering, and home improvement. Often used for thermal insulation, electrical insulation, sound insulation.
- Neutral to technical. Usually uncountable.
# Example:
- _The house needs better insulation to keep warm in winter._
- _Rubber is used for electrical insulation._
# Russian:
- Изоляция; теплоизоляция, звукоизоляция, электроизоляция.
- Материал для изоляции. В зависимости от контекста: утепление, изолирующий материал.
