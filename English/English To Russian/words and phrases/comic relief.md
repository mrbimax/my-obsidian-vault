# comic relief
?
# Transcription:
- /ˈkɒmɪk rɪˈliːf/ (BrE), /ˈkɑːmɪk rɪˈliːf/ (AmE)
# Meaning:
- A humorous character, scene, or interlude introduced into a serious work to relieve tension or add levity.
- The use of humor to lighten the mood in a dramatic or tragic context.
# Usage:
- Common in literature, theatre, film, and television. Often used for a character or scene that provides a break from serious or tragic events.
- Neutral term. Can be countable or uncountable.
# Example:
- _The character of the jester provides comic relief in the tragedy._
- _The movie uses comic relief to balance the intense action scenes._
# Russian:
- Комическая разрядка; юмористическая передышка.
- В литературе, театре и кино: комический персонаж или эпизод, снижающий напряжение.
