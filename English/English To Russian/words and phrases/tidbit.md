# tidbit
?
# Transcription:
- /ˈtɪdbɪt/ (AmE)
# Meaning:
- A small piece of interesting or tasty information; a little interesting fact or gossip.
- A small, delicious piece of food; a morsel.
# Usage:
- Informal and common in American English. British English usually uses "titbit".
- Often used for interesting news, gossip, or a small detail: a tidbit of information, a juicy tidbit.
- For food, it means a small tasty piece, often served as an appetizer or snack.
# Example:
- _She shared a juicy tidbit about the new project._
- _The article is full of interesting tidbits about the city's history._
- _He offered us a few tasty tidbits before dinner._
# Russian:
- Лакомый кусочек; интересная деталь, новость, сплетня.
- В британском английском обычно _titbit_. Может означать как еду, так и небольшую интересную информацию.
