# to propose
?
# Transcription:
- /tə prəˈpəʊz/ (BrE), /tə prəˈpoʊz/ (AmE)
# Meaning:
- To suggest a plan, idea, or course of action for people to consider.
- To ask someone to marry you.
# Usage:
- Common patterns: propose something, propose doing something, propose that..., propose to someone.
- Neutral and fairly formal. For marriage, "propose to someone" is the standard phrase.
# Example:
- _She proposed a new way to solve the problem._
- _He proposed to her on the beach at sunset._
# Russian:
- Предлагать, вносить предложение; делать предложение (о браке).
- В значении «предлагать» часто формальнее, чем _suggest_. Для брака: _propose to someone_ — сделать предложение кому-либо.
