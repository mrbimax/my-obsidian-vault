# deceiving
?
# Transcription:
- /dɪˈsiːvɪŋ/
# Meaning:
- Giving someone a false impression; misleading or tricking.
- Present participle of "deceive": making someone believe something that is not true.
# Usage:
- Used as an adjective or participle. Common patterns: look deceiving, appearances can be deceiving, deceiving someone.
- Often in the phrase "appearances can be deceiving".
# Example:
- _The calm surface of the lake was deceiving; the water was freezing._
- _He was deceiving his clients for years before anyone noticed._
# Russian:
- Обманчивый; вводящий в заблуждение.
- Как причастие: обманывающий. Устойчивое выражение _appearances can be deceiving_ — внешность бывает обманчива.
