# excruciatingly
?
# Transcription:
- /ɪkˈskruːʃieɪtɪŋli/
# Meaning:
- In a way that is extremely painful, unpleasant, or intense.
- Used to emphasize that something is very bad, difficult, or embarrassing.
# Usage:
- Common before adjectives: excruciatingly painful, excruciatingly slow, excruciatingly detailed, excruciatingly shy.
- Neutral to formal. Often describes pain, embarrassment, boredom, or difficulty.
# Example:
- _The injury was excruciatingly painful._
- _The meeting was excruciatingly boring._
- _She was excruciatingly shy as a child._
# Russian:
- Мучительно, невыносимо, до боли; чрезвычайно.
- _Excruciatingly painful_ — мучительно больно; _excruciatingly slow_ — невыносимо медленно.
