# ramming
?
# Transcription:
- /ˈræmɪŋ/
# Meaning:
- Present participle of "ram": hitting or driving into something with great force.
- Forcing something into a place or position by pushing hard.
# Usage:
- Common for vehicles, ships, doors, and military attacks. Often used in phrases like ramming into, ramming through, ramming the door.
- Neutral to forceful. Can describe accidental collisions or deliberate attacks.
# Example:
- _The car went ramming into the barrier._
- _He was ramming the door with his shoulder._
- _The ship was ramming the enemy vessel._
# Russian:
- Таран; врезание; забивание; нанесение удара.
- Причастие/герундий от _ram_ — таранить, врезаться, забивать. _Ramming_ может означать как физическое столкновение, так и насильственное проникновение.
