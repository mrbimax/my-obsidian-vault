# sterile
?
# Transcription:
- BrE /ˈsteraɪl/; AmE /ˈsterəl/
# Meaning:
- Free from bacteria or other living microorganisms; completely clean.
- Unable to produce children, young, or fruit; infertile.
- Lacking interest, excitement, or creativity; producing no useful result.
# Usage:
- Medical/hygiene: sterile equipment, sterile gloves, sterile environment.
- Biological/agricultural: a sterile animal, sterile soil, sterile flower.
- Figurative: a sterile debate, a sterile relationship, sterile writing.
# Example:
- _The nurse used sterile gloves during the procedure._
- _The mule is sterile and cannot reproduce._
- _The discussion became sterile and repetitive._
# Russian:
- Стерильный, обеззараженный; бесплодный, неспособный к размножению; бесплодный, пустой (перен.).
- В медицине: стерильный. В биологии: бесплодный. В переносном смысле: лишённый творчества, не дающий результата.
