# to forbid
?
# Transcription:
- /tə fərˈbɪd/ (AmE), /tə fəˈbɪd/ (BrE)
# Meaning:
- To order someone not to do something; to make something not allowed.
- To refuse to allow something; to prohibit.
# Usage:
- Common patterns: forbid someone to do something, forbid something, forbid someone from doing something.
- Past tense: forbade. Past participle: forbidden. Formal/neutral; stronger than "not allow".
# Example:
- _My parents forbade me to stay out late._
- _The law forbids smoking in public buildings._
# Russian:
- Запрещать, воспрещать; не позволять, не разрешать.
- _Forbid someone to do something_ — запретить кому-либо делать что-либо. Past: forbade, forbidden.
