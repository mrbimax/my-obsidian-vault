# tribal
?
# Transcription:
- /ˈtraɪbl/
# Meaning:
- Relating to a tribe or tribes.
- Relating to strong loyalty to a social group, especially in politics or society.
# Usage:
- Common before nouns: tribal leaders, tribal customs, tribal lands, tribal loyalty, tribal politics.
- Neutral to formal. Can describe traditional societies or modern group behavior.
# Example:
- _The tribal leaders met to discuss the dispute._
- _Tribal customs vary from region to region._
- _Modern politics can sometimes feel deeply tribal._
# Russian:
- Племенной; родовой, трайбалистский.
- В переносном смысле: клановый, групповой, основанный на узкой лояльности. _Tribal loyalty_ — племенная или клановая верность.
