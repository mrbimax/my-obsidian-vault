# sincere
?
# Transcription:
- /sɪnˈsɪər/ (BrE), /sɪnˈsɪr/ (AmE)
# Meaning:
- Genuine and honest; not pretending or lying.
- Coming from real feelings or beliefs, not from a desire to deceive.
# Usage:
- Common patterns: a sincere apology, sincere gratitude, a sincere person, sincerely hope.
- Neutral to formal. Describes people, feelings, statements, or actions that are truly meant.
# Example:
- _She gave a sincere apology for being late._
- _He seemed sincere when he said he wanted to help._
# Russian:
- Искренний, неподдельный, чистосердечный.
- О человеке, чувстве или словах, которые выражают настоящие намерения, без притворства.
