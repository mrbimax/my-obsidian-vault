# to ditch
?
# Transcription:
- /tə dɪtʃ/
# Meaning:
- To get rid of something or someone; to abandon or leave someone or something behind.
- To avoid or skip something, especially school, work, or a commitment.
- To crash-land an aircraft in water and abandon it.
# Usage:
- Informal and common in everyday speech. Common patterns: ditch someone, ditch something, ditch class, ditch a plan, ditch a car.
- Can mean to leave a person, to throw something away, or to skip an obligation. Not usually used in formal writing.
# Example:
- _He ditched his friends at the party and went home early._
- _She ditched class to hang out at the mall._
- _We had to ditch the car and walk the rest of the way._
# Russian:
- Бросить, кинуть, скинуть; избавиться, отказаться.
- Прогулять, пропустить (учёбу, работу).
- Бросить, оставить (машину, самолёт и т. п.). _To ditch someone_ — кинуть, бросить кого-либо; _to ditch class_ — прогулять урок.
