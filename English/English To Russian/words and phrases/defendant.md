# defendant
?
# Transcription:
- /dɪˈfendənt/
# Meaning:
- A person, company, or organization accused of a crime or sued in a court of law.
- The party against whom a legal action is brought.
# Usage:
- Formal legal term. In criminal cases, the defendant is the accused. In civil cases, the defendant is the person or entity being sued.
- Common phrases: the defendant pleaded guilty/not guilty, the defendant's lawyer, the defendant took the stand.
# Example:
- _The defendant pleaded not guilty to all charges._
- _The defendant was ordered to pay damages to the plaintiff._
# Russian:
- Обвиняемый, подсудимый (в уголовном деле); ответчик (в гражданском деле).
- В уголовном процессе чаще «подсудимый» или «обвиняемый», в гражданском — «ответчик».
