# severe
?
# Transcription:
- BrE /sɪˈvɪə(r)/, AmE /sɪˈvɪr/
# Meaning:
- Very bad or serious; causing great pain, difficulty, or damage.
- Strict, harsh, or unkind in treatment or judgment.
- Very plain or simple in style; not decorated.
# Usage:
- Common before nouns: severe pain, severe weather, severe punishment, severe criticism, severe shortage.
- Also used after linking verbs: the situation is severe, the storm was severe.
- Neutral to formal. Stronger than "bad" or "serious".
# Example:
- _She suffered severe injuries in the accident._
- _The region was hit by severe flooding._
- _He received a severe punishment for his actions._
