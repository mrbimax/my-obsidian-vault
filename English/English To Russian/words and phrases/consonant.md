# consonant
?
# Transcription:
- /ˈkɒnsənənt/ (BrE), /ˈkɑːnsənənt/ (AmE)
# Meaning:
- A speech sound produced when the breath is partly or completely blocked by the tongue, teeth, or lips.
- A letter representing such a sound, such as b, c, d, f, g, etc.
- (Adjective) In agreement or harmony with something; consistent.
# Usage:
- In phonetics and language learning, contrasted with "vowel". Common phrases: consonant sound, consonant letter, voiced/voiceless consonant.
- As an adjective, often followed by "with": consonant with the facts, consonant with our values.
# Example:
- _The word "cat" begins and ends with a consonant._
- _His actions are consonant with his words._
# Russian:
- Согласный (звук); согласная (буква).
- Как прилагательное: согласующийся, созвучный, соответствующий (обычно _consonant with_).
