# invention
?
# Transcription:
- /ɪnˈvenʃn/
# Meaning:
- The act of creating or designing something new, especially a process, device, or method.
- A new thing that has been created or designed.
# Usage:
- Common in science, technology, business, and everyday contexts. Often used with verbs like create, patent, develop, and in phrases like a brilliant invention, the invention of the telephone.
# Example:
- _The invention of the printing press changed the world._
- _Her latest invention is a device that turns waste into fuel._
# Russian:
- Изобретение; создание чего-либо нового.
- Может означать как процесс, так и результат — новое устройство, метод или идея. _Patent an invention_ — запатентовать изобретение. _The invention of..._ — изобретение чего-либо.
