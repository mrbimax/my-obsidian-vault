# anticipating
?
# Transcription:
- /ænˈtɪsɪpeɪtɪŋ/
# Meaning:
- Waiting for or expecting something to happen.
- Feeling excited, nervous, or curious about something that is going to happen.
# Usage:
- Often used as an adjective or participle to describe a person or mood. Common patterns: anticipating something, eagerly anticipating, anticipating a problem.
# Example:
- _She sat by the window, anticipating his arrival._
- _We are eagerly anticipating the results of the election._
# Russian:
- Ожидающий; предвкушающий; в ожидании.
- Как причастие: ожидая, предвкушая. От глагола _anticipate_ — ожидать, предвидеть.
