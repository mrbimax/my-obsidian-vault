# convenient
?
# Transcription:
- /kənˈviːniənt/
# Meaning:
- Fitting in well with a person's needs, activities, and plans.
- Involving little trouble or effort; suitable or easy to use.
- Nearby or easily accessible.
# Usage:
- Common patterns: convenient for someone, convenient to do something, a convenient time/place.
- Neutral and common in everyday speech. Can describe time, place, situation, or arrangement.
# Example:
- _Would it be convenient for you to meet tomorrow?_
- _The hotel is convenient for the airport._
- _Online shopping is convenient for busy people._
# Russian:
- Удобный, подходящий; доступный.
- О времени, месте, ситуации или способе, который хорошо подходит и не создает трудностей.
