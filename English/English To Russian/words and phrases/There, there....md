# There, there...
?
# Transcription:
- /ðeər ðeər/
# Meaning:
- A soothing expression used to comfort someone who is crying, upset, or distressed.
- Often said to children or someone in an emotional state, sometimes with a patronizing tone.
# Usage:
- Said twice, usually in a gentle or calming voice. Can be sincere, but can also sound dismissive or condescending if said coldly.
- Common in informal speech and in fictional dialogue.
# Example:
- _There, there, don't cry. It's going to be okay._
- _There, there, you'll find another job soon._
# Russian:
- Ну-ну, тише-тише; не плачь; всё-всё.
- Буквально: «там, там», но смысл — успокоить. Может звучать снисходительно, если произнесено холодно.
