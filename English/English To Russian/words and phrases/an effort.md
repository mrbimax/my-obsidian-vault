# an effort
?
# Transcription:
- BrE /ən ˈefət/; AmE /ən ˈefərt/
# Meaning:
- A determined attempt to do something.
- The physical or mental energy needed to do something.
# Usage:
- Common patterns: make an effort, put effort into something, a great effort, without effort, take effort.
- Often used when something requires energy, will, or determination.
# Example:
- _She made an effort to smile despite the pain._
- _It took a lot of effort to finish the project on time._
- _He lifted the box without any effort._
# Russian:
- Усилие, старание, попытка.
- _Make an effort_ — прилагать усилия, стараться. _A great effort_ — большое усилие. _Without effort_ — без усилий, легко.
