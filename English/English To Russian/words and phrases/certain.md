# certain
?
# Transcription:
- /ˈsɜːrtn/
# Meaning:
- Sure, confident, or having no doubt.
- Particular or specific, but not named or described exactly.
- Some but not much; a limited amount.
# Usage:
- Patterns: certain of/about something, certain that..., a certain person/amount, to a certain extent.
- Neutral, common in spoken and written English.
# Example:
- _I'm certain that I locked the door._
- _A certain Mr. Smith called while you were out._
- _To a certain extent, I agree with you._
# Russian:
- Уверенный, убеждённый; определённый, некий; некоторый.
- _Be certain of_ — быть уверенным в. _A certain_ — некий, какой-то. _To a certain extent_ — в определённой степени
