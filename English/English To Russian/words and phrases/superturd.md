# superturd
?
# Transcription:
- /ˈsuːpərtɜːrd/
# Meaning:
- (Vulgar slang, derogatory) An extremely contemptible, stupid, or disgusting person.
- Literal: an exceptionally large piece of feces.
# Usage:
- Very informal, vulgar, and insulting. Used as a strong insult for someone the speaker despises. Not for polite or formal situations.
# Example:
- _That superturd lied to everyone and then disappeared._
- _He called his boss a superturd and got fired on the spot._
# Russian:
- Супердерьмо; полное дерьмо; крайне презренный человек (груб., оскорб.).
- Буквально: огромная какашка. В переносном смысле — очень сильное оскорбление, обычно о человеке.
