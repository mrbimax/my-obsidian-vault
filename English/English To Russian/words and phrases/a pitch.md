# a pitch
?
# Transcription:
- /ə pɪtʃ/
# Meaning:
- A playing field for sports, especially in British English.
- A persuasive talk or sales presentation.
- A throw of the ball in baseball or cricket.
- The highness or lowness of a sound or musical note.
# Usage:
- Sports sense: BrE; AmE usually "field".
- Business sense: make a pitch, sales pitch.
- Baseball/cricket: throw a pitch, a good pitch.
- Music: perfect pitch, high/low pitch.
# Example:
- _The players ran onto the pitch._
- _He made a strong pitch for the new product._
- _The pitcher threw a fast pitch._
- _She has perfect pitch._
# Russian:
- Поле (спортивное, брит.); презентация, рекламная речь; бросок, подача; высота звука, тон.
- Многозначное слово; выбор значения зависит от контекста. _A pitch_ не используется в значении «смола, битум» (там обычно _pitch_ без артикля).
