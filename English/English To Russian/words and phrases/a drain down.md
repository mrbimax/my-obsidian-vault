# a drain down
?
# Transcription:
- /ə dreɪn daʊn/
# Meaning:
- Not a common fixed phrase by itself. Usually "drain down" is a phrasal verb meaning to flow away downward or to empty a system of liquid.
- As a noun, the usual form is "drain-down": the act of draining water from a plumbing or heating system.
# Usage:
- Verb: drain down the sink, water drained down the pipe, drain down the radiators.
- Noun: a drain-down of the central heating system; the plumber did a drain-down.
- Technical and practical. Common in plumbing, heating, and maintenance contexts.
# Example:
- _The water drained down the plughole._
- _We need to drain down the radiators before replacing the valve._
- _The plumber carried out a full drain-down of the system._
# Russian:
- Слив, сливание; спуск воды вниз.
- Как глагол: _drain down_ — стекать вниз, сливать воду из системы.
- Как существительное: _drain-down_ — слив системы (отопления, водопровода). Форма _a drain down_ менее стандартна; чаще пишут _a drain-down_ или используют _drain down_ как глагол.
