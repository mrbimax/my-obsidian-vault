# to entail
?
# Transcription:
- /ɪnˈteɪl/
# Meaning:
- To involve something as a necessary part or consequence.
- To make something necessary or unavoidable.
# Usage:
- Used when one action, situation, or decision requires or results in something else. Common in formal, academic, or business contexts.
# Example:
- _The job entails a lot of travel._
- _Accepting the offer would entail moving to another city._
# Russian:
- Влечь за собой, означать, предполагать; требовать.
- Не просто «включать», а именно делать что-то необходимым следствием.
