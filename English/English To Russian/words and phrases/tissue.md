# tissue
?
# Transcription:
- /ˈtɪʃuː/ (AmE), /ˈtɪsjuː/ (BrE)
# Meaning:
- A group of similar cells that together perform a particular function in a plant or animal.
- A soft, thin paper used for wrapping, cleaning, or as a disposable handkerchief.
# Usage:
- Biological sense: muscle tissue, nervous tissue, connective tissue, tissue sample.
- Everyday sense: a box of tissues, facial tissue, tissue paper.
- Can be countable or uncountable depending on meaning.
# Example:
- _The doctor examined a sample of tissue under the microscope._
- _She grabbed a tissue and blew her nose._
# Russian:
- Ткань (биологическая).
- Бумажная салфетка; папиросная бумага; тонкая обёрточная бумага.
- В медицине и биологии: _tissue_ — ткань. В быту: _tissue_ — бумажная салфетка или тонкая мягкая бумага.
