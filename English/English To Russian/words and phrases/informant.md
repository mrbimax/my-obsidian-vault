# informant
?
# Transcription:
- /ɪnˈfɔːrmənt/
# Meaning:
- A person who gives information to the police, authorities, or another group, often secretly.
- A person who provides information about a person, group, or situation, especially for money or protection.
# Usage:
- Common in crime, law enforcement, and intelligence contexts: police informant, confidential informant.
- Neutral to formal. Can have a negative shade like "informer" or "snitch" in some contexts.
# Example:
- _The police relied on a confidential informant to locate the gang._
- _He was suspected of being an informant for the government._
# Russian:
- Информатор, осведомитель; доносчик.
- В зависимости от контекста: _police informant_ — полицейский информатор; _confidential informant_ — конфиденциальный источник.
