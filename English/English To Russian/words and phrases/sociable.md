# sociable
?
# Transcription:
- /ˈsəʊʃəbl/
# Meaning:
- Willing to talk and engage in activities with other people; friendly and outgoing.
- Enjoying the company of others rather than being alone.
# Usage:
- Used to describe a person, animal, or mood that is friendly and likes being with others. Common patterns: a sociable person, a sociable evening.
# Example:
- _She's very sociable and makes friends easily._
- _The dog is sociable and loves meeting new people._
# Russian:
- Общительный, коммуникабельный, компанейский.
- Может также переводиться как «дружелюбный» в контексте животного или мероприятия.
