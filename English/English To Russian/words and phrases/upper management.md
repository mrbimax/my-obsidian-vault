# upper management
?
# Transcription:
- BrE /ˌʌpə ˈmænɪdʒmənt/; AmE /ˌʌpər ˈmænɪdʒmənt/
# Meaning:
- The highest level of managers in an organization, responsible for major decisions and overall direction.
- Top executives and senior leaders as a group, such as the CEO, board members, and senior vice presidents.
# Usage:
- Formal business term. Usually uncountable and used collectively: upper management decided..., a decision by upper management.
- Refers to the people at the top of a company or organization, above middle management.
# Example:
- _Upper management approved the new strategy._
- _The complaint was escalated to upper management._
- _She has years of experience dealing with upper management._
# Russian:
- Высшее руководство; топ-менеджмент; руководство компании.
- Обычно собирательно: _upper management_ — руководство верхнего уровня, в отличие от _middle management_ — среднего звена.
