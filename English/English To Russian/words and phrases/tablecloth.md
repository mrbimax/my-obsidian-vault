# tablecloth
?
# Transcription:
- BrE /ˈteɪblklɒθ/; AmE /ˈteɪblklɔːθ/
# Meaning:
- A cloth used to cover a table, especially during meals or for decoration.
- A piece of fabric shaped to fit a table.
# Usage:
- Common in household, dining, and restaurant contexts. Often used with verbs like spread, lay, put on, change, wash.
- Usually countable: a tablecloth, two tablecloths.
# Example:
- _She spread a clean tablecloth over the dining table._
- _The restaurant uses white linen tablecloths._
# Russian:
- Скатерть.
- Обычно о ткани, которой накрывают стол, особенно во время еды или для украшения.
