# to bore
?
# Transcription:
- /tə bɔːr/
# Meaning:
- To make someone feel uninterested, tired, or impatient because something is dull.
- To make a hole in something with a drill or similar tool.
# Usage:
- For feelings, common pattern: bore someone, bore someone to death/tears, get bored.
- For drilling, common in technical or practical contexts: bore a hole, bore a tunnel.
# Example:
- _The long speech bored the audience._
- _He bored a hole in the wall to run the cable._
# Russian:
- Надоедать, наскучить, утомлять (от скуки).
- Сверлить, бурить, проделывать отверстие.
- _Bore_ также past tense of _bear_, но здесь рассматривается как самостоятельный глагол.
