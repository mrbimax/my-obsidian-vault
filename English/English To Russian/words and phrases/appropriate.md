# appropriate
?
# Transcription:
- BrE /əˈprəʊpriət/; AmE /əˈproʊpriət/
# Meaning:
- Suitable or proper in the circumstances; fitting.
- Correct or right for a particular situation, person, or purpose.
# Usage:
- Common patterns: appropriate for something, appropriate to something, appropriate for someone to do something.
- Neutral and common in everyday, formal, and academic contexts. Often used to describe behavior, clothing, language, or timing.
# Example:
- _Wear something appropriate for the interview._
- _His behavior was not appropriate at the funeral._
- _It is not appropriate to ask such personal questions._
# Russian:
- Подходящий, уместный, соответствующий, надлежащий.
- О действии, одежде, слове или поведении, которое соответствует ситуации. Глагол _appropriate_ (присваивать, ассигновать) произносится иначе: /əˈprəʊprieɪt/.
