# bonehead
?
# Transcription:
- /ˈboʊnhed/ (AmE), /ˈbəʊnhed/ (BrE)
# Meaning:
- (Informal, derogatory) A stupid or foolish person; someone who does something silly or thoughtless.
- A stubborn or thick-headed person.
# Usage:
- Informal and mildly derogatory. Often used teasingly or in frustration, not usually as a severe insult.
- Common in American English. Can be used as a noun: What a bonehead! Or as an adjective before a noun: a bonehead move.
# Example:
- _I can't believe I forgot my keys again — what a bonehead._
- _That was a real bonehead mistake._
# Russian:
- Тупица, балбес, олух, дурень; бестолковый человек.
- Разговорно-пренебрежительное. Может использоваться как мягкое или шутливое оскорбление, особенно в американском английском.
