# to sue
?
# Transcription:
- /tə suː/
# Meaning:
- To make a legal claim against someone, usually in a court of law.
- To start legal proceedings against a person, company, or organization to get money or another remedy.
# Usage:
- Common patterns: sue someone, sue someone for something, sue for damages, threaten to sue.
- Formal legal term. Used in civil cases, not criminal prosecutions.
# Example:
- _She threatened to sue the company for discrimination._
- _He sued his former employer for unpaid wages._
# Russian:
- Подавать в суд, судиться, предъявлять иск.
- _Sue someone for something_ — подать в суд на кого-либо за что-либо; _sue for damages_ — требовать возмещения убытков через суд.
