# this is rare
?
# Transcription:
- /ðɪs ɪz reər/
# Meaning:
- Used to say that something does not happen often or is unusual.
- Can express genuine surprise or, depending on tone, sarcasm about something that is actually common.
# Usage:
- A simple comment about the frequency of an event or situation; tone can make it sincere or sarcastic.
# Example:
- _He actually arrived on time — this is rare._
- _You're admitting you were wrong? This is rare._
# Russian:
- Это редкость; такое бывает редко.
- В саркастическом тоне: «Надо же, такое бывает» / «Это редкость» (когда на самом деле это происходит часто).
