# sugar lump
?
# Transcription:
- /ˈʃʊɡər lʌmp/
# Meaning:
- A small, hard, usually cube-shaped piece of sugar used to sweeten drinks.
- A lump of sugar, as opposed to granulated or powdered sugar.
# Usage:
- Common in British English for a cube of sugar served with tea or coffee. In American English, "sugar cube" is more common.
# Example:
- _He dropped two sugar lumps into his tea._
- _She placed a sugar lump on the saucer next to the cup._
# Russian:
- Кусочек сахара; кубик сахара; сахар-рафинад.
- В американском английском чаще говорят _sugar cube_.
