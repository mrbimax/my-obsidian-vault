# custody
?
# Transcription:
- /ˈkʌstədi/
# Meaning:
- The legal right or duty to care for a child, especially after parents separate or divorce.
- The state of being kept in prison or by the police; detention.
# Usage:
- In family law: have custody of a child, joint custody, sole custody, award custody.
- In criminal contexts: in custody, police custody, take someone into custody.
# Example:
- _She was awarded full custody of the children._
- _The suspect is in police custody._
# Russian:
- Опека, попечительство; право на воспитание ребёнка.
- Содержание под стражей, задержание; заключение.
