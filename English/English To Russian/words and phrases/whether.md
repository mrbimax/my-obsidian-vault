# whether
?
# Transcription:
- /ˈweðər/
# Meaning:
- Used to introduce a question or doubt about two or more possibilities; if.
- Used to talk about a choice between alternatives, often with "or" or "or not".
# Usage:
- Common in indirect questions and polite or formal statements. Patterns: whether to do something, whether someone does something, whether ... or ..., whether or not.
# Example:
- _I can't decide whether to stay or leave._
- _She asked whether I had finished the report._
- _We'll go whether or not it rains._
# Russian:
- Ли (в косвенном вопросе); или... или; не то... не то.
- _Whether or not_ — так или иначе, независимо от того. Не путать с _weather_ — погода.
