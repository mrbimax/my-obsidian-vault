# to get sued
?
# Transcription:
- /tə ɡet suːd/
# Meaning:
- To be sued; to have a legal claim or lawsuit brought against you.
- To be the person, company, or organization that someone takes to court in a civil case.
# Usage:
- Passive and informal-neutral form of "to sue". Common patterns: get sued for something, get sued by someone, get sued over something.
- Used in everyday, business, and legal contexts. Focuses on the person receiving the lawsuit, not the one filing it.
# Example:
- _He got sued for damages after the accident._
- _The company got sued by a former employee._
# Russian:
- Быть привлечённым к суду; получить иск; попасть под судебный иск.
- _To get sued_ — пассивная разговорная форма от _to sue_. Означает, что против кого-то подан гражданский иск, и он выступает ответчиком.
