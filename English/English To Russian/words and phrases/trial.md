# trial
?
# Transcription:
- /ˈtraɪəl/
# Meaning:
- A formal examination of evidence in a court of law to decide whether someone is guilty or innocent.
- A test or experiment to evaluate the quality, performance, or suitability of something.
- A difficult or unpleasant experience; an ordeal.
# Usage:
- Legal sense: go on trial, stand trial, trial by jury, murder trial.
- Test sense: clinical trial, field trial, trial run, trial period.
- Ordeal sense: trials and tribulations, the trials of life.
# Example:
- _The trial lasted three weeks and ended in an acquittal._
- _They conducted a clinical trial of the new drug._
- _The explorers faced many trials during their journey._
