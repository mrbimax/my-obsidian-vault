# globule
?
# Transcription:
- /ˈɡlɒbjuːl/ (BrE), /ˈɡlɑːbjuːl/ (AmE)
# Meaning:
- A small, round particle or drop of a substance, especially a liquid or semi-liquid.
- A tiny spherical mass, often seen in fluids or biological material.
# Usage:
- Technical and scientific. Common in phrases like fat globule, oil globule, globule of water.
- Neutral and usually countable. The plural is globules.
# Example:
- _A globule of oil floated on the surface of the water._
- _The microscope showed tiny fat globules in the sample._
# Russian:
- Глобула; капелька, шарик (вещества).
- Мелкая округлая частица или капля, часто в научном или медицинском контексте.
