# disloyal
?
# Transcription:
- /dɪsˈlɔɪəl/
# Meaning:
- Not loyal to a person, group, country, or cause; unfaithful or betraying trust.
- Showing a lack of support or allegiance where it is expected.
# Usage:
- Common patterns: disloyal to someone/something, a disloyal friend, disloyal behavior.
- Stronger than just unreliable; implies betrayal of trust or allegiance.
# Example:
- _He was disloyal to his friends by sharing their secrets._
- _She felt betrayed by his disloyal behavior._
# Russian:
- Нелояльный, неверный, предательский; изменнический.
- О человеке или поступке, который нарушает верность, доверие или преданность.
