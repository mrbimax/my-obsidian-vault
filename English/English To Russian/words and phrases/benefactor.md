# benefactor
?
# Transcription:
- /ˈbenɪfæktər/ (AmE), /ˈbenɪfæktə(r)/ (BrE)
# Meaning:
- A person who gives money or other help to a person, organization, or cause.
- Especially someone who supports a charity, school, museum, or other institution financially.
# Usage:
- Formal and often used in contexts of charity, patronage, and philanthropy. Common patterns: a generous benefactor, a benefactor of the university, anonymous benefactor.
# Example:
- _An anonymous benefactor donated a million dollars to the hospital._
- _She was a generous benefactor of the local library._
# Russian:
- Благодетель, благотворитель; меценат, спонсор.
- О человеке, который оказывает финансовую или иную помощь людям, организациям или учреждениям. Обычно формально.
