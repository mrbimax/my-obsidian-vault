# to rub
?
# Transcription:
- /tə rʌb/
# Meaning:
- To move your hand or an object back and forth over a surface while pressing on it.
- To spread a substance over a surface by pressing and moving your hand or an object.
- To cause friction, often producing heat, soreness, or a rubbing sound.
# Usage:
- Common patterns: rub something, rub something on/into/over something, rub your hands/eyes, rub something dry.
- Neutral and common in everyday, cooking, medical, and practical contexts. Also in the idiom rub someone up the wrong way.
# Example:
- _She rubbed her eyes and yawned._
- _He rubbed sunscreen into his shoulders._
- _The cat rubbed against my leg._
# Russian:
- Тереть, растирать, натирать; тереться.
- _Rub something into something_ — втирать что-либо во что-либо. _Rub against_ — тереться о что-либо. _Rub someone up the wrong way_ — раздражать кого-либо.
