# bribes
?
# Transcription:
- /braɪbz/
# Meaning:
- Money or gifts given to someone in order to persuade them to act dishonestly or illegally.
- Illegal payments made to influence a person's decisions or actions.
# Usage:
- Plural noun of "bribe". Common in legal, political, business, and crime contexts. Often used with verbs like take, accept, offer, pay, give.
# Example:
- _The politician was accused of accepting bribes from construction companies._
- _Employees are forbidden from taking bribes from suppliers._
# Russian:
- Взятки.
- Деньги или подарки, даваемые для подкупа должностного лица или иного человека.
