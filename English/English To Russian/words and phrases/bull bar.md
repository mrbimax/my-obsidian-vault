# bull bar
?
# Transcription:
- /ˈbʊl bɑːr/
# Meaning:
- A sturdy metal frame or bar attached to the front of a vehicle, especially a truck, SUV, or off-road vehicle, to protect the front end from impact.
- Also used to protect against brush, debris, or animal strikes; similar to a push bar or grille guard.
# Usage:
- Common in automotive, off-road, and truck contexts. In some regions, "bull bar" is the usual term; in the US, "grille guard" or "push bar" may be more common. In Australia, a similar device is often called a "roo bar".
# Example:
- _He fitted a bull bar to his pickup truck for off-road driving._
- _The bull bar protected the front of the vehicle in the collision._
# Russian:
- Кенгурятник; силовой бампер; защита передка.
- Разговорное «кенгурятник» часто используется именно для bull bar. В Австралии аналогичное устройство называют _roo bar_.
