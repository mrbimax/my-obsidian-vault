# to cuff someone
?
# Transcription:
- /tə kʌf ˈsʌmwʌn/
# Meaning:
- To hit someone with an open hand, usually on the head or ear.
- To put handcuffs on someone; to arrest or restrain someone.
# Usage:
- The hitting sense is informal and can mean a slap, smack, or light blow, often as punishment.
- The handcuff sense is neutral and common in police, crime, and law contexts.
# Example:
- _She cuffed him on the ear for swearing._
- _The officer cuffed the suspect and read him his rights._
# Russian:
- Шлёпнуть, стукнуть, дать оплеуху (ладонью, обычно по голове или уху).
- Надеть наручники на кого-либо, заковать, арестовать.
