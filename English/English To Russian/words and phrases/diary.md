# diary
?
# Transcription:
- /ˈdaɪəri/
# Meaning:
- A book with a space for each day of the year, used for recording appointments, events, or personal experiences.
- A personal record of daily events, thoughts, or feelings.
# Usage:
- In British English, "diary" often means a personal journal. In American English, a personal one is usually called a "journal", while "diary" can also mean an appointment book or planner.
# Example:
- _She writes in her diary every night before bed._
- _I checked my diary to see if I was free on Friday._
# Russian:
- Дневник (личный); ежедневник, записная книжка для дел.
- В британском английском чаще личный дневник; в американском для этого обычно _journal_, а _diary_ — это ещё и ежедневник.
