# to arrange
?
# Transcription:
- /tə əˈreɪndʒ/
# Meaning:
- To put things in a particular order or position.
- To plan or organize something in advance.
- To adapt a piece of music for particular instruments or voices.
# Usage:
- Common patterns: arrange something, arrange for something, arrange to do something, arrange something with someone.
- Neutral and common in everyday, business, event-planning, and music contexts.
# Example:
- _She arranged the flowers in a vase._
- _We arranged to meet at the station at six._
- _He arranged the song for piano and violin._
# Russian:
- Располагать, расставлять, приводить в порядок.
- Организовывать, устраивать, договариваться.
- Аранжировать, перекладывать (музыку).
