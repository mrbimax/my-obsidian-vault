# a booby trap
?
# Transcription:
- /ə ˈbuːbi træp/
# Meaning:
- A hidden or disguised trap, often designed to harm or surprise someone who triggers it.
- A trap set to catch someone unawares, especially one triggered by an innocent action.
# Usage:
- Common in military, security, and everyday contexts. Can be literal (a physical trap) or figurative (a tricky situation). Often used with set, trigger, fall into.
# Example:
- _The soldiers were trained to spot booby traps._
- _He fell into a booby trap set by his friends._
- _The door was rigged with a booby trap._
# Russian:
- Ловушка, западня; мина-ловушка.
- Буквально: ловушка для простаков. Может быть как настоящей ловушкой, так и розыгрышем.
