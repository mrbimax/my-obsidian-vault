# champagne
?
# Transcription:
- /ʃæmˈpeɪn/
# Meaning:
- A sparkling white wine from the Champagne region of France.
- A similar sparkling wine made elsewhere, often called champagne informally.
# Usage:
- Usually uncountable. Common in celebrations and formal events. Phrases: a glass of champagne, a bottle of champagne, champagne reception, champagne socialist (figurative).
# Example:
- _They toasted the bride and groom with champagne._
- _We had champagne to celebrate the promotion._
# Russian:
- Шампанское.
- Буквально: игристое вино из провинции Шампань. В русском языке часто обозначает любое игристое вино, но в английском champagne обычно именно французское, а другие — sparkling wine.
