# entrepreneurial
?
# Transcription:
- /ˌɒntrəprəˈnɜːriəl/ (BrE), /ˌɑːntrəprəˈnɜːriəl/ (AmE)
# Meaning:
- Relating to an entrepreneur or entrepreneurship.
- Having the qualities of an entrepreneur, such as initiative, risk-taking, and innovation.
# Usage:
- Common before nouns: entrepreneurial spirit, entrepreneurial skills, entrepreneurial venture.
- Neutral and fairly formal. Describes people, attitudes, activities, or environments.
# Example:
- _She has a strong entrepreneurial spirit and started three companies._
- _The city offers grants to support entrepreneurial projects._
# Russian:
- Предпринимательский; инициативный, склонный к предпринимательству.
- О человеке, деятельности или среде, связанной с созданием бизнеса и принятием рисков.
