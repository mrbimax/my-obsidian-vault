# to tune out
?
# Transcription:
- /tə tuːn aʊt/
# Meaning:
- To stop paying attention to someone or something; to ignore or block out what is being said or what is happening around you.
- To mentally withdraw or dissociate, especially when something is boring, overwhelming, or unpleasant.
# Usage:
- Common phrasal verb, often separable: tune someone/something out, tune out the noise, tune him out.
- Also used without an object: I just tuned out during the lecture.
- Informal to neutral. Common in everyday speech about conversations, lectures, background noise, and emotional withdrawal.
# Example:
- _I tuned out after the first ten minutes of the meeting._
- _She tunes out the traffic noise when she works._
- _He was talking so much that I just tuned him out._
# Russian:
- Отключиться, перестать слушать, пропускать мимо ушей; не обращать внимания.
- В переносном смысле: мысленно отстраниться, абстрагироваться, уйти в себя. _Tune someone out_ — перестать слушать кого-либо; _tune out the noise_ — отключиться от шума.
