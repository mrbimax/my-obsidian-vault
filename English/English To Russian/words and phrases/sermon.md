# sermon
?
# Transcription:
- /ˈsɜːrmən/
# Meaning:
- A talk on a religious or moral subject, especially one given during a church service by a priest or minister.
- (Informal) A long, serious talk about someone's behavior, often when it is not wanted.
# Usage:
- Common in religious contexts: give a sermon, hear a sermon, Sunday sermon.
- Figurative or informal: a lecture or moralizing talk, often boring or annoying.
# Example:
- _The priest delivered a sermon about forgiveness._
- _My father gave me a sermon about being more responsible._
# Russian:
- Проповедь.
- В переносном смысле: нравоучение, наставление, нотация.
