# an enterprise
?
# Transcription:
- BrE /ˈentəpraɪz/; AmE /ˈentərpraɪz/
# Meaning:
- A business or company, especially one involved in commercial activity.
- A project or undertaking, especially a bold, difficult, or important one.
- Initiative and resourcefulness; the ability to start new ventures.
# Usage:
- Common in business, formal, and everyday contexts. Often used in phrases like private enterprise, state enterprise, enterprise software, business enterprise.
- Neutral to formal. Can refer to a company, a venture, or a personal quality.
# Example:
- _She works for a large enterprise in the tech sector._
- _Starting a school in the village was a bold enterprise._
- _He showed great enterprise in solving the problem._
# Russian:
- Предприятие, компания; предпринимательство, инициатива; проект, начинание.
- Может означать как коммерческую организацию, так и смелое дело или качество человека. _Private enterprise_ — частное предпринимательство; _enterprise software_ — корпоративное ПО.
