# to admire
?
# Transcription:
- /tə ədˈmaɪər/ (BrE), /tə ədˈmaɪr/ (AmE)
# Meaning:
- To respect and like someone or something, often because of their qualities, achievements, or appearance.
- To look at someone or something with pleasure, approval, or wonder.
# Usage:
- Common patterns: admire someone for something, admire something, admire someone's courage/patience/work.
- Neutral and common. Can be about people, qualities, actions, art, nature, or objects.
# Example:
- _I admire her for speaking up so honestly._
- _We stood on the hill and admired the view._
# Russian:
- Восхищаться, любоваться; уважать, ценить.
- _Admire someone for something_ — восхищаться кем-либо за что-либо. Не путать с _admire_ + to + verb: обычно говорят _admire someone for doing something_.
