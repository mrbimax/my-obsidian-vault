# cheat sheet
?
# Transcription:
- /tʃiːt ʃiːt/
# Meaning:
- A small piece of paper with notes or answers, used secretly during an exam to cheat.
- A short, concise set of notes or reference information used as a quick guide, often not for cheating.
# Usage:
- In exams, it means hidden notes used dishonestly. In work or study, it can mean a helpful summary or quick-reference guide.
- Informal to neutral. Common in school, university, and workplace contexts. The dishonest sense is negative; the reference sense is usually neutral or positive.
# Example:
- _He was caught using a cheat sheet during the final exam._
- _She made a cheat sheet of all the formulas for the test._
- _The manual is basically a cheat sheet for new employees._
# Russian:
- Шпаргалка.
- В переносном смысле: краткая памятка, шпаргалка-справочник, сводка ключевой информации. В учебном контексте — обычно именно шпаргалка для списывания.
