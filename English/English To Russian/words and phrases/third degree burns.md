# third degree burns
?
# Transcription:
- BrE /ˌθɜːd dɪˈɡriː bɜːnz/; AmE /ˌθɜːrd dɪˈɡriː bɜːrnz/
# Meaning:
- Burns that damage all layers of the skin and may also damage underlying tissues such as fat, muscle, or bone.
- The most severe category of burns, often requiring urgent medical treatment and possibly skin grafts.
# Usage:
- Medical and formal. Usually plural: third-degree burns. Singular: a third-degree burn.
- Common in news, medical reports, and first-aid contexts. Hyphenated before a noun: third-degree burns; also written "third degree burns" informally.
# Example:
- _He suffered third-degree burns over 40 percent of his body._
- _Third-degree burns require immediate specialized medical care._
# Russian:
- Ожоги третьей степени.
- Самая тяжёлая степень ожога: поражаются все слои кожи и могут повреждаться глубже лежащие ткани. Обычно множественное: _third-degree burns_; один ожог — _a third-degree burn_.
