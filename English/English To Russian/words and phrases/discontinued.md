# discontinued
?
# Transcription:
- /ˌdɪskənˈtɪnjuːd/
# Meaning:
- No longer produced, sold, or available; having been stopped or ended.
- Past tense and past participle of "discontinue": to stop doing, making, or providing something.
# Usage:
- Common for products, services, models, or lines that a company no longer makes or sells. Often used before a noun: discontinued item, discontinued model, discontinued line.
- Can also describe a practice or activity that has been stopped: discontinued treatment, discontinued use.
# Example:
- _This model has been discontinued and is no longer available._
- _The company discontinued the product due to low sales._
# Russian:
- Снятый с производства; прекращённый, отменённый.
- Причастие от _discontinue_ — прекращать, снимать с производства. _Discontinued item_ — товар, снятый с производства; _discontinued model_ — модель, выпуск которой прекращён.
