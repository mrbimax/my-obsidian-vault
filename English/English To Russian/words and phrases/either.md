# either
?
# Transcription:
- BrE /ˈaɪðə(r)/ or /ˈiːðə(r)/; AmE /ˈiːðər/ or /ˈaɪðər/
# Meaning:
- One or the other of two people or things.
- Used before a choice between two possibilities: either...or...
- In negative sentences, also; too: I don't like it either.
# Usage:
- As a determiner/pronoun: either book, either of them, either one.
- As a conjunction in either...or: Either you go or I do.
- As an adverb in negatives: I don't want any either.
- Both pronunciations /ˈaɪðər/ and /ˈiːðər/ are standard.
# Example:
- _Either option is fine with me._
- _You can take either road; they both lead to the station._
- _I don't like coffee, and I don't like tea either._
# Russian:
- Любой из двух; тот или другой; либо... либо...
