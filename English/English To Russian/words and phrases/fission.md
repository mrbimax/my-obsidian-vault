# fission
?
# Transcription:
- /ˈfɪʃn/
# Meaning:
- The splitting of an atomic nucleus into two or more smaller nuclei, releasing a large amount of energy.
- A process of reproduction in which a cell or organism divides into two or more parts; binary fission.
# Usage:
- Technical and scientific. Common in physics, nuclear energy, and biology. Often used in phrases like nuclear fission, atomic fission, binary fission.
# Example:
- _Nuclear fission releases energy that can be used to generate electricity._
- _Bacteria reproduce by binary fission._
# Russian:
- Деление, расщепление (ядра); fission.
- В физике: _nuclear fission_ — деление атомного ядра. В биологии: _binary fission_ — бинарное деление. Технический термин.
