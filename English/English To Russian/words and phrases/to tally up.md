# to tally up
?
# Transcription:
- /tuː ˈtæli ʌp/
# Meaning:
- To add up numbers, amounts, or scores to get a total.
- To calculate the final total of something.
# Usage:
- A phrasal verb, often transitive and separable: tally up the numbers / tally the numbers up / tally them up. Common in informal and business contexts.
# Example:
- _Let me tally up the expenses before we decide._
- _The referee tallied up the points at the end of the match._
# Russian:
- Подсчитывать, суммировать, подводить итог.
- Часто используется о счетах, расходах, очках или голосах.
