# capectomy
?
# Transcription:
- /kæˈpɛktəmi/ or /kəˈpɛktəmi/
# Meaning:
- Not a standard English word in general dictionaries.
- If used, it is a technical or coined term meaning the surgical removal of a cap or cap-like structure.
- The suffix -ectomy means surgical removal; cap- refers to a cap or covering.
# Usage:
- Rare or nonstandard. In medicine, the standard term depends on what is being removed: capsulectomy (removal of a capsule), circumcision (removal of foreskin), etc.
- Do not use in formal writing unless defined by the context.
# Example:
- _The surgeon performed a capectomy to remove the cap-like covering._
- _Capectomy is not a standard medical term and should be replaced by the precise procedure name._
