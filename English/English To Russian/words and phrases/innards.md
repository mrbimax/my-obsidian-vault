# innards
?
# Transcription:
- /ˈɪnərdz/
# Meaning:
- The internal organs of a person or animal, especially the stomach and intestines; guts.
- The inner parts or workings of a machine, object, or system.
# Usage:
- Informal. Often used for guts or internal organs, and figuratively for the inside parts of things. Common in speech and writing that wants a slightly crude or vivid tone.
# Example:
- _The butcher cleaned the innards before weighing the meat._
- _He took apart the old radio to see its innards._
# Russian:
- Внутренности, кишки, потроха.
- В переносном смысле: внутреннее устройство, «начинка» (механизма, системы). Неформально.
