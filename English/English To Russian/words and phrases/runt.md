# runt
?
# Transcription:
- /rʌnt/
# Meaning:
- The smallest and weakest animal in a litter, especially a pig.
- A small, weak, or undersized person; often used as an insult.
# Usage:
- Literal for animals: the runt of the litter.
- Derogatory or teasing for people: don't call him a runt. Informal and potentially offensive.
# Example:
- _He was the runt of the litter and needed extra feeding._
- _The bullies called him a runt because he was short._
# Russian:
- Заморыш, последыш, слабак; самый маленький и слабый в помёте.
- Чаще всего о животном, особенно о поросёнке. О человеке — пренебрежительно или оскорбительно.
