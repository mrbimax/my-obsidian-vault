# to amuse
?
# Transcription:
- /tə əˈmjuːz/
# Meaning:
- To make someone laugh or smile; to entertain someone in a light or playful way.
- To keep someone interested or occupied, especially so they do not get bored.
# Usage:
- Common patterns: amuse someone, be amused by/at something, amuse oneself.
- Neutral and common in everyday speech. Can mean to entertain, to make someone laugh, or to keep someone busy pleasantly.
# Example:
- _The clown amused the children at the party._
- _She was amused by his silly joke._
- _He amused himself by reading old magazines._
# Russian:
- Забавлять, развлекать, смешить, веселить.
- _Be amused by/at_ — находить что-либо забавным. _Amuse oneself_ — развлекаться, занимать себя чем-либо.
