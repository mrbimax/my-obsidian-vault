# subtlety
?
# Transcription:
- /ˈsʌtəlti/
# Meaning:
- The quality of being subtle; the ability to notice or express fine distinctions.
- A small but important detail or nuance that is not obvious.
# Usage:
- Common in phrases like subtlety of meaning, subtlety of flavor, subtlety in argument, with subtlety, lack subtlety.
- Neutral to formal. Often used when talking about language, art, taste, behavior, or reasoning.
# Example:
- _The subtlety of her argument was lost on the audience._
- _He explained the subtlety of the wine's flavor._
# Russian:
- Тонкость, неуловимость, утончённость; нюанс, тонкое различие.
- Может означать как качество (тонкость), так и конкретную деталь (нюанс).
