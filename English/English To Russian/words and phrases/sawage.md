# savage
?
# Transcription:
- /ˈsævɪdʒ/
# Meaning:
- Fierce, violent, wild, or cruel; not civilized or controlled.
- (Informal slang) Excellent, impressive, or brutally good, especially in a bold or harsh way.
# Usage:
- Literal sense describes violent people, animals, or attacks. Slang sense is informal and often used about a witty comeback, a performance, or someone’s style: “That was savage.”
# Example:
- _The dog gave a savage growl and lunged at the intruder._
- _Her comeback was absolutely savage — everyone went silent._
# Russian:
- Дикий, свирепый, жестокий; зверский.
- В сленге: крутой, жёсткий, беспощадный (о шутке, ответе, стиле).
