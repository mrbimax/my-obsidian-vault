# A train of thought
?
# Transcription:
- BrE /ə treɪn əv θɔːt/; AmE /ə treɪn əv θɔːt/
# Meaning:
- A sequence of connected thoughts or ideas that follow one another in the mind.
- The continuous line of reasoning or mental focus that a person is following.
# Usage:
- Common in phrases like lose one's train of thought, interrupt someone's train of thought, follow a train of thought.
- Neutral and common in everyday speech, writing, psychology, and communication.
# Example:
- _I lost my train of thought when the phone rang._
- _Don't interrupt me — you'll break my train of thought._
- _She followed a logical train of thought to its conclusion._
# Russian:
- Ход мыслей; цепочка мыслей; нить рассуждений.
- _To lose one's train of thought_ — потерять мысль, сбиться. _To interrupt someone's train of thought_ — прервать чей-либо ход мыслей.
