# distinct
?
# Transcription:
- /dɪˈstɪŋkt/
# Meaning:
- Clearly separate and different from something else.
- Easy to see, hear, smell, or notice; clear and noticeable.
# Usage:
- Common patterns: distinct from something, a distinct possibility, a distinct advantage, a distinct sound/smell.
- Neutral and fairly formal. Often used when two or more things are clearly not the same.
# Example:
- _These two species are distinct from one another._
- _There was a distinct smell of smoke in the room._
# Russian:
- Отчётливый, ясный, явный; отдельный, различный.
- _Distinct from_ — отличный от, не совпадающий с.
