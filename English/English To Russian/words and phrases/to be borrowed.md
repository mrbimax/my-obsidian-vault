# to be borrowed
?
# Transcription:
- BrE /tə biː ˈbɒrəʊd/; AmE /tə biː ˈbɑːroʊd/
# Meaning:
- Passive infinitive of "borrow": to be taken and used temporarily, with the intention of returning it.
- Of words, ideas, customs, or designs: to be adopted or taken from another language or source.
# Usage:
- Used after modal verbs, verbs like need, want, expect, and adjectives: needs to be borrowed, is expected to be borrowed, likely to be borrowed.
- Not usually used as a standalone imperative. The active form is "to borrow"; "to be borrowed" focuses on the thing being taken.
# Example:
- _These tools are not to be borrowed without permission._
- _The design appears to be borrowed from an older model._
- _There are still many books to be borrowed from the library._
# Russian:

- Пассивный инфинитив от _borrow_. _Borrow_ — брать взаймы; не путать с _lend_ — давать взаймы.
