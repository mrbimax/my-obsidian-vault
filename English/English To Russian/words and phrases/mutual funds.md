# mutual funds
?
# Transcription:
- /ˈmjuːtʃuəl fʌndz/
# Meaning:
- Investment funds that pool money from many investors to buy a diversified portfolio of stocks, bonds, or other securities.
- A type of collective investment managed by a professional fund manager.
# Usage:
- Common in finance and investing. Usually plural: invest in mutual funds. Singular: a mutual fund.
- Common phrases: mutual fund manager, mutual fund company, index mutual fund, actively managed mutual fund.
# Example:
- _She invests in mutual funds for long-term growth._
- _A mutual fund pools money from many investors._
# Russian:
- Паевые инвестиционные фонды; взаимные фонды.
- _Mutual fund_ — паевой инвестиционный фонд; _mutual funds_ — паевые фонды. В американском контексте часто переводится как «взаимный фонд», но по смыслу ближе к ПИФ.
