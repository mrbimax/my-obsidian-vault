# invulnerable
?
# Transcription:
- /ɪnˈvʌlnərəbl/
# Meaning:
- Impossible to harm, damage, or wound; not vulnerable.
- Unable to be affected or hurt by something, especially criticism, attack, or danger.
# Usage:
- Formal and often followed by "to": invulnerable to attack, invulnerable to criticism.
- Can describe a person, place, system, or position. Stronger and more absolute than just "safe".
# Example:
- _The fortress seemed invulnerable to attack._
- _He acted as if he were invulnerable to criticism._
# Russian:
- Неуязвимый, невосприимчивый; защищённый от вреда.
- Часто с _to_: _invulnerable to something_ — неуязвимый для чего-либо. Не путать с _invincible_ — непобедимый.
