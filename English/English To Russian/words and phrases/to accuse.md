# to accuse
?
# Transcription:
- /tə əˈkjuːz/
# Meaning:
- To say that someone has done something wrong, illegal, or blameworthy.
- To charge someone with an offense or fault, often formally or publicly.
# Usage:
- Common pattern: accuse someone of something / of doing something.
- Neutral to formal. Can be used in everyday arguments, news reports, and legal contexts.
# Example:
- _She accused him of stealing her idea._
- _He was accused of murder and put on trial._
# Russian:
- Обвинять, предъявлять обвинение.
- Управление: _accuse someone of something_ — обвинять кого-либо в чём-либо. Не _accuse for_.
