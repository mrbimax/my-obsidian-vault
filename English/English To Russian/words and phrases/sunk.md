# sunk
?
# Transcription:
- /sʌŋk/
# Meaning:
- Past participle of "sink": having gone down below the surface of water or another liquid.
- (Informal) Ruined, doomed, or in a hopeless situation.
- Deeply absorbed or immersed in something, as in "sunk in thought".
# Usage:
- As a verb form, used in perfect tenses and passive: has sunk, had sunk, was sunk.
- As an adjective, common in informal speech: we're sunk, he's sunk.
- Also used in "sunk cost" (money already spent and impossible to recover) and "sunk in" (deeply involved or absorbed).
# Example:
- _The ship has sunk to the bottom of the sea._
- _If we miss this deadline, we're sunk._
- _He sat sunk in thought, staring at the floor._
