# multiple aggravated homicide
?
# Transcription:
- /ˈmʌltɪpl ˈæɡrəveɪtɪd ˈhɒmɪsaɪd/
# Meaning:
- The killing of more than one person under circumstances that make the crime more serious, such as cruelty, premeditation, or another aggravating factor.
- A legal phrase describing multiple homicide offenses that are each classified as aggravated.
# Usage:
- Formal legal and news contexts. Often appears as "multiple counts of aggravated homicide" or "multiple aggravated homicides", because "aggravated homicide" is usually a charge or category, not a single fixed offense in every jurisdiction.
# Example:
- _He was charged with multiple counts of aggravated homicide._
- _The court heard evidence of multiple aggravated homicides committed over several years._
# Russian:
- Множественное отягчённое убийство; убийство нескольких лиц при отягчающих обстоятельствах.
- Часто точнее: несколько эпизодов отягчённого убийства; несколько пунктов обвинения в отягчённом убийстве.
