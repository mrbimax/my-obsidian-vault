# persuasion
?
# Transcription:
- /pərˈsweɪʒn/
# Meaning:
- The act of persuading someone or the process of being persuaded.
- A belief, opinion, or set of beliefs, especially religious or political.
# Usage:
- Common in communication, psychology, politics, and sales. Often in phrases: powers of persuasion, gentle persuasion, political persuasion.
- Neutral and fairly formal. Not the same as "coercion": persuasion relies on argument or influence, not force.
# Example:
- _She used her powers of persuasion to change his mind._
- _People of all political persuasions attended the meeting._
# Russian:
- Убеждение, уговоры; убедительность.
- В значении «убеждения» (взгляды, вера): _political persuasion_ — политические убеждения. _Powers of persuasion_ — дар убеждения.
