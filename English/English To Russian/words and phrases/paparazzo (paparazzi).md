# paparazzo (paparazzi)
?
# Transcription:
- paparazzo: /ˌpæpəˈrætsoʊ/ (AmE), /ˌpæpəˈrætsəʊ/ (BrE)
- paparazzi: /ˌpæpəˈrætsi/
# Meaning:
- Paparazzo is singular: one freelance photographer who follows celebrities to take candid photos.
- Paparazzi is plural: two or more such photographers. It is not a singular noun in standard English.
# Usage:
- Use "a paparazzo" for one photographer; "the paparazzi" for the group.
- In Italian, paparazzi is plural; in English, it is often treated as a plural collective. Avoid "a paparazzi" in careful English.
# Example:
- _A paparazzo was waiting outside the hotel._
- _The paparazzi surrounded the actress as she left the restaurant._
# Russian:
- Папараццо — единственное число; папарацци — множественное.
- В русском языке слово «папарацци» обычно используется и для одного, и для нескольких фотографов. В английском же лучше различать: a paparazzo (один), the paparazzi (много).
