# obedience
?
# Transcription:
- /əˈbiːdiəns/
# Meaning:
- The act of obeying; compliance with an order, request, law, or authority.
- Willingness to submit to someone else's rules or commands.
# Usage:
- Formal or neutral. Common in psychology, law, military, religion, and training animals. Often followed by "to": obedience to authority, obedience to the law.
# Example:
- _The dog's obedience was impressive._
- _Soldiers are trained in strict obedience to orders._
# Russian:
- Послушание, повиновение, подчинение.
- Может означать как добровольное следование, так и вынужденное подчинение власти.
