# accompanied
?
# Transcription:
- /əˈkʌmpənid/
# Meaning:
- Past tense and past participle of "accompany".
- Went somewhere with someone; escorted or joined by someone.
- Happened or existed at the same time as something else; often used as "accompanied by".
- Played music to support a singer or instrumentalist.
# Usage:
- Common patterns: accompanied by someone, accompanied by something, accompanied someone to a place, accompanied on the piano.
- Neutral and common. Often passive: was accompanied by.
# Example:
- _She was accompanied by her brother to the ceremony._
- _The storm was accompanied by strong winds._
- _He accompanied her on the piano._
# Russian:
- Сопровождаемый, в сопровождении; сопутствующий; аккомпанировал.
- _Accompanied by_ — в сопровождении кого/чего-либо; вместе с чем-либо.
- Как причастие от _accompany_ — сопровождать, сопутствовать; аккомпанировать.
