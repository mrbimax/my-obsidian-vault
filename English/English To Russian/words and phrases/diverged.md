# diverged
?
# Transcription:
- /daɪˈvɜːdʒd/
# Meaning:
- Past tense and past participle of "diverge".
- Separated and went in different directions; became different or deviated.
# Usage:
- Used for roads, paths, routes, opinions, interests, or courses of action that split or become different.
- Common patterns: diverge from something, diverge into something, paths diverged.
# Example:
- _The two roads diverged in the woods, and we took the left one._
- _Their opinions diverged sharply after the meeting._
# Russian:
- Разошлись, разветвились, отклонились; стали разными.
- _Diverge_ — расходиться, отклоняться; _diverged_ — прошедшая форма.
