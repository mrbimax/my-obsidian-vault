# quite
?
# Transcription:
- /kwaɪt/
# Meaning:
- To a considerable degree; fairly, rather, or very.
- Completely or entirely, especially in British English with certain adjectives.
# Usage:
- In British English, "quite" often means "fairly" or "rather" before gradable adjectives: quite good = fairly good.
- Before non-gradable adjectives or verbs, it can mean "completely": quite impossible, quite sure.
- In American English, it often means "very" or "completely": quite good = very good.
# Example:
- _The film was quite good, but not amazing._ (BrE: fairly good)
- _Are you quite sure you locked the door?_ (completely sure)
- _She's quite talented._ (AmE: very talented)
# Russian:
- Довольно, вполне, совсем, очень; иногда совершенно.
- В британском английском часто «довольно» (умеренно), в американском чаще «очень» или «совершенно».
