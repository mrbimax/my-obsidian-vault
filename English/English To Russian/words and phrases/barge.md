# barge
?
# Transcription:
- BrE /bɑːdʒ/; AmE /bɑːrdʒ/
# Meaning:
- A large, flat-bottomed boat used for carrying goods on rivers and canals.
- (Verb) To move clumsily or forcefully; usually in "barge in/into" = to enter rudely or interrupt.
# Usage:
- As a noun, common for freight boats on canals and rivers.
- As a verb, often followed by in or into: barge into a room, barge in on a conversation.
# Example:
- _The barge carried coal along the canal._
- _He barged into the meeting without knocking._
# Russian:
- Баржа; грузовое судно.
- Как глагол: вломиться, ворваться, грубо вмешаться (обычно _barge in/into_).
