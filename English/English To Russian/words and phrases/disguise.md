# disguise
?
# Transcription:
- /dɪsˈɡaɪz/
# Meaning:
- (Noun) A change in appearance or behavior to hide one's identity.
- (Verb) To change one's appearance or manner to hide who one is; to conceal or misrepresent.
# Usage:
- Common as both noun and verb. Patterns: in disguise, wear a disguise, disguise oneself as, disguise the fact that...
- Neutral. Used for costumes, undercover work, deception, and hiding feelings or truth.
- Common idiom: a blessing in disguise — something that seems bad but turns out to be good.
# Example:
- _He wore a disguise so no one would recognize him._
- _She disguised herself as a waiter to enter the building._
- _The company tried to disguise its losses._
# Russian:
- Маскировка, переодевание, маска; маскировать, скрывать, замаскировывать.
- _In disguise_ — замаскированный, переодетый. _A blessing in disguise_ — не было бы счастья, да несчастье помогло.
