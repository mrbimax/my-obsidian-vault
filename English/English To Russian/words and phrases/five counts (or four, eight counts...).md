# five counts _(or four, eight counts...)_
?
# Transcription:
- /faɪv kaʊnts/
# Meaning:
- In legal contexts, five separate charges or allegations listed in an indictment, complaint, or court case.
- Each "count" is a distinct offense the defendant is accused of committing.
# Usage:
- Formal legal and news usage. Often followed by "of": five counts of fraud, five counts of murder.
- Common phrases: charged with five counts, convicted on five counts, pleaded guilty to five counts.
# Example:
- _He was charged with five counts of fraud._
- _The jury convicted her on five counts._
# Russian:
- Пять пунктов обвинения; пять эпизодов или составов преступления.
- _Count_ здесь — не «считать», а «пункт обвинения» в уголовном деле.
