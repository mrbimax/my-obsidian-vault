# popped
?
# Transcription:
- /pɒpt/
# Meaning:
- Past tense and past participle of "pop".
- Went somewhere quickly or briefly; made a short sharp sound; burst suddenly.
# Usage:
- Informal. Common in phrases like popped in, popped out, popped round, popped up.
- Also used for a sudden bursting sound or action: the balloon popped, my ears popped.
# Example:
- _He popped out to buy milk and hasn't come back yet._
- _The champagne cork popped loudly._
# Russian:
- Заскочил, быстро сходил, заглянул; хлопнул, лопнул.
- В зависимости от контекста: «выскочил», «забежал», «отскочил». Также о звуке: «хлопнул», «щёлкнул».
