# dissolving
?
# Transcription:
- BrE /dɪˈzɒlvɪŋ/; AmE /dɪˈzɑːlvɪŋ/
# Meaning:
- Becoming liquid or passing into a liquid; causing something to become liquid.
- Disappearing gradually or fading away.
- Ending or bringing something to an end officially, such as a company, parliament, or marriage.
# Usage:
- Common as the -ing form of "dissolve": sugar dissolving in water, dissolving a tablet, dissolving a company.
- As an adjective, it often describes something that is gradually disappearing or breaking down: dissolving boundaries, dissolving into laughter.
- Neutral and common in science, business, law, and everyday contexts.
# Example:
- _The sugar is dissolving in the hot tea._
- _The tablet is dissolving in water._
- _The parliament was dissolved before the election._
- _She burst out laughing, dissolving into giggles._
