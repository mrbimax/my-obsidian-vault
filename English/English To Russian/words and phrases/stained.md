# stained
?
# Transcription:
- /steɪnd/
# Meaning:
- Marked or discolored with a stain; not clean or not spotless.
- Tainted or damaged in reputation; morally or figuratively marked.
- Past tense and past participle of "stain".
# Usage:
- Common before a noun or after a linking verb: a stained shirt, the carpet was stained.
- Often used with "with" or "by": stained with coffee, stained by grease.
- Figurative use: a stained reputation, stained hands.
# Example:
- _His shirt was stained with coffee._
- _The old carpet was badly stained._
- _The scandal left his reputation stained._
