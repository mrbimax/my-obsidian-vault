# no hard feelings
?
# Transcription:
- /noʊ hɑːrd ˈfiːlɪŋz/
# Meaning:
- Used to say that you are not angry or upset with someone after an argument, disagreement, or disappointing situation.
- It expresses a desire to remain friendly and not hold a grudge.
# Usage:
- Informal and common in everyday speech. Often said after an argument, a competition, or when parting ways. Can be a question: "No hard feelings?" or a statement: "No hard feelings."
# Example:
- _We argued about it, but no hard feelings, okay?_
- _I'm sorry I beat you in the final. No hard feelings?_
# Russian:
- Без обид; не держи зла; без злобы.
- Обычно говорится, чтобы показать, что обида или гнев прошли и ты не сердишься.
