# lousy
?
# Transcription:
- /ˈlaʊzi/
# Meaning:
- Very bad or of poor quality; unpleasant.
- (Informal) Feeling ill or unwell.
- (Literal, rare) Infested with lice.
# Usage:
- Informal and negative. Common before nouns: a lousy job, lousy weather, lousy service. Also after linking verbs: I feel lousy.
# Example:
- _The weather was lousy, so we stayed inside._
- _I feel lousy today — I think I'm getting sick._
# Russian:
- Паршивый, отвратительный, скверный; вшивый (букв.).
- В разговорной речи часто о плохом качестве или самочувствии. _I feel lousy_ — я плохо себя чувствую.
