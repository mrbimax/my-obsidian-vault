# to implicate
?
# Transcription:
- /tə ˈɪmplɪkeɪt/
# Meaning:
- To show or suggest that someone is involved in a crime, wrongdoing, or something bad.
- To imply or involve something as a necessary part or consequence.
# Usage:
- Common patterns: implicate someone in something, be implicated in a scandal/crime.
- Formal/neutral. Often used in legal, political, and news contexts.
# Example:
- _The evidence implicated him in the robbery._
- _Several officials were implicated in the corruption scandal._
# Russian:
- Вовлекать, впутывать; указывать на причастность кого-либо (к преступлению, скандалу).
- Также: подразумевать, влечь за собой. _Implicate someone in something_ — указать на чью-либо причастность к чему-либо.
