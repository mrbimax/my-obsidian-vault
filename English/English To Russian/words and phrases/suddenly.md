# suddenly
?
# Transcription:
- /ˈsʌdənli/
# Meaning:
- Quickly and unexpectedly; without warning.
- Happening in a way that is surprising because it was not planned or expected.
# Usage:
- Common adverb used to describe an action or event that happens fast and without warning.
- Often placed before the verb or at the beginning of a sentence: Suddenly, the lights went out. / He suddenly stopped.
# Example:
- _Suddenly, the door flew open._
- _She suddenly realized she had forgotten her keys._
# Russian:
- Внезапно, вдруг, неожиданно.
- О действии или событии, которое происходит быстро и без предупреждения. Часто стоит в начале предложения или перед глаголом.
