# conscious
?
# Transcription:
- BrE /ˈkɒnʃəs/; AmE /ˈkɑːnʃəs/
# Meaning:
- Aware of and responding to one's surroundings; awake.
- Aware of something; noticing or realizing something.
# Usage:
- Common patterns: conscious of something, conscious that..., self-conscious, health-conscious, environmentally conscious.
- Neutral. Can describe a person's state (awake) or awareness of a fact.
# Example:
- _She was conscious of someone watching her._
- _He was barely conscious after the accident._
- _I'm very conscious of the need to save money._
# Russian:
- Сознающий, в сознании; осознающий, отдающий себе отчёт.
- _Conscious of_ — осознающий что-либо; _self-conscious_ — застенчивый, стесняющийся; _health-conscious_ — следящий за здоровьем.
