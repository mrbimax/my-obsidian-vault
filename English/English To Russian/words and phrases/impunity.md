# impunity
?
# Transcription:
- /ɪmˈpjuːnəti/
# Meaning:
- Freedom from punishment, harm, or unpleasant consequences.
- The ability to do something wrong or illegal without being punished.
# Usage:
- Formal and common in legal, political, and news contexts. Usually in the phrase "with impunity": to do something with impunity.
# Example:
- _The gang operated with impunity for years._
- _No one should be allowed to break the law with impunity._
# Russian:
- Безнаказанность; свобода от наказания или последствий.
- _With impunity_ — безнаказанно.
