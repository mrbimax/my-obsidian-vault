# duct-taped
?
# Transcription:
- /ˈdʌkt teɪpt/
# Meaning:
- Fastened, repaired, or covered with duct tape.
- Past tense and past participle of "duct-tape"; also used as an adjective.
# Usage:
- Common for makeshift or quick repairs, especially in everyday, DIY, and emergency situations.
- Patterns: duct-taped together, duct-taped to something, get duct-taped.
# Example:
- _The broken window was duct-taped shut._
- _He duct-taped the box to keep it from opening._
# Russian:
- Заклеенный скотчем; обмотанный армированной лентой.
- _Duct tape_ — армированная клейкая лента, часто «скотч» в быту. _Duct-taped_ — заклеенный или скреплённый такой лентой.
