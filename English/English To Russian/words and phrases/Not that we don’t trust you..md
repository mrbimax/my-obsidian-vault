# Not that we don’t trust you.
?
# Transcription:
- BrE /nɒt ðæt wiː dəʊnt trʌst juː/; AmE /nɑːt ðæt wiː doʊnt trʌst juː/
# Meaning:
- Used to soften a statement that might otherwise sound like an accusation of dishonesty or incompetence.
- Literally: We are not saying that we do not trust you. It usually introduces a "but..." clause.
# Usage:
- Informal to neutral. Often followed by "but..." or used before asking for proof, checking someone's work, or setting a rule.
- It tries to prevent the other person from feeling offended or distrusted.
# Example:
- _Not that we don’t trust you, but we need to double-check the numbers._
- _Not that we don’t trust you — it’s just company policy._
# Russian:
- Не то чтобы мы тебе не доверяли.
- Обычно смягчает последующее «но...»: «Не то чтобы мы не доверяли, но...» / «Дело не в том, что мы тебе не доверяем, но...»
