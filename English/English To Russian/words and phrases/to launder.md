# to launder
?
# Transcription:
- /tə ˈlɔːndə(r)/
# Meaning:
- To wash and iron clothes, linen, or other fabrics.
- To move money obtained illegally through legitimate businesses or accounts to hide its origin.
# Usage:
- Literal sense is common in British English for washing clothes. Financial sense is formal/legal, usually in "launder money" or "money laundering".
# Example:
- _She laundered the sheets and hung them to dry._
- _The gang used a chain of restaurants to launder drug money._
# Russian:
- Стирать и гладить (бельё).
- Отмывать (деньги), легализовать незаконные доходы.
