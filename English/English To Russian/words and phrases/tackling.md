# tackling
?
# Transcription:
- /ˈtæklɪŋ/
# Meaning:
- Present participle of "tackle": dealing with a problem, task, or difficult situation in a determined way.
- In sports, intercepting or stopping an opponent, especially in football, rugby, or hockey.
- The act of confronting or struggling with something difficult.
# Usage:
- Common as a gerund or participle: tackling a problem, tackling climate change, tackling an opponent.
- Neutral and common in everyday, business, academic, and sports contexts.
# Example:
- _She is tackling the biggest issue in the report._
- _The government promised to start tackling unemployment._
- _He was sent off for tackling the striker too roughly._
# Russian:
- Решение, преодоление; борьба с чем-либо.
- В спорте: захват, отбор мяча, единоборство.
- Причастие/герундий от _tackle_ — браться за решение, бороться; в спорте — атаковать, отбирать мяч.
