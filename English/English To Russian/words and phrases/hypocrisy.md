# hypocrisy
?
# Transcription:
- /hɪˈpɒkrəsi/
# Meaning:
- The practice of claiming to have moral standards or beliefs to which one's own behavior does not conform.
- Pretending to be what one is not, or saying one thing and doing another.
# Usage:
- Used to criticize someone who acts in contradiction to their stated values or beliefs.
# Example:
- _His criticism of others for being dishonest was pure hypocrisy, since he lied constantly._
- _The politician's hypocrisy was exposed when his private actions contradicted his public speeches._
# Russian:
- Лицемерие; ханжество; двуличие.
- Буквально: показная добродетель, не соответствующая реальному поведению.
