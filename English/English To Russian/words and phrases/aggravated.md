# aggravated
?
# Transcription:
- /ˈæɡrəveɪtɪd/
# Meaning:
- Made worse or more serious; (of a crime) made more serious by additional circumstances.
- (Informal) annoyed or irritated.
- Past tense and past participle of "aggravate".
# Usage:
- Legal/formal: aggravated assault, aggravated circumstances.
- General: a condition aggravated by stress or cold.
- Informal: I'm so aggravated by this noise.
# Example:
- _His injury was aggravated by walking too soon._
- _She was clearly aggravated by all the delays._
- _He was charged with aggravated assault._
# Russian:
- Ухудшенный, обострённый; отягчённый (о преступлении).
- Раздражённый, разозлённый (разг.).
- Причастие от _aggravate_ — ухудшать, обострять; раздражать.
