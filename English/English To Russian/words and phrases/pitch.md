# pitch
?
# Transcription:
- /pɪtʃ/
# Meaning:
- (Noun) A playing field in sports, especially in British English: a football pitch.
- (Noun) The highness or lowness of a sound or musical note.
- (Noun) A persuasive talk or sales presentation: a sales pitch.
- (Noun) A black, sticky substance like tar; bitumen.
- (Verb) To throw something with force.
- (Verb) To set up a tent or camp.
- (Verb) To fall or move heavily forward.
# Usage:
- Sports sense is common in BrE; AmE usually uses "field".
- Music sense: perfect pitch, high/low pitch.
- Business sense: make a pitch, pitch an idea.
- Verb throw: pitch a ball; set up: pitch a tent; fall: pitched forward.
# Example:
- _The players ran onto the pitch._
- _She has perfect pitch and can name any note._
- _He made a strong pitch for the new product._
- _They pitched their tent by the river._
- _He pitched the ball to the batter._
# Russian:
- Поле (спортивное, брит.); высота звука, тон; презентация, рекламная речь; бросок, подача; смола, битум.
- Как глагол: бросать, подавать; ставить палатку; падать, валиться. Многозначное слово, выбор значения зависит от контекста.
