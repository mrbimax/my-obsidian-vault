# nabbed
?
# Transcription:
- /næbd/
# Meaning:
- Past tense and past participle of "nab".
- Caught, arrested, or seized someone, often suddenly.
- Grabbed, took, or stole something quickly.
# Usage:
- Informal. Common in crime, police, and everyday contexts: he was nabbed by police; she nabbed the last ticket.
# Example:
- _The thief was nabbed by police outside the store._
- _She nabbed the last seat by the window._
# Russian:
- Поймали, сцапали, задержали; схватил, стащил.
- _Nab_ — разговорное: поймать, задержать, цапнуть. _Nabbed_ — прошедшая форма.
