# leverage
?
# Transcription:
- BrE /ˈliːvərɪdʒ/; AmE /ˈlevərɪdʒ/
# Meaning:
- (Noun) The power to influence people, decisions, or situations to get what you want.
- (Noun) The use of borrowed money to increase potential return, especially in finance.
- (Noun) The mechanical advantage gained by using a lever.
- (Verb) To use something to maximum advantage; to influence or exploit.
# Usage:
- Business/politics: gain leverage, have leverage over someone, use something as leverage.
- Finance: financial leverage, high leverage, leverage ratio.
- Physics: a lever provides leverage.
- Verb: leverage resources, leverage technology, leverage one's connections.
# Example:
- _She used her connections as leverage to get the deal approved._
- _The company took on too much leverage and struggled to repay its debts._
- _They leveraged new technology to improve efficiency._

- В финансах: заёмные средства, финансовый рычаг. Как глагол: использовать с максимальной выгодой, задействовать.
