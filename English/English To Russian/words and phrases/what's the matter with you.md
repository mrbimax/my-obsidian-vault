# what's the matter with you
?
# Transcription:
- /wʌts ðə ˈmætər wɪð juː/
# Meaning:
- An expression used to ask why someone is upset, behaving strangely, or having a problem.
- Can show concern, confusion, or annoyance.
# Usage:
- Common in everyday speech. Tone decides whether it sounds caring, puzzled, or irritated.
# Example:
- _What's the matter with you? You've been quiet all evening._
- _What's the matter with you? That was completely out of line._
# Russian:
- Что с тобой? Что случилось?
- В раздражённом тоне: «Что на тебя нашло?» / «Ты что, с ума сошёл?» (по контексту).
