# frightening
?
# Transcription:
- /ˈfraɪtnɪŋ/
# Meaning:
- Making someone feel afraid or anxious; causing fear.
- Very alarming or disturbing.
# Usage:
- Used to describe something that causes fear: a frightening experience, a frightening thought, it is frightening to...
- Can describe situations, events, stories, or possibilities. Often followed by "to" + verb or "for" + person.
# Example:
- _The storm was frightening for the children._
- _It's frightening to think how easily it could have happened._
# Russian:
- Пугающий, страшный, внушающий страх.
- Может описывать событие, зрелище, мысль или ситуацию. Не путать с _frightened_ — испуганный (о человеке). Frightening вызывает страх, frightened испытывает страх.
