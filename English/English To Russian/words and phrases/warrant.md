# warrant
?
# Transcription:
- /ˈwɒrənt/ (BrE), /ˈwɔːrənt/ (AmE)
# Meaning:
- (Noun) A legal document from a judge or court that allows police to do something, such as arrest someone or search a place.
- (Verb) To justify, require, or make something necessary or appropriate.
# Usage:
- As a noun, common in legal and law enforcement contexts: arrest warrant, search warrant, bench warrant.
- As a verb, formal: the situation warrants further investigation; his behavior does not warrant such punishment.
# Example:
- _The police obtained a search warrant for the suspect's apartment._
- _The evidence does not warrant a criminal trial._
