# looseleaf
?
# Transcription:
- /ˈluːs liːf/
# Meaning:
- Describing paper, a binder, or a book whose pages can be removed or added individually.
- Usually written with a hyphen: loose-leaf. The one-word form looseleaf is also used, especially in product names.
# Usage:
- Common before nouns: looseleaf paper, looseleaf binder, looseleaf notebook.
- Neutral and common in stationery, school, and office contexts.
# Example:
- _She bought a looseleaf binder for her notes._
- _The recipe book comes in a looseleaf format so you can add pages._
# Russian:
- Съёмный, разъёмный; с отдельными листами; на кольцах.
- О бумаге, папке или тетради, страницы которых можно вынимать и вставлять. Чаще пишется _loose-leaf_.
