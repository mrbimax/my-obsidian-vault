# to persuade
?
# Transcription:
- /tə pəˈsweɪd/
# Meaning:
- To cause someone to do or believe something through reasoning or argument.
- To convince someone to take a particular action or adopt a certain view.
# Usage:
- Common patterns: persuade someone to do something, persuade someone of something, persuade someone that...
# Example:
- _She persuaded him to apply for the job._
- _I persuaded her that it was the right decision._
# Russian:
- Убеждать, уговаривать; склонять к чему-либо.
- Отличается от "convince": persuade часто подразумевает действие, а не только изменение мнения.
