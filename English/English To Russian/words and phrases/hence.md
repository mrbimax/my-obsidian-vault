# hence
?
# Transcription:
- /hens/
# Meaning:
- For this reason; as a consequence of this fact.
- From this time; from now (archaic or literary).
- From this place; from here (archaic).
# Usage:
- Formal and common in academic, legal, and logical writing. Often used to introduce a conclusion or result: hence the delay, hence the name, hence it follows that.
- In older or literary English, it can mean "from here" or "from now": go hence, henceforth.
# Example:
- _The roads were icy; hence the accident._
- _He grew up in a small town, hence his accent._
- _Hence, we can conclude that the theory is correct._
# Russian:
- Следовательно, поэтому, отсюда; с этого времени (уст.); отсюда (уст.).
- В формальном контексте _hence_ вводит следствие или вывод. В устаревшем или литературном языке: отсюда, с этого времени.
