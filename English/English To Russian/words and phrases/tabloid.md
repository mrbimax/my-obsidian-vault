# tabloid
?
# Transcription:
- /ˈtæblɔɪd/
# Meaning:
- A newspaper with a small page size, often focused on sensational stories, celebrity gossip, and scandals.
- Relating to such newspapers or their style: tabloid journalism, tabloid press.
# Usage:
- Common in media and journalism. "The tabloids" means the popular sensational press.
- Often used critically or dismissively: tabloid headlines, tabloid tactics.
- Can be a noun or an adjective before a noun.
# Example:
- _The story was all over the tabloids the next day._
- _He accused the tabloid press of ruining his reputation._
# Russian:
- Таблоид; бульварная газета; малоформатная газета с сенсационными материалами.
- Как прилагательное: таблоидный, бульварный (о прессе). В русском «таблоид» часто означает именно бульварное издание.
