# I wouldn't know!
?
# Transcription:
- /aɪ ˈwʊdnt noʊ/
# Meaning:
- Used to say that you have no knowledge or experience of something and therefore cannot answer or comment.
- Often implies that the subject is not part of your world, habits, or interests; can sound dismissive, defensive, or slightly sarcastic.
# Usage:
- Informal and common in conversation. Often used as a short reply to a question about something you have never experienced or would not be expected to know about.
- Tone matters: it can be neutral, dry, defensive, or sarcastic depending on context.
# Example:
- _What's it like to be rich? I wouldn't know._
- _Does he always act like that? I wouldn't know — I just met him._
- _Is that restaurant any good? I wouldn't know, I've never been there._
# Russian:
- Откуда мне знать; понятия не имею; я не в курсе.
- Часто звучит с оттенком «это не ко мне» или «мне это незнакомо». Буквально: «Я бы не знал», но по-русски так обычно не говорят.
