# But what do I find
?
# Transcription:
- /bʌt wɒt duː aɪ faɪnd/
# Meaning:
- A rhetorical question used to introduce a surprising, disappointing, or ironic discovery in a story or anecdote.
- Literally means "But what do I discover/find?" though it is not a real question.
# Usage:
- Used in conversational or narrative style, often humorous or dramatic. Usually followed by the unexpected thing the speaker found.
- Common in first-person anecdotes: "I went there, and what do I find?"
# Example:
- _I opened the fridge for a snack. But what do I find? An empty shelf and a note saying "Sorry."_
- _I came home early to surprise him. But what do I find? He's already asleep on the couch._
# Russian:
- А что я обнаруживаю? / А что я вижу? / И что же я нахожу?
- Буквально: «Но что я нахожу?» Это риторический вопрос, который вводит неожиданную или разочаровывающую находку.
