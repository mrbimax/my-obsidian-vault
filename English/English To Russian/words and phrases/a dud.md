# a dud
?
# Transcription:
- /ə dʌd/
# Meaning:
- (Informal) A thing that fails to work, is defective, or is disappointing.
- A bomb, firework, or missile that fails to explode or function.
# Usage:
- Common for products, movies, plans, fireworks, and bombs: the movie was a dud, the firework was a dud.
- Informal and often slightly dismissive.
# Example:
- _The new phone turned out to be a dud._
- _One of the fireworks was a dud and didn't go off._
# Russian:
- Неудачная вещь, провал, осечка; неразорвавшийся снаряд или петарда.
- Разг.: «пустышка», «неудачная затея». О фейерверке: «петарда не сработала».
