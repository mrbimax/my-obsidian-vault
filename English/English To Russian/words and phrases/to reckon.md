# to reckon
?
# Transcription:
- /tə ˈrekən/
# Meaning:
- To think, believe, or suppose something; to have an opinion.
- To calculate or estimate an amount, number, or value.
- To expect or anticipate something.
# Usage:
- Common in informal British and American English: I reckon it's going to rain.
- Also formal or literary for calculating: reckon the cost, reckon without something.
- Common patterns: reckon (that)..., reckon on/upon, reckon with, reckon without.
# Example:
- _I reckon we should leave now._
- _They reckon the repairs will cost about a thousand dollars._
- _You should reckon with the possibility of delays._
# Russian:
- Считать, полагать, думать; рассчитывать, подсчитывать; учитывать.
- _I reckon_ — разг. «я думаю», «по-моему», «я считаю». _Reckon on/upon_ — рассчитывать на; _reckon with_ — считаться с, учитывать; _reckon without_ — не учесть.
