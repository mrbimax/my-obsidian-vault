# hand-over
?
# Transcription:
- /ˈhændˌəʊvə(r)/ (noun)
- /hænd ˈəʊvə(r)/ (phrasal verb)
# Meaning:
- (Noun) The act of transferring power, responsibility, possession, or control to someone else.
- (Phrasal verb, usually written "hand over") To give something to someone, especially officially or after resistance.
# Usage:
- Standard spelling: the verb is two words, "hand over"; the noun is usually one word, "handover". The hyphenated form "hand-over" often appears as an attributive adjective or as a British variant.
- Common in business, politics, law enforcement, and formal contexts: a hand-over of power, the hand-over ceremony, hand over the documents.
# Example:
- _The hand-over of power took place peacefully._
- _He refused to hand over the documents to the investigators._
- _The hand-over ceremony was attended by both leaders._
# Russian:
- Передача (власти, дел, ответственности); передача, вручение.
- Как глагол: передавать, вручать, сдавать (что-либо кому-либо). В стандартном написании глагол обычно пишется раздельно: _hand over_.
