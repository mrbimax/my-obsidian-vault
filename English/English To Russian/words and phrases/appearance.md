# appearance
?
# Transcription:
- /əˈpɪərəns/
# Meaning:
- The way that someone or something looks or seems to other people.
- An act of coming into view or being present.
# Usage:
- Common when talking about physical looks, style, or the impression someone makes. Also used for the moment something becomes visible.
# Example:
- _She was very concerned about her appearance before the interview._
- _The sudden appearance of the ship on the horizon surprised everyone._
# Russian:
- Внешность, внешний вид; появление.
- В зависимости от контекста: наружность, облик; возникновение, появление.
