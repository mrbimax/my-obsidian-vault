# to stall
?
# Transcription:
- /tə stɔːl/
# Meaning:
- To delay or stop making progress, often intentionally, especially to avoid doing something or giving an answer.
- Of an engine or vehicle: to stop running suddenly, usually unintentionally.
# Usage:
- For delaying, common patterns: stall for time, stall on a decision, stop stalling.
- For engines, used about cars, planes, and machines: the engine stalled, the car stalled at the lights.
# Example:
- _Stop stalling and give me an answer._
- _The car stalled at the intersection._
# Russian:
- Медлить, тянуть, оттягивать; затягивать (с решением).
- Глохнуть (о двигателе); заглохнуть.
