# allegiance
?
# Transcription:
- /əˈliːdʒəns/
# Meaning:
- Loyalty or commitment to a person, group, country, or cause.
- The duty of support and obedience owed to a ruler, government, or flag.
# Usage:
- Formal and often used with "to": allegiance to the crown, allegiance to a country, pledge allegiance.
- Common in political, legal, historical, and patriotic contexts. Usually uncountable.
# Example:
- _They swore allegiance to the new government._
- _His allegiance to the company never wavered._
# Russian:
- Верность, преданность; присяга, allegiance.
- Обычно с _to_: _allegiance to..._ — верность кому/чему-либо. _Pledge allegiance_ — присягать на верность. Формально.
