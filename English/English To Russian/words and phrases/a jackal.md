# a jackal
?
# Transcription:
- BrE /ˈdʒækɔːl/; AmE /ˈdʒækəl/
# Meaning:
- A wild dog-like animal found in Africa and Asia, often hunting in packs and known for scavenging.
- A person who does dishonest or unpleasant work for someone else, especially in a servile or opportunistic way.
# Usage:
- Literal sense: common in nature, wildlife, and animal contexts.
- Figurative sense: negative and often used in politics or business to describe someone who serves a more powerful person.
# Example:
- _A pack of jackals scavenged around the campsite at night._
- _He was accused of being a jackal who did the dirty work for his boss._
# Russian:
- Шакал.
- В переносном смысле: прислужник, лакей, подхалим; тот, кто выполняет грязную работу за других.
