# pledge
?
# Transcription:
- /pledʒ/
# Meaning:
- A serious or formal promise to do something or to give something.
- (Verb) To make a serious or formal promise; to promise to give or do something.
# Usage:
- Common as noun and verb. Often followed by "to": a pledge to help, pledge to support, pledge allegiance.
- Formal to neutral. Used in politics, law, charities, relationships, and everyday promises.
# Example:
- _She made a pledge to donate half her salary to charity._
- _He pledged to support the new policy._
- _The government pledged millions in aid._
# Russian:
- Обещание, обязательство; залог, поручительство.
- Как глагол: обещать, давать обещание, обязаться; закладывать (вещь). _Pledge allegiance_ — присягать на верность.
