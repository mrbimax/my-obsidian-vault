# mob turf
?
# Transcription:
- /mɑːb tɜːrf/
# Meaning:
- Territory or area controlled by a criminal organization, especially the Mafia or a gang.
- "Mob" means an organized crime group; "turf" means an area someone claims as their own.
# Usage:
- Informal, common in crime stories, news, and gang contexts. Often used in phrases like fight over mob turf, control mob turf, protect one's turf.
# Example:
- _The two families went to war over mob turf in the city._
- _Police say the shooting was part of a dispute over mob turf._
# Russian:
- Территория мафии; бандитская территория; «зона влияния» мафии.
- Буквально: «дёрн мафии», но _turf_ здесь — сленговое «территория, район влияния».
