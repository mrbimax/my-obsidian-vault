# to bump someone
?
# Transcription:
- /tə bʌmp ˈsʌmwʌn/
# Meaning:
- To hit or knock against someone, usually accidentally.
- Of an airline: to deny a passenger a seat on an overbooked flight.
# Usage:
- For physical contact, often with a shoulder, arm, or body; can be accidental or deliberate.
- For airlines, usually passive: be bumped from a flight.
# Example:
- _I accidentally bumped someone in the crowded subway._
- _The airline bumped several passengers because the flight was overbooked._
# Russian:
- Толкнуть, задеть, наткнуться на кого-либо (часто случайно).
- В авиации: снять с рейса из-за овербукинга; отказать в посадке.
- Не путать с _bump into someone_ — случайно встретить кого-либо.
