# closet
?
# Transcription:
- /ˈklɒzɪt/ (BrE), /ˈklɑːzɪt/ (AmE)
# Meaning:
- A small cupboard or wardrobe, especially one used for storing clothes or household items.
- A small room or space for storing things.
# Usage:
- Common in American English for a wardrobe or built-in storage space. In British English, "wardrobe" is more common for clothes, and "cupboard" for kitchen or household storage.
- Can also be an adjective meaning secret or hidden, as in "closet alcoholic", but the noun is the main everyday meaning.
# Example:
- _She hung her coat in the closet._
- _The cleaning supplies are in the closet under the stairs._
# Russian:
- Шкаф, чулан, кладовка; встроенный шкаф.
- В американском английском часто означает шкаф для одежды. В британском для одежды обычно _wardrobe_, а для посуды и продуктов — _cupboard_.
