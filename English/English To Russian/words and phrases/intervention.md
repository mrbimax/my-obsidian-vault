# intervention
?
# Transcription:
- BrE /ˌɪntəˈvenʃn/; AmE /ˌɪntərˈvenʃn/
# Meaning:
- The act of becoming involved in a situation in order to change or improve it.
- Interference by a government, organization, or person in the affairs of another.
- A planned meeting or program in which people confront someone about a problem, especially addiction or mental health.
# Usage:
- Common patterns: intervention in something, government intervention, medical intervention, early intervention, crisis intervention.
- Neutral to formal. Used in politics, medicine, psychology, social work, business, and everyday contexts.
# Example:
- _Government intervention helped stabilize the economy._
- _Early intervention can prevent serious health problems._
- _His family organized an intervention to help him with his addiction._
# Russian:
- Вмешательство; интервенция.
- В медицине и психологии: интервенция, коррекционное вмешательство, кризисная помощь.
- _Intervention_ может означать как вмешательство государства или организации, так и специальную встречу с зависимым человеком.
