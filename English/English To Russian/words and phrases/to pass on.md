# to pass on
?
# Transcription:
- /tə pɑːs ɒn/ (BrE), /tə pæs ɑːn/ (AmE)
# Meaning:
- To give, send, or transmit something to another person, especially after receiving it or finishing with it.
- To die (a polite or euphemistic way to say someone has died).
- To decline an offer, invitation, or opportunity.
# Usage:
- For giving/transmitting: pass on a message, pass on information, pass on clothes/books.
- For death: "She passed on peacefully last night." More gentle than "died".
- For declining: "I'll pass on dessert, thanks." Often just "pass" or "pass on something".
# Example:
- _Could you pass on the message to your colleagues?_
- _He passed on peacefully at home surrounded by family._
- _I think I'll pass on the invitation this time._
# Russian:
- Передавать, передать (сообщение, информацию, вещь).
- Скончаться, уйти из жизни (эвфемизм).
- Отказаться, пропустить (предложение, приглашение).
- В зависимости от контекста: _pass on_ — передать дальше; _pass on_ — умереть; _pass on_ — вежливо отказаться.
