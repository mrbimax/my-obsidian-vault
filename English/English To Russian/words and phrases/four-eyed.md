# four-eyed
?
# Transcription:
- /ˌfɔːrˈaɪd/
# Meaning:
- (Informal, derogatory) Wearing glasses; used as an insult for someone who wears glasses.
- Literal: having four eyes, as a creature or in fantasy.
# Usage:
- A childish or offensive insult, often used to mock someone with glasses. Usually hyphenated before a noun or after a linking verb. The noun form is "four eyes".
# Example:
- _The bullies called him four-eyed and knocked his glasses off._
- _She was a four-eyed bookworm, but she didn't care what they said._
# Russian:
- Четырёхглазый (оскорбительное, о человеке в очках).
- Буквально: с четырьмя глазами. Русский аналог: «очкарик».
