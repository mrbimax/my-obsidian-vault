# padding
?
# Transcription:
- /ˈpædɪŋ/
# Meaning:
- Soft material used to fill, protect, or shape something, such as clothes, furniture, or packaging.
- Extra words or information added to make speech or writing longer, often unnecessarily.
- Extra data or space added in computing to align data or fill a block.
# Usage:
- Uncountable noun. Also the -ing form of "pad". Common phrases: padding in a jacket, padding in an essay, remove the padding, add padding.
# Example:
- _The jacket has extra padding in the shoulders._
- _His essay was full of padding and said very little._
- _The program adds padding to the data before sending it._
# Russian:
- Набивка, прокладка, наполнитель; мягкая подкладка.
- Словесный балласт, «вода» (в тексте); лишние слова.
- Заполнение, паддинг (в вычислениях).
