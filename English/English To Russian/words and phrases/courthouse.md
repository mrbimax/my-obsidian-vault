# courthouse
?
# Transcription:
- /ˈkɔːrthaʊs/ (AmE), /ˈkɔːthaʊs/ (BrE)
# Meaning:
- A building where legal cases are heard and trials take place.
- A public building that houses a court of law.
# Usage:
- Common in legal, news, and everyday contexts. Often used in phrases like at the courthouse, go to the courthouse, county courthouse.
- Neutral and formal. Can refer to a local, state, or federal building.
# Example:
- _The trial will be held at the federal courthouse._
- _We met outside the courthouse before the hearing._
# Russian:
- Здание суда; суд (как здание).
- В русском языке часто просто «суд» по контексту.
