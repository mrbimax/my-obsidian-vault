# relief
?
# Transcription:
- /rɪˈliːf/
# Meaning:
- A feeling of reassurance and relaxation after anxiety, pain, or distress has ended.
- The act of reducing or removing pain, worry, or difficulty; help or aid given to people in need.
# Usage:
- Common in phrases: relief from pain, a sigh of relief, to one's relief, disaster relief, tax relief.
- Neutral and common in everyday, medical, emotional, and humanitarian contexts.
# Example:
- _She felt a great sense of relief when the exam was over._
- _The charity provides relief to families affected by the flood._
# Russian:
- Облегчение; снятие (боли, напряжения); помощь, подмога.
- В словосочетаниях: _a sigh of relief_ — вздох облегчения; _disaster relief_ — помощь пострадавшим от стихийного бедствия; _tax relief_ — налоговая льгота.
