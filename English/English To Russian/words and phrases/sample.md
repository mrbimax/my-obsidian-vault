# sample
?
# Transcription:
- /ˈsɑːmpl/ (BrE), /ˈsæmpl/ (AmE)
# Meaning:
- A small part or quantity of something that shows what the whole is like.
- A specimen taken for testing or analysis.
- A small piece of recorded sound or music used in a new track.
- (Verb) To take a sample of something; to test or try a small amount.
# Usage:
- Noun: common in science, medicine, statistics, marketing, and music. Patterns: take/collect/test a sample, blood sample, random sample, free sample, use a sample.
- Verb: sample the food, sample a song, sample data.
# Example:
- _The doctor took a blood sample for testing._
- _We handed out free samples at the store._
- _The producer sampled an old jazz record._
- _She sampled the soup to check the seasoning._
