# foyer
?
# Transcription:
- /ˈfɔɪeɪ/ (BrE), /ˈfɔɪər/ (AmE)
# Meaning:
- An entrance hall or lobby in a building, especially a hotel, theater, or apartment block.
- A large open area just inside the main entrance, where people can gather or wait.
# Usage:
- Common in architecture, hospitality, and everyday descriptions of buildings. Often used for theaters, hotels, and large public buildings.
- Not usually used for a small private hallway in a house; "hallway" or "entryway" is more common there.
# Example:
- _We waited in the foyer before the play began._
- _The hotel foyer was decorated with marble and fresh flowers._
# Russian:
- Фойе, вестибюль, холл.
- Обычно о просторном входном помещении в театре, гостинице или общественном здании.
