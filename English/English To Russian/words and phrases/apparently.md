# apparently
?
# Transcription:
- /əˈpærəntli/
# Meaning:
- As far as one knows or can see; it seems that...
- According to what people say or what appears to be true.
# Usage:
- Common adverb, often used to report something heard or observed without confirming it. Can imply doubt or irony depending on tone.
# Example:
- _Apparently, the meeting has been cancelled._
- _She was apparently upset by the news._
# Russian:
- По-видимому, очевидно; как говорят, якобы.
- Может выражать как уверенное предположение, так и сомнение или иронию в зависимости от тона.
