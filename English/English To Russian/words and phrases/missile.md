# missile
?
# Transcription:
- BrE /ˈmɪsaɪl/; AmE /ˈmɪsl/ or /ˈmɪsaɪl/
# Meaning:
- A weapon that is self-propelled or guided, carrying explosives or a warhead and designed to hit a target at a distance.
- Any object thrown or propelled as a weapon, but in modern use usually a rocket or guided projectile.
# Usage:
- Common in military, political, and news contexts. Often used in phrases like launch a missile, missile strike, ballistic missile, cruise missile, missile defense.
- Neutral to formal. Usually refers to a guided weapon, not a simple thrown object.
# Example:
- _The country tested a new ballistic missile._
- _The missile was intercepted before it reached its target._
# Russian:
- Ракета; управляемый снаряд; метательный снаряд.
- В военном контексте обычно «ракета». Может означать любой метательный снаряд, но в современном английском чаще управляемая ракета.
