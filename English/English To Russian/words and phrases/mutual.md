# mutual
?
# Transcription:
- /ˈmjuːtʃuəl/
# Meaning:
- Experienced or done by each toward the other; reciprocal.
- Shared by two or more people or groups; common to both sides.
# Usage:
- Common before nouns: mutual respect, mutual trust, mutual friend, mutual agreement, mutual benefit.
- Neutral. Used in relationships, business, diplomacy, and everyday contexts. Not the same as "common" in all cases: mutual implies reciprocity between two sides.
# Example:
- _They have a mutual respect for each other._
- _We met through a mutual friend._
- _The agreement was based on mutual benefit._
# Russian:
- Взаимный, обоюдный; общий.
- _Mutual friend_ — общий знакомый; _mutual respect_ — взаимное уважение; _mutual agreement_ — обоюдное согласие. Подчёркивает, что чувство или действие направлено с обеих сторон.
