# distraught
?
# Transcription:
- /dɪˈstrɔːt/
# Meaning:
- Extremely upset, worried, or anxious, often to the point of not being able to think clearly.
- Deeply agitated by grief, fear, or distress.
# Usage:
- Common after be, feel, look, seem: she was distraught, he looked distraught.
- Often followed by over/about something, or by to + verb: too distraught to speak.
- Stronger and more emotional than simply "upset".
# Example:
- _She was distraught when her son didn't come home._
- _He looked distraught after the accident._
# Russian:
- Крайне расстроенный, убитый горем, вне себя от горя или тревоги.
- Не путать с _distracted_ — отвлечённый, невнимательный. _Distraught_ — очень сильное эмоциональное состояние.
