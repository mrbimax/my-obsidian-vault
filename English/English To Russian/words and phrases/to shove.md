# to shove
?
# Transcription:
- /tə ʃʌv/
# Meaning:
- To push someone or something roughly or forcefully.
- To put something somewhere quickly or carelessly.
# Usage:
- Common patterns: shove someone/something, shove something into/through something, shove someone aside.
- Forceful and often rude depending on context. Phrasal verb: shove off (rude: go away).
# Example:
- _He shoved me out of the way._
- _She shoved the keys into her pocket._
- _Don't shove — wait your turn._
# Russian:
- Толкать, пихать, засовывать.
- Может быть резким или грубым. _Shove off_ — грубо: отвали, убирайся.
