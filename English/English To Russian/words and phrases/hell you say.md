# hell you say
?
# Transcription:
- /hel juː seɪ/
# Meaning:
- (Informal, chiefly US) An exclamation of disbelief, surprise, or rejection of what someone has just said.
- Often means "I don't believe you" or "That's nonsense", sometimes with sarcasm or confrontation.
# Usage:
- Very informal. Common variant: "The hell you say!" Tone can be joking, shocked, or aggressive. Not used in formal speech.
# Example:
- _He says he's quitting? Hell you say._
- _The hell you say! I don't believe a word of it._
# Russian:
- Да ну?! / Врёшь! / Не может быть! / Чё ты говоришь?
- Буквально: «ад ты говоришь», но смысл — недоверие или резкое несогласие. Может звучать грубовато.
