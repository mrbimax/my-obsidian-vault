# to stick around
?
# Transcription:
- /tə stɪk əˈraʊnd/
# Meaning:
- To stay in a place or remain with someone for a period of time.
- To not leave, especially when waiting for something or someone.
# Usage:
- Informal and common in everyday speech. Often used in invitations or requests: Stick around, I'll be back soon. Also used to mean remain nearby.
# Example:
- _Stick around after the show, and I'll introduce you to the band._
- _I decided to stick around for a while and see what happened._
# Russian:
- Оставаться, не уходить, задержаться; быть поблизости.
- В разговорной речи может переводиться как «потусоваться здесь», «побыть рядом».
