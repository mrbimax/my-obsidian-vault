# slightest
?
# Transcription:
- /ˈslaɪtɪst/
# Meaning:
- Superlative of "slight": the smallest, least important, or least noticeable.
- Used especially in negative sentences to emphasize that there is none at all.
# Usage:
- Common in phrases like not the slightest idea, not the slightest doubt, not the slightest chance, without the slightest hesitation.
- Neutral and often emphatic. Usually appears with a negative or in questions.
# Example:
- _I don't have the slightest idea what you mean._
- _She didn't show the slightest interest in the offer._
# Russian:
- Малейший; ни малейший.
- В отрицаниях: ни малейшего (понятия, сомнения, шанса).
