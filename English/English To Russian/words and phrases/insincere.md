# insincere
?
# Transcription:
- BrE /ˌɪnsɪnˈsɪə(r)/; AmE /ˌɪnsɪnˈsɪr/
# Meaning:
- Not expressing genuine feelings or beliefs; pretending to be sincere.
- False or hypocritical in manner, words, or behavior.
# Usage:
- Common before nouns or after linking verbs: an insincere apology, an insincere smile, he seemed insincere.
- Neutral and common. Often describes compliments, apologies, promises, or people who say things they do not mean.
# Example:
- _Her apology sounded insincere, so no one believed her._
- _He gave an insincere smile and walked away._
- _It was an insincere compliment meant only to flatter._
# Russian:
- Неискренний, лицемерный, фальшивый.
- О словах, чувствах или поведении, которые не выражают настоящих намерений. Не путать с _sincere_ — искренний.
