# labor
?
# Transcription:
- /ˈleɪbər/ (AmE), /ˈleɪbə(r)/ (BrE)
# Meaning:
- (Noun) Work, especially physical work; effort or exertion.
- (Noun) Workers as a group, especially in relation to employers or politics.
- (Noun) The process of giving birth; childbirth.
- (Verb) To work hard; to make a great effort.
# Usage:
- American spelling is labor; British spelling is labour.
- Common in phrases like manual labor, labor force, labor union, labor market, go into labor, labor over something.
- Neutral to formal. Used in business, politics, medicine, and everyday contexts.
# Example:
- _The job requires a lot of physical labor._
- _The company is negotiating with labor._
- _She went into labor early this morning._
- _He labored for hours to finish the report._
