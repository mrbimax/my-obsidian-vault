# hinge
?
# Transcription:
- /hɪndʒ/
# Meaning:
- (Noun) A movable joint or piece of metal on which a door, gate, lid, or window swings.
- (Verb) To attach or join something with a hinge.
- (Verb) To depend entirely on something; usually in "hinge on/upon".
# Usage:
- Noun: common for doors, gates, lids, cabinets, and boxes.
- Verb for attaching: hinge a door, hinge the lid to the box.
- Verb for depending: the outcome hinges on the decision, everything hinges on this.
# Example:
- _The door squeaked on its rusty hinges._
- _He hinged the lid to the wooden box._
- _The whole plan hinges on whether she agrees._
# Russian:
- Петля (дверная, оконная); навешивать, прикреплять на петли; зависеть от, висеть на.
- _Hinge on/upon_ — зависеть от чего-либо, держаться на чём-либо.
