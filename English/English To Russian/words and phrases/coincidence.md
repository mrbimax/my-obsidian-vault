# coincidence
?
# Transcription:
- /kəʊˈɪnsɪdəns/ (BrE), /koʊˈɪnsɪdəns/ (AmE)
# Meaning:
- A situation in which two or more things happen at the same time or in a way that seems surprising or unlikely, but is not planned.
- The fact of two things being similar or happening together by chance.
# Usage:
- Common patterns: what a coincidence, by coincidence, a strange coincidence, pure coincidence, not a coincidence.
- Neutral and common in everyday speech and writing.
# Example:
- _What a coincidence — I was just thinking about you!_
- _By coincidence, we both booked the same flight._
# Russian:
- Совпадение; случайное совпадение.
- Обычно о неожиданном совпадении событий, обстоятельств или фактов, которое произошло случайно.
