# decoy
?
# Transcription:
- /ˈdiːkɔɪ/ (noun), /dɪˈkɔɪ/ (verb)
# Meaning:
- (Noun) A person, object, or animal used to lure or mislead someone or something into a trap.
- (Noun) A fake target or object used to distract attention from the real one.
- (Verb) To lure or entice someone or something into a trap or dangerous situation.
# Usage:
- Common in hunting, military, police, and security contexts. Often used as a noun: a decoy duck, a police decoy, a decoy operation.
- As a verb, less common but used in formal or descriptive writing: decoy someone into a trap.
# Example:
- _The hunters used a wooden decoy to attract ducks._
- _The police set up a decoy car to catch the thieves._
- _He was decoyed into a back alley and robbed._
# Russian:
- Приманка, подсадная утка; ложная цель; отвлекающий объект.
- Как глагол: заманивать, приманивать, выманивать в ловушку.
- Буквально: _decoy_ — то, что приманивает или отвлекает. В военном деле: ложная цель. В полиции: подставное лицо или приманка.
