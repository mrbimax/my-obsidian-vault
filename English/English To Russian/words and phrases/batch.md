# batch
?
# Transcription:
- /bætʃ/
# Meaning:
- A group of things or people of the same type, especially produced, processed, or handled together.
- An amount of something made at one time.
# Usage:
- Common patterns: a batch of cookies, a batch of emails, a batch of applicants, in batches, batch processing.
- Countable. Often used in cooking, manufacturing, computing, and administration.
# Example:
- _She baked a batch of cookies for the party._
- _The factory produced a batch of defective parts._
# Russian:
- Партия, порция, группа; пачка.
- В зависимости от контекста: _a batch of cookies_ — партия печенья; _a batch of emails_ — пачка или группа писем; _batch processing_ — пакетная обработка.
