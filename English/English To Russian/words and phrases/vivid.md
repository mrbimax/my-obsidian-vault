# vivid
?
# Transcription:
- /ˈvɪvɪd/
# Meaning:
- Producing powerful, clear images in the mind; very bright, intense, or lively.
- (Of a description, memory, etc.) Very clear and detailed.
# Usage:
- Common before nouns: vivid imagination, vivid memory, vivid dream, vivid colors, vivid description.
- Neutral. Used for colors, memories, dreams, descriptions, and mental images.
# Example:
- _She has a vivid imagination and writes wonderful stories._
- _I had a vivid dream about flying over the city._
- _The painting is full of vivid colors._
# Russian:
- Яркий, живой, отчётливый; образный.
- О цветах, воспоминаниях, снах, описаниях и воображении. _Vivid memory_ — яркое воспоминание; _vivid imagination_ — богатое воображение.
