# legitimate front outfits
?
# Transcription:
- /lɪˈdʒɪtɪmət frʌnt ˈaʊtfɪts/
# Meaning:
- Businesses or organizations that appear lawful but are actually used as a cover for illegal activity.
- "Front" means a cover or facade; "outfit" here means an organization, company, or group.
# Usage:
- Used mainly in crime, business, and financial contexts. Often describes shell companies, fake charities, or stores used to launder money or hide criminal operations.
# Example:
- _The gang used several legitimate front outfits to launder the money._
- _Police suspected the charity was one of several legitimate front outfits for smuggling._
# Russian:
- Легальные подставные фирмы; компании-прикрытия, выглядящие законными.
- Буквально: «законные организации-фасады». Смысл: внешне легальный бизнес, который служит прикрытием для незаконной деятельности.
