# settlement
?
# Transcription:
- /ˈsetlmənt/
# Meaning:
- An official agreement or decision that ends a disagreement or legal case.
- The payment or arrangement of money, property, or other matters, especially as part of such an agreement.
- A small community or place where people have settled, often in a new area.
# Usage:
- Legal/business: reach a settlement, out-of-court settlement, divorce settlement.
- Financial: settlement of a debt, settlement date, settlement payment.
- Community: a small settlement, a fishing settlement, a settlement on the coast.
- Neutral to formal. Common in law, finance, and geography/history.
# Example:
- _The two companies reached an out-of-court settlement._
- _She received a large settlement in the divorce._
- _They built a small settlement near the river._
# Russian:
- Урегулирование, соглашение; мировое соглашение.
- Расчёт, оплата, погашение (долга); поселение, посёлок.
- В юридическом контексте: _settlement_ — мировое соглашение или урегулирование спора. В финансовом: расчёт, выплата. В географическом: поселение.
