# the Pledge of Allegiance
?
# Transcription:
- /ðə pledʒ əv əˈliːdʒəns/
# Meaning:
- A formal oath of loyalty to the United States and its flag, usually recited standing with the right hand over the heart.
- The text begins: "I pledge allegiance to the flag of the United States of America..."
# Usage:
- Common in American schools, civic ceremonies, and patriotic events. Often recited daily in some schools, though rules vary by state and school district.
- Formal and patriotic. The correct spelling is "Allegiance", not "Allegiane".
# Example:
- _The students stood to recite the Pledge of Allegiance._
- _He learned the Pledge of Allegiance in elementary school._
# Russian:
- Клятва верности (флагу США).
- Официальная присяга верности США и американскому флагу, которую часто произносят в школах и на гражданских церемониях. Правильное написание: _Allegiance_.
