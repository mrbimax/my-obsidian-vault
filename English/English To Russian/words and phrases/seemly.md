# seemly
?
# Transcription:
- /ˈsiːmli/
# Meaning:
- Conforming to accepted standards of conduct or propriety; proper, decent, or suitable.
- Appropriate for the situation or social norms.
# Usage:
- Formal or literary. Often used in negative constructions: not seemly, hardly seemly. Describes behavior, speech, or appearance that fits social expectations.
# Example:
- _It would not be seemly to argue at a funeral._
- _She maintained a seemly distance from the scandal._
# Russian:
- Подобающий, приличный, благопристойный, уместный.
- Обычно о поведении, манерах или внешнем виде, соответствующих нормам приличия.
