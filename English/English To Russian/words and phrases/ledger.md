# ledger
?
# Transcription:
- /ˈledʒər/
# Meaning:
- A book or computer file in which a company or person records financial transactions.
- The principal accounting record where all debits and credits are entered.
# Usage:
- Common in accounting, bookkeeping, and finance. Can be physical or digital. Also used in "distributed ledger" for blockchain technology.
# Example:
- _The accountant entered every transaction into the ledger._
- _We switched from paper ledgers to accounting software last year._
# Russian:
- Бухгалтерская книга; гроссбух; регистр; учётная книга.
- В современном контексте также «реестр» (например, distributed ledger — распределённый реестр).
