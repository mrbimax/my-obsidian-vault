# ought
?
# Transcription:
- /ɔːt/
# Meaning:
- A modal verb used to say what is right, advisable, expected, or probable.
- It is almost always used as "ought to" + base verb.
# Usage:
- Correct pattern: ought to do something. Negative: ought not to / oughtn't to. Questions: Ought I to...? (formal).
- Do not say "to ought"; the particle "to" comes after "ought". Not used with do/does/did.
# Example:
- _You ought to apologize._
- _He ought to be home by now._
# Russian:
- Следует, должен, нужно; вероятно.
- _Ought to_ выражает долженствование, совет или вероятность.
