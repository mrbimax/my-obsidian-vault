# munitions
?
# Transcription:
- /mjuːˈnɪʃənz/
# Meaning:
- Military weapons, ammunition, equipment, and supplies used in war.
- Often plural; singular "munition" is rare and usually refers to a single item or material.
# Usage:
- Formal and military. Common in phrases like munitions factory, munitions depot, munitions industry.
- Typically plural; can be used attributively before another noun: munitions worker, munitions plant.
# Example:
- _The factory produced munitions for the army._
- _The rebels captured a munitions depot._
# Russian:
- Боеприпасы, вооружение и военное снаряжение; военные материалы.
- Обычно во множественном числе. _Munitions factory_ — завод по производству боеприпасов. Singular "munition" почти не употребляется в повседневной речи.
