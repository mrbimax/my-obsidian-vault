# domestic
?
# Transcription:
- /dəˈmestɪk/
# Meaning:
- Relating to the home, household, or family.
- Existing or occurring within a particular country; not foreign.
- (Of an animal) kept as a pet or on a farm; tame, not wild.
# Usage:
- Common in everyday, legal, political, and travel contexts. For flights, "domestic" means within the same country. For animals, contrasts with "wild".
# Example:
- _She handles all the domestic chores while he works._
- _The airline operates both domestic and international flights._
- _Dogs and cats are domestic animals._
# Russian:
- Домашний, бытовой; семейный.
- Внутренний (в пределах страны), не международный.
- Одомашненный (о животных), не дикий.
