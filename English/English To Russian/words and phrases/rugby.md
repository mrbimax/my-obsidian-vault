# rugby
?
# Transcription:
- /ˈrʌɡbi/
# Meaning:
- A team sport played with an oval ball, in which players can carry, pass, kick, and tackle.
- The game is known for physical contact and is popular in many countries, especially the UK, Ireland, France, Australia, New Zealand, and South Africa.
# Usage:
- Common as an uncountable noun: play rugby, a game of rugby, rugby match, rugby player, rugby team.
- Neutral and common in everyday speech. Not usually used with "a" before the sport itself.
# Example:
- _He plays rugby every Saturday._
- _We watched a rugby match at the stadium._
# Russian:
- Регби.
- Командный вид спорта с овальным мячом, где разрешены захваты и силовой контакт. Обычно неисчисляемое: _play rugby_ — играть в регби.
