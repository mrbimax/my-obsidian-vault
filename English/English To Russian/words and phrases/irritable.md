# irritable
?
# Transcription:
- /ˈɪrɪtəbl/
# Meaning:
- Easily annoyed, angered, or upset; having a tendency to become irritated quickly.
- (Medical) Reacting abnormally to a stimulus; often used in terms like irritable bowel syndrome.
# Usage:
- Common for describing a person's mood or temperament: an irritable boss, feeling irritable.
- Also used in medical contexts, especially "irritable bowel syndrome" (IBS).
# Example:
- _He gets irritable when he's tired._
- _The heat made everyone irritable by the afternoon._
# Russian:
- Раздражительный, вспыльчивый; легко раздражающийся.
- В медицине: раздражённый, с повышенной чувствительностью. Например, _irritable bowel syndrome_ — синдром раздражённого кишечника.
