# zeal
?
# Transcription:
- /ziːl/
# Meaning:
- Great energy and enthusiasm for a cause, goal, or activity.
- Intense eagerness or devotion, sometimes more than is reasonable.
# Usage:
- Formal or literary. Often used with "with zeal", "religious zeal", "reformist zeal", or "excessive zeal".
- Describes strong motivation, especially for a belief, mission, or cause.
# Example:
- _She pursued her research with great zeal._
- _His religious zeal sometimes made others uncomfortable._
# Russian:
- Рвение, усердие, энтузиазм, пыл.
- Может означать как сильную преданность делу, так и чрезмерное рвение. Часто в религиозном или идеологическом контексте.
