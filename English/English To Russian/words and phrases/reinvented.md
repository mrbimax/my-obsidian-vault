# reinvented
?
# Transcription:
- /ˌriːɪnˈventɪd/
# Meaning:
- Past tense and past participle of "reinvent".
- Created again in a new form; changed something radically or improved it by starting over.
# Usage:
- Common in phrases like reinvented himself/herself, reinvented the wheel, a reinvented brand.
- Neutral; used in business, personal development, technology, and creativity.
# Example:
- _She reinvented herself as a writer after leaving her corporate job._
- _The company reinvented its flagship product and sales soared._
# Russian:
- Переизобрёл, заново изобрёл; переосмысленный, обновлённый.
- _Reinvented himself_ — заново себя нашёл, кардинально изменился. _Reinvent the wheel_ — изобретать велосипед.
