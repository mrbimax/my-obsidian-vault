# collateral
?
# Transcription:
- /kəˈlætərəl/
# Meaning:
- (Noun) Property or money pledged as security for a loan, to be forfeited in case of default.
- (Adjective) Accompanying but secondary or subordinate; (of damage) unintended, incidental, especially civilian casualties in military operations.
# Usage:
- As a noun, common in finance and law: provide collateral, use something as collateral.
- As an adjective, often in "collateral damage" (military) or "collateral effect" (secondary result).
# Example:
- _The bank required collateral before approving the loan._
- _He used his house as collateral for the business loan._
- _The bombing caused significant collateral damage to nearby buildings._
