# to lend
?
# Transcription:
- /tə lend/
# Meaning:
- To give something to someone for a period of time, expecting it to be returned.
- To provide money temporarily, usually with the expectation of repayment.
# Usage:
- Common patterns: lend someone something, lend something to someone. Also used figuratively: lend support, lend a hand.
- Do not confuse with "borrow", which means to take and use something temporarily.
# Example:
- _Can you lend me your pen?_
- _The bank agreed to lend them money for the house._
# Russian:
- Давать взаймы, одалживать; ссужать.
- Не путать с _borrow_ — брать взаймы. _Lend_ — давать, _borrow_ — брать.
