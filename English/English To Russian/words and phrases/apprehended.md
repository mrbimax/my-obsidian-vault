# apprehended
?
# Transcription:
- /ˌæprɪˈhendɪd/
# Meaning:
- Past tense and past participle of "apprehend".
- Arrested or taken into custody by police or authorities.
- Understood or perceived something, especially an idea or meaning.
# Usage:
- Formal. The legal/police sense is common: the suspect was apprehended.
- The understanding sense is formal and less common in everyday speech: he apprehended the meaning.
# Example:
- _The police apprehended the suspect near the border._
- _She quickly apprehended the main idea of the text._
# Russian:
- Задержан, арестован; взят под стражу.
- Понял, осознал, воспринял (формально).
