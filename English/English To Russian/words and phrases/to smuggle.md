# to smuggle
?
# Transcription:
- /tə ˈsmʌɡl/
# Meaning:
- To take goods, people, or other items illegally into or out of a country, often secretly to avoid paying duties or because they are forbidden.
- To move something secretly and often illegally.
# Usage:
- Common patterns: smuggle something into/out of a place, smuggle goods/drugs/weapons, smuggle people across a border.
- Neutral to formal; common in news, legal, and crime contexts. The noun is "smuggler", and the activity is "smuggling".
# Example:
- _They tried to smuggle drugs through customs._
- _He smuggled the documents out of the country._
- _The gang smuggled people across the border._
