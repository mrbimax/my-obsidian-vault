# to rough someone up
?
# Transcription:
- /tə rʌf ˈsʌmwʌn ʌp/
# Meaning:
- To attack and physically manhandle or beat someone, usually to intimidate, punish, or threaten them.
- To use force or violence against someone, often without intending to kill.
# Usage:
- Informal and often associated with crime, gangs, police brutality, or intimidation. Usually transitive: rough someone up. Often passive: he was roughed up by thugs.
- Implies a beating or rough handling, not necessarily serious injury.
# Example:
- _The loan sharks sent two men to rough him up._
- _He was roughed up by security guards outside the club._
- _They threatened to rough her up if she talked to the police._
# Russian:
- Избить, отделать, помять, устроить физическую расправу.
- Обычно означает насилие для устрашения или наказания, не обязательно тяжкие телесные повреждения. _He was roughed up_ — его избили, с ним грубо обошлись.
