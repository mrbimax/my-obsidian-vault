# attorney-client privilege
?
# Transcription:
- /əˈtɜːrni ˈklaɪənt ˈprɪvəlɪdʒ/
# Meaning:
- The legal right that keeps confidential communications between a lawyer and their client private and protected from forced disclosure.
- It allows clients to speak honestly with their attorney without fear that the information will be revealed in court or to others.
# Usage:
- Formal legal term. Common in law, court cases, and legal advice. Often used in phrases like invoke attorney-client privilege, waive attorney-client privilege, protected by attorney-client privilege.
# Example:
- _The attorney refused to answer, citing attorney-client privilege._
- _Anything you tell your lawyer may be protected by attorney-client privilege._
# Russian:
- Адвокатская тайна; привилегия адвоката и клиента.
- Означает конфиденциальность общения между адвокатом и клиентом, которая защищена законом от раскрытия.
