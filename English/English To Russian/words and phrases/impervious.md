# impervious
?
# Transcription:
- BrE /ɪmˈpɜːviəs/; AmE /ɪmˈpɜːrviəs/
# Meaning:
- Not allowing water, air, or another substance to pass through; impenetrable.
- Not affected or influenced by something; unable to be harmed or moved by it.
# Usage:
- Common patterns: impervious to water, impervious to criticism, impervious to reason, impervious to change.
- Formal and technical. Used for materials, surfaces, and also for people who seem unaffected by emotions, arguments, or pressure.
# Example:
- _The jacket is impervious to rain._
- _She seemed impervious to criticism._
- _He was impervious to all their arguments._
# Russian:
- Непроницаемый, водонепроницаемый; невосприимчивый, не поддающийся.
- _Impervious to_ — не пропускающий что-либо; не поддающийся чему-либо. Может описывать материал или человека, на которого не действуют критика, уговоры, эмоции.
