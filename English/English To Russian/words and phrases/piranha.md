# piranha
?
# Transcription:
- /pɪˈrɑːnə/ (BrE), /pɪˈrænə/ (AmE)
# Meaning:
- A small freshwater fish from South America with very sharp teeth, known for eating meat and sometimes attacking in groups.
- Figuratively, a person who attacks others fiercely or greedily, especially in business or politics.
# Usage:
- Literal sense is common in nature, travel, and animal contexts: a school of piranhas, piranha-infested waters.
- Figurative sense is informal and critical: the press were like piranhas, corporate piranhas.
# Example:
- _A school of piranhas stripped the carcass in minutes._
- _The journalists descended on him like piranhas._
# Russian:
- Пиранья.
- В переносном смысле: хищник, акула (о человеке), тот, кто яростно атакует или обдирает других.
