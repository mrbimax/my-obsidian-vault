# pierced
?
# Transcription:
- BrE /pɪəst/; AmE /pɪrst/
# Meaning:
- Past tense and past participle of "pierce": made a hole through something with a sharp object.
- Having a hole made in the body for wearing jewelry, especially ears, nose, or other body parts.
- Penetrated sharply, as by sound, light, cold, or emotion.
# Usage:
- Common for body modification: pierced ears, pierced nose, get something pierced.
- Also used literally or figuratively: pierced by an arrow, pierced the silence, pierced my heart.
- Neutral. As an adjective, it often describes ears or body parts with jewelry.
# Example:
- _She got her ears pierced last week._
- _The arrow pierced his armour._
- _A loud scream pierced the silence._
# Russian:
- Проколотый; пронзённый, проткнутый.
