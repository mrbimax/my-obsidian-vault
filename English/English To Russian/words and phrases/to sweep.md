# to sweep
?
# Transcription:
- /tə swiːp/
# Meaning:
- To clean a floor or other surface by brushing away dirt with a broom or brush.
- To move quickly, powerfully, or smoothly over an area.
- To win all or most of the games, prizes, or votes in a competition or election.
- To search or examine a place or area thoroughly.
# Usage:
- Common patterns: sweep the floor, sweep up the mess, sweep through the city, sweep the board, sweep for bugs.
- Neutral and common in everyday, sports, political, military, and cleaning contexts.
# Example:
- _She swept the kitchen floor after dinner._
- _A storm swept across the coast last night._
- _The team swept the series in three games._
- _Police swept the building for explosives._
