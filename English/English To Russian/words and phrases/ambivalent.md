# ambivalent
?
# Transcription:
- /æmˈbɪvələnt/
# Meaning:
- Having mixed or contradictory feelings about someone or something.
- Unable to decide because you have both positive and negative reactions.
# Usage:
- Common patterns: ambivalent about something, ambivalent toward someone, feel ambivalent.
- Neutral and fairly formal. Not the same as indifferent: you do have feelings, but they conflict.
# Example:
- _She felt ambivalent about moving abroad — excited but also afraid._
- _He is ambivalent toward his old job: he misses the people but not the stress._
# Russian:
- Амбивалентный, двойственный; испытывающий противоречивые чувства.
- Означает одновременное притяжение и отторжение, а не равнодушие.
