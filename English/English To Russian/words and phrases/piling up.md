# piling up
?
# Transcription:
- /ˈpaɪlɪŋ ʌp/
# Meaning:
- Accumulating in a pile; increasing in quantity or amount.
- Growing steadily, especially when things are not dealt with.
- Present participle of "pile up": to form a pile, or to increase and accumulate.
# Usage:
- Common with work, bills, debts, laundry, emails, problems, and tasks.
- Neutral and common in everyday speech. Often suggests that things are becoming difficult to manage.
# Example:
- _The work is piling up while I'm on holiday._
- _Bills kept piling up, so he had to ask for help._
# Russian:
- Накапливаться, скапливаться, нагромождаться; расти как снежный ком.
- Обычно о делах, счетах, долгах, проблемах или вещах, которых становится всё больше.
