# to brush
?
# Transcription:
- /tə brʌʃ/
# Meaning:
- To clean, smooth, or groom something with a brush.
- To touch lightly against something while moving past it.
- To apply a substance with a brush.
# Usage:
- Common patterns: brush your teeth/hair, brush something off/away, brush against someone/something, brush something on/onto something.
- Also used in phrasal verbs: brush up on (improve knowledge), brush aside (dismiss), brush off (ignore).
# Example:
- _She brushed her hair before going out._
- _He brushed the dirt off his jacket._
- _Her arm brushed against mine in the crowd._
- _Brush the paint on evenly._
# Russian:
- Чистить щёткой, причёсывать; смахивать, счищать.
- Легко касаться, задевать при движении.
- Наносить кистью. _Brush up on_ — подтянуть знания; _brush off_ — отмахнуться, проигнорировать.
