# a lineup
?
# Transcription:
- /ə ˈlaɪnʌp/
# Meaning:
- A group of people or things arranged in a line or assembled for a purpose.
- The set of people or items taking part in an event, competition, or show.
- In sports, the list of players on a team for a particular game.
- In police work, a group of people shown to a witness so they can identify a suspect.
# Usage:
- Common in sports, entertainment, police, and general contexts. Often used in phrases like a strong lineup, the starting lineup, a police lineup, a lineup of speakers.
- Usually countable. British English often writes it with a hyphen: line-up.
# Example:
- _The festival has a great lineup of bands this year._
- _The coach announced the starting lineup for tomorrow's match._
- _The witness picked him out of a police lineup._
# Russian:
- Состав, список участников; ряд, очередь; опознание в полиции.
- В спорте: стартовый состав. В музыке или мероприятии: список выступающих. В полиции: предъявление для опознания. Британское написание: _line-up_.
