# to take quite a shine
?
# Transcription:
- /tə teɪk kwaɪt ə ʃaɪn/
# Meaning:
- To develop a strong liking for someone or something, often quickly and noticeably.
- Usually used as "take a shine to someone/something"; "quite a shine" means a clear or strong liking.
# Usage:
- Informal and friendly. Common in British and American English. Usually followed by "to": take quite a shine to someone.
# Example:
- _She's taken quite a shine to the new neighbor._
- _He took quite a shine to that old guitar the moment he saw it._
# Russian:
