# to examine
?
# Transcription:
- /tə ɪɡˈzæmɪn/
# Meaning:
- To look at something carefully and in detail in order to learn about it or find something.
- To test someone's knowledge or skill, especially in an exam.
- To inspect or investigate something officially.
# Usage:
- Common patterns: examine something, examine someone, examine a patient, examine the evidence, examine for something.
- Neutral and common in education, medicine, science, law, and everyday contexts.
# Example:
- _The doctor examined the patient carefully._
- _The committee will examine the evidence._
- _Students are examined at the end of the term._
# Russian:
- Осматривать, обследовать, изучать, рассматривать; проверять знания, экзаменовать.
- Может означать «внимательно рассматривать», «медицински обследовать», «исследовать» или «экзаменовать».
