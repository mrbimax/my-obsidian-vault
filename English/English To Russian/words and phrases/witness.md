# witness
?
# Transcription:
- /ˈwɪtnəs/
# Meaning:
- (Noun) A person who sees an event, especially a crime or accident, take place.
- (Noun) A person who gives evidence in a court of law.
- (Verb) To see something happen, especially an accident or crime.
- (Verb) To be present at an event and see it.
# Usage:
- Common as both noun and verb. Legal contexts: witness for the prosecution, witness stand, call someone as a witness.
- General: witness an accident, witness a historic moment, bear witness to something.
# Example:
- _She was the only witness to the robbery._
- _He witnessed the car crash from his window._
- _The court heard from several witnesses._
# Russian:
- Свидетель; свидетельница.
- Быть свидетелем, видеть, наблюдать; давать показания.
- _Witness_ может быть существительным и глаголом. В юридическом контексте: _witness for the prosecution_ — свидетель обвинения; _witness stand_ — место для дачи показаний.
