# to decide
?
# Transcription:
- /tə dɪˈsaɪd/
# Meaning:
- To make a choice or judgment about something, especially after thinking about it.
- To resolve or settle a question, dispute, or matter.
# Usage:
- Common patterns: decide to do something, decide on/upon something, decide between two things, decide that...
- Neutral and very common in everyday, formal, and academic contexts.
# Example:
- _She decided to study abroad next year._
- _We need to decide on a date for the meeting._
- _He decided against buying the car._
# Russian:
- Решать, принимать решение; решить.
- _Decide to do something_ — решить сделать что-либо; _decide on something_ — остановиться на чём-либо, выбрать; _decide against_ — решить не делать.
