# goes with everything
?
# Transcription:
- /ɡoʊz wɪð ˈɛvriθɪŋ/ (AmE), /ɡəʊz wɪð ˈɛvriθɪŋ/ (BrE)
# Meaning:
- Matches or complements anything; looks good with any other item, especially clothes or colors.
- Is suitable or compatible with any situation, person, or thing.
# Usage:
- Common about clothing, accessories, colors, and design: black goes with everything.
- Also used more generally about something flexible or universally suitable.
- Neutral and common in everyday speech.
# Example:
- _A white shirt goes with everything._
- _She loves that bag because it goes with everything she owns._
# Russian:
- Подходит ко всему; сочетается со всем; идёт ко всему.
- Буквально: «идёт со всем». Обычно о вещах, цветах или одежде, которые легко сочетаются с чем угодно.
