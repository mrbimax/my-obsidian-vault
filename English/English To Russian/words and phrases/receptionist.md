# receptionist
?
# Transcription:
- /rɪˈsepʃənɪst/
# Meaning:
- A person employed to greet visitors, answer phone calls, and handle basic administrative tasks in an office, hotel, clinic, or other organization.
- The first person a visitor or guest usually speaks to at a reception desk.
# Usage:
- Common in business, hospitality, healthcare, and office contexts. Often used with "hotel receptionist", "medical receptionist", "front desk receptionist".
- Neutral and common in everyday speech.
# Example:
- _The receptionist greeted us and asked us to wait._
- _She works as a receptionist at a dental clinic._
- _Call the receptionist to book an appointment._
# Russian:
- Администратор; секретарь на приёме; регистратор.
- В отеле — портье или администратор стойки; в поликлинике — регистратор; в офисе — секретарь на ресепшене.
