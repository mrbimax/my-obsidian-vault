# jaywalking
?
# Transcription:
- /ˈdʒeɪwɔːkɪŋ/ (BrE), /ˈdʒeɪwɑːkɪŋ/ (AmE)
# Meaning:
- The act of crossing a street illegally or carelessly, especially not at a designated crossing or against traffic signals.
- A minor traffic offense in many places, though it can be dangerous.
# Usage:
- Neutral, common in legal, urban, and traffic contexts. Often used with get a ticket for, be fined for, crack down on.
# Example:
- _He got a ticket for jaywalking._
- _Jaywalking is dangerous and illegal in many cities._
# Russian:
- Переход дороги в неположенном месте; нарушение правил перехода.
- От глагола _jaywalk_ — переходить улицу в неположенном месте или на красный свет.
