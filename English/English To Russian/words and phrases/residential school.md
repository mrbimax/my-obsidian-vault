# residential school
?
# Transcription:
- /ˌrezɪˈdenʃl skuːl/
# Meaning:
- A school where students live during the school term; a boarding school.
- In Canada, a government-funded school where Indigenous children were forcibly placed, often run by churches, as part of assimilation policies.
# Usage:
- General sense: common in British and Commonwealth English for boarding schools or special residential institutions.
- Canadian sense: often refers to the Indian residential school system; a sensitive historical topic involving forced assimilation, abuse, and cultural loss.
# Example:
- _He was sent to a residential school in Ontario._
- _The report documented abuses at residential schools._
- _Many British children attend residential schools from an early age._
# Russian:
