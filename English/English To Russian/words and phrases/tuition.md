# tuition
?
# Transcription:
- BrE /tjuˈɪʃn/; AmE /tuˈɪʃn/
# Meaning:
- The money paid for being taught, especially at a college or university.
- Teaching or instruction, especially for a small group or individual; the act of teaching.
# Usage:
- In American English, usually means the fee for education: pay tuition, tuition fees, tuition costs.
- In British English, it can also mean teaching itself, especially private lessons: give tuition, receive tuition, private tuition.
- Common in education, university, finance, and school contexts.
# Example:
- _University tuition has risen sharply in recent years._
- _She pays for private tuition in French._
- _He receives extra tuition in maths._
# Russian:
- Плата за обучение; стоимость обучения (особенно в вузе).
- Обучение, занятия, репетиторство (брит.).
- В американском английском обычно _tuition_ — плата за обучение; в британском может означать само преподавание или частные уроки. _Tuition fees_ — плата за обучение.
