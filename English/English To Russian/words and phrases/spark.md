# spark
?
# Transcription:
- /spɑːrk/
# Meaning:
- A small flash of light, fire, or electricity.
- A small thing that causes something bigger, such as interest, conflict, or change.
- (Verb) To produce sparks; to cause something to start or develop.
# Usage:
- Common as noun and verb. Literal for fire/electricity: a spark from the fire, spark plug. Figurative: spark interest, spark a debate, spark a romance.
# Example:
- _A spark from the campfire landed on the dry grass._
- _The teacher's enthusiasm sparked my interest in science._
- _The argument sparked a wider debate._
# Russian:
- Искра; вспышка; искрить; вызывать, разжигать, пробуждать.
- В прямом смысле: искра, разряд. В переносном: искра интереса, чувства; дать начало, спровоцировать. _Spark plug_ — свеча зажигания.
