# shattered
?
# Transcription:
- /ˈʃætəd/ (BrE), /ˈʃætərd/ (AmE)
# Meaning:
- Broken into many small pieces.
- (Informal, BrE) Extremely tired; exhausted.
- Emotionally devastated or completely destroyed.
# Usage:
- Literal sense is common for glass, windows, or hard objects.
- Informal British sense means very tired: I'm absolutely shattered.
- Figurative sense is used for hopes, confidence, or a person's emotional state.
# Example:
- _The vase lay shattered on the floor._
- _I've been working all day and I'm absolutely shattered._
- _He was shattered by the news of her death._
