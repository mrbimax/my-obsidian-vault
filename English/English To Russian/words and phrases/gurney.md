# gurney
?
# Transcription:
- /ˈɡɜːrni/ (AmE), /ˈɡɜːni/ (BrE)
# Meaning:
- A wheeled stretcher used for moving patients in a hospital.
- A flat, wheeled bed or trolley for transporting sick or injured people.
# Usage:
- Common in American English and medical contexts. In British English, "trolley" is often used instead. Neutral and standard.
# Example:
- _The paramedics rushed him into the emergency room on a gurney._
- _She was wheeled down the corridor on a gurney._
# Russian:
- Каталка; медицинская кушетка на колёсах; носилки-каталка.
- В американском английском — _gurney_; в британском чаще _trolley_. Используется для перевозки пациентов в больнице.
