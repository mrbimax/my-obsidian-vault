# fuse wire
?
# Transcription:
- /fjuːz ˈwaɪər/
# Meaning:
- A thin wire made of metal or alloy that melts at a low temperature, used in a fuse to break an electrical circuit when the current becomes too high.
- The replaceable wire element inside certain types of electrical fuses.
# Usage:
- Technical and practical. Common in electrical work, old fuse boxes, appliances, and engineering. Often used with verbs like replace, fit, blow, melt.
- Not the same as "fuse" itself: "fuse" is the safety device, "fuse wire" is the wire inside or used with it.
# Example:
- _The fuse wire melted and cut off the power before the wiring could overheat._
- _He replaced the fuse wire in the old fuse box._
# Russian:
- Плавкая проволока; проволока для предохранителя; плавкая вставка.
- Тонкая проволока, которая плавится и разрывает электрическую цепь при перегрузке. Не путать с _fuse_ — предохранитель.
