# outward
?
# Transcription:
- /ˈaʊtwərd/ (AmE), /ˈaʊtwəd/ (BrE)
# Meaning:
- Directed or moving away from the centre or from the inside; external.
- Relating to the outside or to what is visible or apparent.
- (Adverb, especially AmE) Away from the centre or inside; outwards.
# Usage:
- As an adjective: outward appearance, outward journey, outward signs.
- As an adverb: the door opens outward (AmE); British English often uses outwards.
- Neutral; common in everyday, descriptive, and formal contexts.
# Example:
- _She kept a calm outward appearance despite the stress._
- _The door opens outward, so be careful._
# Russian:
- Внешний, наружный; направленный наружу.
- Как наречие: наружу, вовне. В британском варианте наречие чаще _outwards_.
