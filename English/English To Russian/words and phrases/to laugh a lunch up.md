# to laugh a lunch up
?
# Transcription:
- /tə lɑːf ə lʌntʃ ʌp/
# Meaning:
- (Informal, humorous) To laugh so hard that one vomits or nearly vomits; to bring up one's lunch from laughing.
- Nonstandard word order; the usual form is "laugh up one's lunch" or "laugh one's lunch up".
# Usage:
- Very informal and jokey. Used to exaggerate how hard someone laughed. Not for formal writing.
# Example:
- _I laughed so hard I almost laughed my lunch up._
- _He was laughing a lunch up after that joke._
# Russian:
- Смеяться до тошноты; ржать так, что чуть не вывернуло наизнанку.
- Буквально: «высмеять обед наружу». Обычная форма: _laugh up one's lunch_ — «смеяться так, что тошнит».
