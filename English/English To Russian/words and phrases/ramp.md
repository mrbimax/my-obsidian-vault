# ramp
?
# Transcription:
- /ræmp/
# Meaning:
- A sloping surface or structure that connects two different levels, used for walking, driving, or moving things.
- A road or highway section that slopes and connects different roads, especially an entrance or exit.
# Usage:
- Common for wheelchair ramps, car ramps, skateboard ramps, and highway ramps.
- Can be a physical structure or part of a road. Also used figuratively in phrases like "ramp up" (increase), but "ramp" alone is usually the noun.
# Example:
- _The building has a ramp for wheelchair access._
- _Take the exit ramp and then turn left._
# Russian:
- Наклонная плоскость, пандус, рампа; съезд, въезд.
- В зависимости от контекста: _wheelchair ramp_ — пандус для инвалидной коляски; _exit ramp_ — съезд с шоссе; _skateboard ramp_ — скейт-рампа.
