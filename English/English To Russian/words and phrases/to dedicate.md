# to dedicate
?
# Transcription:
- /tə ˈdedɪkeɪt/
# Meaning:
- To give time, effort, or resources to a particular purpose, person, or cause.
- To devote oneself to something seriously and wholeheartedly.
- To inscribe or address a book, song, performance, etc. to someone as a tribute or in honor.
# Usage:
- Common patterns: dedicate something to something/someone, dedicate oneself to something, dedicate time/energy to something.
- Neutral and common in everyday, formal, creative, and personal contexts. Often implies commitment and seriousness.
# Example:
- _She dedicated her life to helping refugees._
- _He dedicated the song to his late father._
- _You need to dedicate more time to your studies._
# Russian:
- Посвящать, отдавать, уделять; посвятить (книгу, песню и т. п.).
- _Dedicate oneself to_ — посвятить себя чему-либо; _dedicate something to someone_ — посвятить что-либо кому-либо. Подразумевает серьёзную преданность делу или человеку.
