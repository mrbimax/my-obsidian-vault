# to lease
?
# Transcription:
- /tə liːs/
# Meaning:
- To grant the use of property, land, equipment, or a vehicle to someone for a period of time in return for payment.
- To take or hold property, land, equipment, or a vehicle under a lease; to rent something on a long-term basis.
# Usage:
- Common patterns: lease something to someone, lease something from someone, lease a car, lease an apartment, lease out a property.
- Formal and common in business, real estate, law, and finance. Often implies a written contract for a longer period than ordinary renting.
# Example:
- _The company leased office space in the city centre._
- _They leased the land to a local farmer._
- _She leases her car instead of buying one._
# Russian:
- Сдавать в аренду; брать в аренду; арендовать.
- _Lease something to someone_ — сдавать что-либо кому-либо в аренду. _Lease something from someone_ — арендовать что-либо у кого-либо. Обычно о долгосрочной аренде по договору.
