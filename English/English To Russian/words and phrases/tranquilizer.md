# tranquilizer
?
# Transcription:
- /ˈtræŋkwɪlaɪzər/ (AmE), /ˈtræŋkwɪlaɪzə(r)/ (BrE)
# Meaning:
- A drug used to reduce anxiety, tension, or agitation, and sometimes to induce calm or sleep.
- A substance that calms a person or animal; often used in medicine, veterinary practice, and sometimes informally for sedatives.
# Usage:
- Medical and formal, but also common in everyday speech. Common patterns: take a tranquilizer, prescribe a tranquilizer, a mild/strong tranquilizer.
- American spelling: tranquilizer. British spelling: tranquilliser or tranquillizer.
# Example:
- _The doctor prescribed a mild tranquilizer to help her sleep._
- _The vet used a tranquilizer to calm the frightened animal._
