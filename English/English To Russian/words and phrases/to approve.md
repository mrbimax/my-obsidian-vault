# to approve
?
# Transcription:
- /tə əˈpruːv/
# Meaning:
- To officially agree to or accept something, such as a plan, request, or law.
- To have a positive opinion of someone or something; to consider it good or acceptable.
# Usage:
- Common patterns: approve of someone/something, approve a plan/request/budget, approve a drug/treatment.
- Neutral to formal. Used in everyday, business, legal, and political contexts.
# Example:
- _The committee approved the new budget._
- _Her parents don't approve of her boyfriend._
- _The drug was approved for use in children._
# Russian:
- Одобрять, утверждать, соглашаться; санкционировать.
- _Approve of_ — одобрять, хорошо относиться. _Approve a plan_ — утвердить план. Не путать с _approve_ без _of_: официально утвердить, разрешить.
