# to slip
?
# Transcription:
- /tə slɪp/
# Meaning:
- To slide accidentally and lose one's balance or footing.
- To move quietly, quickly, or unnoticed.
- To make a small mistake or error.
# Usage:
- Common patterns: slip on something, slip out/in, slip up (make a mistake), slip one's mind (forget).
# Example:
- _She slipped on the wet floor and fell._
- _He slipped out of the room without anyone noticing._
- _I'm sorry, I slipped up and gave you the wrong date._
# Russian:
- Поскользнуться, оступиться.
- Проскользнуть, незаметно проскользнуть.
- Ошибиться, допустить оплошность; _slip up_ — оплошать.
