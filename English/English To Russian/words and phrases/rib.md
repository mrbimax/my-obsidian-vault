# rib
?
# Transcription:
- /rɪb/
# Meaning:
- (Noun) One of the curved bones in the chest that protect the heart and lungs.
- (Noun) A cut of meat containing a rib, especially from a cow or pig.
- (Verb, informal) To tease someone in a friendly or playful way.
# Usage:
- As a noun, common in anatomy, medicine, cooking, and butchery: broken rib, spare ribs, rib cage.
- As a verb, informal and usually friendly: rib someone about something.
# Example:
- _He broke a rib playing rugby._
- _We ordered a rack of ribs for dinner._
- _His friends ribbed him about his new haircut._
# Russian:
- Ребро; рёбрышко (кусок мяса); подшучивать, подкалывать.
- Как существительное: ребро, рёбра (мясо). Как глагол: добродушно подтрунивать, подкалывать.
