# to rescue
?
# Transcription:
- /tə ˈreskjuː/
# Meaning:
- To save someone or something from danger, harm, or a difficult situation.
- To free someone from a bad situation or distress.
# Usage:
- Common patterns: rescue someone/something from something, come to someone's rescue, rescue operation, rescue team.
- Neutral and common in everyday, emergency, military, and animal contexts.
# Example:
- _The firefighters rescued the family from the burning house._
- _She was rescued from the sinking ship._
- _He came to my rescue when I lost my wallet._
# Russian:
- Спасать, выручать, вызволять.
- _Rescue someone from something_ — спасти кого-либо от чего-либо. _Come to someone's rescue_ — прийти кому-либо на помощь.
