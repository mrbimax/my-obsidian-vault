# possession
?
# Transcription:
- /pəˈzeʃn/
# Meaning:
- The act or state of owning, having, or controlling something.
- Something that is owned; (plural) belongings or property.
- In law, actual control or occupancy of property or goods.
- (Formal) The state of being controlled by an emotion, spirit, or idea.
# Usage:
- Common patterns: in possession of, take possession of, come into possession of, unlawful possession, worldly possessions.
- Neutral to formal. In legal contexts, possession of drugs or weapons is a common phrase.
# Example:
- _She lost all her possessions in the fire._
- _He was arrested for possession of stolen goods._
- _The team had possession of the ball for most of the match._
# Russian:
- Владение, обладание; собственность, имущество (часто мн. ч.).
- В праве: владение, фактическое обладание.
- В переносном смысле: одержимость (например, демоническая). _In possession of_ — владеющий чем-либо; _take possession of_ — вступить во владение.
