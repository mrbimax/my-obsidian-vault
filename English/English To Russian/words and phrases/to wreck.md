# to wreck
?
# Transcription:
- /tə rek/
# Meaning:
- To destroy or badly damage something, especially a vehicle, building, or object.
- To completely ruin something such as a plan, relationship, or opportunity.
- To upset or disturb someone deeply; to break someone emotionally.
# Usage:
- Common patterns: wreck a car, wreck a plan, wreck someone's life, wreck one's health, wreck a relationship.
- Can be literal (physical damage) or figurative (ruin, destroy). Informal to neutral.
# Example:
- _He wrecked his car in the accident._
- _The scandal wrecked her career._
- _The divorce left him emotionally wrecked._
# Russian:
- Разбить, разрушить, сломать; испортить, загубить.
- Может означать физическое разрушение (машины, здания) или полное крушение планов, отношений, жизни. _Wrecked_ — разбитый, разрушенный; опустошённый.
