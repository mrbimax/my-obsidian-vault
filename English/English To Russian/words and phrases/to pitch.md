# to pitch
?
# Transcription:
- /tə pɪtʃ/
# Meaning:
- To throw something with force.
- To set up a tent or camp.
- To fall or move heavily forward.
- To make a persuasive sales talk or presentation.
- In baseball: to throw the ball to the batter.
# Usage:
- Throw: pitch a ball, pitch something to someone.
- Camp: pitch a tent, pitch camp.
- Fall: pitched forward, pitched into the water.
- Business: pitch an idea, pitch to investors.
- Baseball: pitch a game, pitch to a batter.
# Example:
- _He pitched the ball to the batter._
- _We pitched our tent by the lake._
- _She pitched forward and hit her head._
- _He pitched his startup to a group of investors._
# Russian:
- Бросать, кидать; подавать (в бейсболе).
- Ставить (палатку), разбивать (лагерь).
- Падать, валиться (вперёд).
- Продвигать, рекламировать (идею, товар); выступать с презентацией.
- Многозначный глагол; выбор значения зависит от контекста.
