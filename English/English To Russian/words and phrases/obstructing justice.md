# obstructing justice
?
# Transcription:
- /əbˈstrʌktɪŋ ˈdʒʌstɪs/
# Meaning:
- The crime of intentionally interfering with the administration of justice.
- Includes lying to investigators, hiding or destroying evidence, intimidating witnesses, or helping a suspect avoid arrest.
# Usage:
- Formal legal term. Often used as "obstruction of justice" (noun) or "obstructing justice" (verb phrase). Common in law, politics, and crime reporting.
# Example:
- _He was charged with obstructing justice after lying to the FBI._
- _Destroying those documents is obstruction of justice._
# Russian:
- Воспрепятствование правосудию; противодействие правосудию.
- Юридический термин: умышленное вмешательство в работу следствия или суда.
