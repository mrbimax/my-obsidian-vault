# representative
?
# Transcription:
- /ˌreprɪˈzentətɪv/
# Meaning:
- A person chosen or elected to act or speak on behalf of others.
- Typical of a class, group, or category; serving as an example.
# Usage:
- As a noun, common in business, politics, and sales: a sales representative, a union representative.
- As an adjective, often followed by "of": representative of a group, representative sample.
# Example:
- _She was elected as the students' representative._
- _This sample is representative of the whole batch._
# Russian:
- Представитель (сущ.); представительный, типичный, характерный (прил.).
- В бизнесе: торговый представитель, представитель компании.
