# spare
?
# Transcription:
- /speər/ (BrE), /sper/ (AmE)
# Meaning:
- (Adjective) Kept in reserve as an extra; not currently used but available if needed.
- (Adjective) Free or not taken, especially of time: spare time, a spare moment.
- (Verb) To give something, especially if it is inconvenient or generous to do so.
- (Verb) To save someone from something unpleasant, or to refrain from harming them.
- (Noun) An extra part, especially for a machine or vehicle.
# Usage:
- Adjective: spare key, spare tire, spare room, spare time.
- Verb: spare a few minutes, spare some change, spare someone's life, spare someone the details.
- Noun: a spare for the car; usually in "spare part" or "spare tire".
# Example:
- _We keep a spare key under the mat._
- _I read a lot in my spare time._
- _Could you spare a few minutes to help me?_
- _The soldier spared the prisoner's life._
- _The car has a spare in the trunk._
# Russian:
- Запасной, свободный, лишний; уделить, выделить; пощадить, избавить; запасная часть.
- В зависимости от контекста: _spare time_ — свободное время; _spare key_ — запасной ключ; _spare someone_ — пощадить или избавить кого-либо. _Spare_ может быть прилагательным, глаголом и существительным.
