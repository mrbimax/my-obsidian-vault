# fastidiousness
?
# Transcription:
- /fæˈstɪdiəsnəs/
# Meaning:
- The quality of being very attentive to detail, accuracy, or cleanliness, often to an excessive degree.
- The state of being hard to please or very difficult to satisfy.
# Usage:
- Formal. Used to describe a person's character or behavior, especially when they are very particular about small details or standards.
- Can be positive (meticulous, careful) or negative (picky, overly demanding), depending on context.
# Example:
- _Her fastidiousness about grammar makes her an excellent editor._
- _His fastidiousness about cleanliness can make him difficult to live with._
# Russian:
- Привередливость, щепетильность, педантичность; чрезмерная требовательность.
- Может означать как тщательность и аккуратность, так и излишнюю придирчивость.
