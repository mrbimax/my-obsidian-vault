# that's the sound of all your trouble going up in smoke
?
# Transcription:
- /ðæts ðə saʊnd əv ɔːl jɔː ˈtrʌbl ˈɡəʊɪŋ ʌp ɪn sməʊk/
# Meaning:
- Literally: that is the sound of all your problems being destroyed by fire or disappearing.
- "Go up in smoke" is an idiom meaning to be ruined, wasted, or vanish completely.
# Usage:
- Dramatic, informal, often humorous. Said when something burns or is destroyed, implying that the listener's troubles are gone.
- Can be used literally about smoke and fire, or figuratively about problems disappearing.
# Example:
- _As the papers burned, she smiled: "That's the sound of all your trouble going up in smoke."_
- _He watched the old shed burn and said, "That's the sound of all your trouble going up in smoke."_
# Russian:
- Это звук того, как все твои проблемы сгорают дотла / улетучиваются.
- Буквально: «Это звук того, как все твои неприятности уходят в дым». Идиома _go up in smoke_ — сгореть, пойти прахом, исчезнуть.
