# hereby
?
# Transcription:
- BrE /ˌhɪəˈbaɪ/; AmE /ˌhɪrˈbaɪ/
# Meaning:
- By this statement, document, or action; as a result of this.
- Used in formal or legal writing to indicate that something is being declared, agreed, or done at this moment.
# Usage:
- Formal and legal. Common in official documents, contracts, oaths, and proclamations: I hereby declare..., You are hereby notified..., This is hereby approved.
- Not used in everyday conversation.
# Example:
- _I hereby declare the meeting open._
- _You are hereby notified that your contract has been terminated._
# Russian:
- Настоящим; сим; этим; в силу этого.
- Формальное/юридическое слово, часто переводится как «настоящим»: _I hereby declare..._ — настоящим заявляю...; _You are hereby notified..._ — настоящим вы уведомляетесь...
