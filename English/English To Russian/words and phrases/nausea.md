# nausea
?
# Transcription:
- BrE /ˈnɔːziə/; AmE /ˈnɔːʒə/
# Meaning:
- The feeling that you are going to vomit; sickness in the stomach.
- A strong feeling of disgust or revulsion.
# Usage:
- Uncountable noun. Common patterns: a wave of nausea, feel nausea, experience nausea, nausea and vomiting.
- Neutral and common in medical, health, and everyday contexts.
# Example:
- _A wave of nausea washed over her._
- _He felt nausea after the rough boat ride._
- _The smell filled me with nausea._
# Russian:
- Тошнота, дурнота; чувство тошноты.
- Отвращение, омерзение. Не путать с _nauseous_ — чувствующий тошноту или вызывающий тошноту (в зависимости от контекста).
