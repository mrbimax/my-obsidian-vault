# irreplaceable
?
# Transcription:
- BrE /ˌɪrɪˈpleɪsəbl/; AmE /ˌɪrɪˈpleɪsəbl/
# Meaning:
- Impossible to replace; too valuable, special, or unique to be replaced by someone or something else.
- Having a quality or role that cannot be duplicated.
# Usage:
- Common before nouns or after linking verbs: an irreplaceable loss, an irreplaceable friend, she is irreplaceable.
- Neutral to formal. Often used about people, experiences, objects, or contributions that are unique and cannot be substituted.
# Example:
- _Her knowledge and experience make her irreplaceable._
- _The vase was old and irreplaceable._
- _He was an irreplaceable part of the team._
# Russian:
- Незаменимый; неповторимый, уникальный.
- О человеке или вещи, которую невозможно заменить или восполнить. _Irreplaceable loss_ — невосполнимая утрата.
