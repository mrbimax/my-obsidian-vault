# to recite
?
# Transcription:
- /tə rɪˈsaɪt/
# Meaning:
- To say a poem, passage, or list aloud from memory, especially in front of an audience.
- To repeat something aloud, often formally or as part of a performance or lesson.
# Usage:
- Common patterns: recite a poem/prayer/speech, recite something from memory, recite to someone.
- Neutral to formal. Used in school, religious, theatrical, and ceremonial contexts.
# Example:
- _She recited the poem perfectly in front of the class._
- _He can recite the entire speech from memory._
# Russian:
- Декламировать, читать наизусть; повторять вслух.
- О стихах, молитвах, отрывках или списках, которые произносят по памяти или вслух перед другими.
