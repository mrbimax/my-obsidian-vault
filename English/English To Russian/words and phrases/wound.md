# wound
?
# Transcription:
- /wuːnd/ (injury)
- /waʊnd/ (past tense of wind)
# Meaning:
- An injury to the body, especially one made by a weapon or sharp object.
- To injure someone or something, especially with a weapon; to hurt someone's feelings.
# Usage:
- As a noun: a deep wound, gunshot wound, open wound.
- As a verb: wound someone, wounded in battle; figuratively wound someone's pride.
- Not to confuse with the past tense of "wind", which is also written "wound" but pronounced /waʊnd/.
# Example:
- _The soldier had a deep wound in his leg._
- _His remarks wounded her deeply._
# Russian:
- Рана; ранение; ранить, наносить рану.
- Не путать с _wound_ /waʊnd/ — прошедшая форма от _wind_ (наматывать, виться).
