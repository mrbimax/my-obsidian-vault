# janitor
?
# Transcription:
- BrE /ˈdʒænɪtə(r)/; AmE /ˈdʒænɪtər/
# Meaning:
- A person employed to clean and maintain a building, especially a school, office, or apartment block.
- In American English, a cleaner or custodian; in British English, sometimes a caretaker, doorkeeper, or building superintendent.
# Usage:
- Common in American English for someone who cleans and does minor repairs in a building. In British English, "caretaker" or "cleaner" is often more common for this role.
- Neutral. Can be used for schools, offices, hospitals, and residential buildings.
# Example:
- _The janitor mopped the hallway after school._
- _We asked the janitor to fix the broken light in the lobby._
