# so to speak
?
# Transcription:
- /ˌsəʊ tə ˈspiːk/
# Meaning:
- Used to show that a word or phrase is being used figuratively, loosely, or not literally.
- Used to soften or comment on an unusual, exaggerated, or metaphorical way of speaking.
# Usage:
- Common in conversation and writing, often after a metaphor, idiom, or exaggeration. Usually parenthetical and informal to neutral.
# Example:
- _He’s the captain of the team, so to speak._
- _My career is on life support, so to speak._
# Russian:
- Так сказать; если можно так выразиться; в некотором смысле.
- Оборот указывает, что выражение употреблено не буквально, а образно или приблизительно.
