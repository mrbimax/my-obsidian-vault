# coat hangers
?
# Transcription:
- /ˈkoʊt ˌhæŋərz/ (AmE), /ˈkəʊt ˌhæŋəz/ (BrE)
# Meaning:
- Plural of "coat hanger": devices used for hanging coats and other clothes.
- Shaped supports with a hook, usually made of wood, plastic, or wire.
# Usage:
- Common in everyday and household contexts. Often shortened to "hangers" in conversation.
- In British English, "coat hangers" and "clothes hangers" are both used.
# Example:
- _She bought a pack of wooden coat hangers._
- _The closet was full of wire coat hangers._
# Russian:
- Плечики; вешалки для одежды.
- Обычно для пальто, пиджаков и другой верхней одежды. В разговорной речи часто просто _hangers_.
