# bruise
?
# Transcription:
- /bruːz/
# Meaning:
- (Noun) An injury that appears as a discolored area on the skin, caused by broken blood vessels under the surface.
- (Verb) To cause a bruise on someone or something; to hurt or damage.
# Usage:
- Common as both noun and verb. As a verb, often used with an object: she bruised her arm; he was badly bruised. Also used figuratively for hurt feelings or damaged reputation, though less common.
# Example:
- _She had a large bruise on her leg after the fall._
- _He bruised his knee playing football._
# Russian:
