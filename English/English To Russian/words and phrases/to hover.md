# to hover
?
# Transcription:
- /tə ˈhɒvə(r)/ (BrE), /tə ˈhʌvər/ (AmE)
# Meaning:
- To stay in one place in the air, especially by flapping wings or using rotors.
- To wait or stay near someone or something, often nervously or uncertainly.
- To move the cursor over an item on a screen without clicking.
# Usage:
- Common patterns: hover over/above/around, hover in the air, hover over a link.
- Neutral and common in everyday, aviation, technology, and behavioral contexts.
# Example:
- _The hummingbird hovered near the flower._
- _He hovered around the door, unsure whether to knock._
- _Hover over the icon to see more options._
# Russian:
- Парить, зависать, нависать; колебаться, топтаться рядом.
- В компьютере: наводить курсор, задерживать курсор над элементом. _Hover over_ — навести курсор на.
