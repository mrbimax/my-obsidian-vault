# clash
?
# Transcription:
- /klæʃ/
# Meaning:
- A fight, argument, or serious disagreement between people or groups.
- A loud sound made by metal objects hitting each other.
- (Of colors, styles, events, etc.) To look bad together or to conflict.
# Usage:
- As a noun: a clash between fans, a clash of opinions, a clash of colors.
- As a verb: the two teams clashed, the colors clash, the meeting clashes with my flight.
# Example:
- _There was a violent clash between police and protesters._
- _The two colors clash badly._
- _My appointment clashes with the conference._
# Russian:
- Столкновение, конфликт, перепалка; лязг, грохот.
- Как глагол: сталкиваться, конфликтовать; не сочетаться (о цветах); совпадать по времени (и мешать).
