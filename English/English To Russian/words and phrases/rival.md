# rival
?
# Transcription:
- /ˈraɪvl/
# Meaning:
- A person, team, company, or thing that competes with another for the same goal or prize.
- Someone or something that tries to equal or surpass another.
# Usage:
- As a noun: a rival for something, a rival to someone, a political rival, an arch-rival.
- As an adjective before a noun: a rival company, a rival team, rival claims.
- As a verb: to rival something means to be as good as or comparable to it: few can rival her talent.
# Example:
- _The two companies are fierce rivals in the smartphone market._
- _She has no rival in the sport._
- _Their new product rivals the best on the market._
# Russian:
- Соперник, конкурент; противник.
- Как прилагательное: конкурирующий, соперничающий.
- Как глагол: соперничать, не уступать, равняться. _Arch-rival_ — главный, заклятый соперник.
