# treasure
?
# Transcription:
- /ˈtreʒər/
# Meaning:
- (Noun) Valuable things such as gold, jewels, or money; a collection of precious objects.
- (Noun) Something that is very valuable or cherished, not necessarily material.
- (Verb) To cherish or value someone or something greatly.
# Usage:
- Noun: common for literal treasure (buried treasure, pirate treasure) and figurative (a treasure of information, national treasure).
- Verb: formal or emotional; treasure a memory, treasure someone's friendship.
- Neutral; can be literal or emotional.
# Example:
- _The pirates buried their treasure on a deserted island._
- _This old photograph is a real treasure to me._
- _I treasure the time we spent together._
# Russian:
- Сокровище, клад; ценные вещи.
- Дорожить, ценить, беречь (как глагол).
- Может означать как материальные ценности, так и что-то очень дорогое сердцу. _National treasure_ — национальное достояние.
