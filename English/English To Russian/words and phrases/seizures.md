# seizures
?
# Transcription:
- /ˈsiːʒərz/
# Meaning:
- Sudden attacks of illness, especially those caused by abnormal electrical activity in the brain, often with convulsions or loss of consciousness.
- Sudden acts of taking control of something by force or legal authority; confiscations.
# Usage:
- Medical sense is common in plural: he has seizures, epileptic seizures, febrile seizures.
- Legal/political sense: seizures of property, drug seizures, seizures by customs.
- Neutral to formal. Singular is seizure.
# Example:
- _She was diagnosed with epilepsy after having several seizures._
- _The police reported a record number of drug seizures last year._
# Russian:
- Приступы, судороги; эпилептические приступы.
- Изъятия, конфискации; захваты (имущества, груза). Единственное число: seizure.
