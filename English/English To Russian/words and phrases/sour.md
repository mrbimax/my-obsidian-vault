# sour
?
# Transcription:
- /ˈsaʊər/ (BrE), /ˈsaʊər/ (AmE)
# Meaning:
- Having a sharp, acid taste like lemon or vinegar.
- (Of a situation, mood, or relationship) unpleasant, bitter, or unfriendly.
- Past tense and past participle of "sear"? No: "sour" can also be a verb meaning to become or make sour.
# Usage:
- Common for food and drinks: sour milk, sour taste, sour cherries.
- Figurative use: a sour face, a sour mood, the relationship turned sour.
# Example:
- _The milk has gone sour._
- _Their friendship turned sour after the argument._
# Russian:
- Кислый; прокисший.
- В переносном смысле: испортившийся, мрачный, неприязненный. _Turn sour_ — испортиться, стать напряжённым.
