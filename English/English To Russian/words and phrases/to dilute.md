# to dilute
?
# Transcription:
- /tə daɪˈluːt/
# Meaning:
- To make a liquid weaker or less concentrated by adding water or another liquid.
- To reduce the strength, force, value, or effect of something.
# Usage:
- Common patterns: dilute something with something, dilute something in something, dilute the effect/impact/power.
- Used literally for drinks, chemicals, and solutions; figuratively for ideas, policies, or influence.
# Example:
- _Dilute the juice with a little water before giving it to the child._
- _The new regulations diluted the original purpose of the law._
# Russian:
- Разбавлять, разводить; ослаблять, снижать.
- В прямом смысле: делать раствор менее концентрированным. В переносном: ослаблять эффект, силу или значение чего-либо.
