# Transcription:
- /ˈvaʊəl/
# Meaning:
- A speech sound produced when the breath flows out through the mouth without being blocked by the teeth, tongue, or lips.
- A letter that represents such a sound, especially a, e, i, o, u, and sometimes y.
# Usage:
- Common in language learning, phonetics, and grammar. Contrasted with "consonant".
- Used in phrases like vowel sound, vowel letter, short/long vowel, vowel shift.
# Example:
- _The word "cat" has a short vowel sound._
- _A, E, I, O, and U are vowels in English._
# Russian:
- Гласный (звук); гласная (буква).
- В английском к гласным обычно относят A, E, I, O, U, иногда Y. Не путать с _consonant_ — согласный.
