# cut the crap
?
# Transcription:
- /kʌt ðə kræp/
# Meaning:
- Stop talking nonsense, making excuses, or being evasive; get to the point.
- Used to tell someone to stop saying things that are not true, relevant, or useful.
# Usage:
- Informal, mildly vulgar, and often rude. Common in arguments or when someone wants the real facts.
- Variants: cut the shit (vulgar), cut the bullshit (vulgar).
# Example:
- _Cut the crap and tell me what really happened._
- _We don't have time for excuses, so cut the crap._
# Russian:
- Хватит нести чушь; прекрати эту ерунду; кончай базар; говори по делу.
- Буквально: «прекрати [говорить] дерьмо/ерунду». _Crap_ — грубоватое, но не самое матерное слово.
