# revulsion
?
# Transcription:
- /rɪˈvʌlʃn/
# Meaning:
- A very strong feeling of disgust, horror, or dislike.
- A sudden violent reaction against something offensive or repulsive.
# Usage:
- Often followed by "at", "against", "toward", or "to": revulsion at the sight, revulsion against violence.
- Neutral to formal. Stronger than simple dislike or disgust.
# Example:
- _She felt revulsion at the sight of the wound._
- _His cruelty filled me with revulsion._
- _There is growing public revulsion against the war._
# Russian:
- Отвращение, омерзение; резкое неприятие, отвращение с оттенком ужаса.
- Сильное чувство, часто вызванное чем-то отталкивающим или жестоким. Обычно с _at/against/toward_.
