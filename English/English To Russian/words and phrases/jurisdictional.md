# jurisdictional
?
# Transcription:
- /ˌdʒʊərɪsˈdɪkʃənl/
# Meaning:
- Relating to jurisdiction; concerning the official power or authority to make legal decisions and judgments.
- Relating to a particular area or sphere of legal authority.
# Usage:
- Formal legal term. Common in phrases like jurisdictional issue, jurisdictional dispute, jurisdictional boundaries, and jurisdictional authority.
# Example:
- _The court dismissed the case for jurisdictional reasons._
- _There was a jurisdictional dispute between the two states._
# Russian:
- Юрисдикционный; относящийся к юрисдикции, подсудности или подведомственности.
- Часто в сочетаниях: _jurisdictional dispute_ — юрисдикционный спор, спор о подсудности.
