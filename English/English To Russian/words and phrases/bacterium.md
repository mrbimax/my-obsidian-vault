# bacterium
?
# Transcription:
- /bækˈtɪəriəm/ (BrE), /bækˈtɪriəm/ (AmE)
# Meaning:
- A microscopic single-celled organism. Some bacteria cause disease, while many are harmless or beneficial.
- Singular form of "bacteria"; the plural is "bacteria".
# Usage:
- Technical and scientific. Used in biology, medicine, and microbiology. Common patterns: a bacterium causes/infects, a strain of bacterium, harmful/beneficial bacterium.
- In everyday speech, people often use "bacteria" for both singular and plural, but scientifically "bacterium" is singular.
# Example:
- _A single bacterium can multiply rapidly under the right conditions._
- _The bacterium responsible for tuberculosis is spread through the air._
# Russian:
- Бактерия; одноклеточный микроорганизм.
- В научном контексте _bacterium_ — единственное число, _bacteria_ — множественное. В быту часто говорят _bacteria_ и для одного микроорганизма.
