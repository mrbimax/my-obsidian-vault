# to write off
?
# Transcription:
- /tə raɪt ɒf/
# Meaning:
- To cancel a debt or remove an amount from accounts as a loss.
- To damage something, especially a vehicle, so badly that it is not worth repairing.
- To dismiss someone or something as unimportant, a failure, or not worth considering.
# Usage:
- Financial sense is common in accounting: write off a debt, write off bad loans. Often separable: write it off.
- Vehicle sense is usually passive: the car was written off.
- Dismissive sense is informal: write someone off as a lost cause.
# Example:
- _The company wrote off the unpaid invoices at the end of the year._
- _The car was written off in the crash._
- _Don't write him off just because he failed once._
