# to dent
?
# Transcription:
- /tə dent/
# Meaning:
- To make a hollow or depression in a surface by hitting or pressing it.
- To damage or weaken something, especially confidence, reputation, profits, or chances.
# Usage:
- Common patterns: dent something, dent one's confidence, dent profits, leave a dent in something.
- Neutral. Can describe physical damage to a car, door, or metal, or figurative damage to plans, pride, or finances.
# Example:
- _He dented the car door in the parking lot._
- _The scandal dented her reputation._
- _The delay dented our chances of winning._
# Russian:
- Вмять, помять, оставить вмятину; повредить, подорвать.
- В переносном смысле: нанести урон, пошатнуть (уверенность, репутацию, прибыль). _A dent_ — вмятина; _to make a dent in_ — заметно уменьшить или повредить.
