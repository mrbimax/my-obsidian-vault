# facility
?
# Transcription:
- /fəˈsɪləti/
# Meaning:
- A place, building, or piece of equipment provided for a particular purpose.
- An ability to do something easily or well; aptitude.
- (Plural: facilities) services, amenities, or features available at a place.
# Usage:
- Common patterns: a sports facility, a medical facility, educational facilities, facilities for disabled people, with facility (formal, meaning easily).
- Neutral and common in business, education, healthcare, and everyday contexts.
# Example:
- _The new sports facility includes a pool and a gym._
- _The hospital has modern facilities for cancer treatment._
- _She plays the piano with remarkable facility._
# Russian:
- Учреждение, объект, комплекс; оборудование, помещение.
- Facilities — удобства, инфраструктура, услуги.
- Способность, лёгкость (with facility — с лёгкостью, легко).
