# munitions vs ammunition(s)
?
# Transcription:
- munitions: /mjuːˈnɪʃənz/
- ammunitions: /ˌæmjəˈnɪʃənz/
# Meaning:
- Munitions: military weapons, ammunition, equipment, and supplies used in war. Usually plural and broader.
- Ammunitions: technically the plural of ammunition, but in standard English "ammunition" is uncountable and has no plural. "Ammunitions" is nonstandard or very rare.
# Usage:
- Use "munitions" for military supplies and weapons, often in formal or military contexts: munitions factory, munitions depot.
- Use "ammunition" (uncountable) for bullets, shells, etc.: ammunition for a gun. Do not normally say "ammunitions".
- If you need a plural, use "rounds of ammunition" or "types of ammunition", not "ammunitions".
# Example:
- _The army stored munitions at the depot._
- _The soldiers ran out of ammunition._
- _Not "ammunitions" — say "rounds of ammunition" or "ammunition supplies"._
