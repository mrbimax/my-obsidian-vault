# that is rich
?
# Transcription:
- /ðæt ɪz rɪtʃ/
# Meaning:
- Used sarcastically to say that someone's criticism or statement is hypocritical, ironic, or absurd, especially when it applies to the speaker themselves.
- Can also mean that something is hard to believe or ironically funny.
# Usage:
- Informal and sarcastic. Often used in response to an accusation, especially when the person making it is guilty of the same thing. Common variant: That's rich.
# Example:
- _You're calling me lazy? That is rich, coming from you._
- _He says I'm always late? That's rich, considering he missed the meeting twice._
# Russian:
- Саркастически: «Ну это вообще!», «Это надо же!», «Это ты мне говоришь?»
- Эквивалент для лицемерного упрёка: «Чья бы корова мычала...»
- Буквально «это богато» не используется; смысл — «это иронично/лицемерно».
