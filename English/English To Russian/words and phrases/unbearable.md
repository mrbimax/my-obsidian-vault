# unbearable
?
# Transcription:
- /ʌnˈbeərəbl/ (BrE), /ʌnˈberəbl/ (AmE)
# Meaning:
- Too unpleasant, painful, or difficult to accept or endure.
- Impossible to bear or tolerate.
# Usage:
- Describes pain, heat, noise, behavior, situations, or feelings that are too much to handle.
- Common patterns: unbearable pain, unbearable heat, become unbearable, find something unbearable.
# Example:
- _The heat in the room was unbearable._
- _The suspense became unbearable._
# Russian:
- Невыносимый, нестерпимый, непереносимый.
- О боли, жаре, шуме, поведении или ситуации, которые невозможно вытерпеть.
