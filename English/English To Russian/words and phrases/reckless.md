# reckless
?
# Transcription:
- /ˈrekləs/
# Meaning:
- Doing something dangerous or careless without thinking about the risks or consequences.
- Showing a complete lack of caution or concern for safety.
# Usage:
- Common before nouns or after linking verbs: a reckless driver, reckless behavior, he was reckless.
- Often used in legal and safety contexts: reckless driving, reckless disregard, reckless endangerment.
- Stronger and more negative than just "careless".
# Example:
- _He was arrested for reckless driving._
- _She made a reckless decision to quit her job without a plan._
# Russian:
- Безрассудный, опрометчивый, беспечный; неосторожный.
- О действиях или людях, которые не думают о риске и последствиях. В юридическом контексте: _reckless driving_ — опасное вождение; _reckless disregard_ — преступная небрежность.
