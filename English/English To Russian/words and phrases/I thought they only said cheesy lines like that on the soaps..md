# I thought they only said cheesy lines like that on the soaps.
?
# Transcription:
- /aɪ θɔːt ðeɪ ˈəʊnli sed ˈtʃiːzi laɪnz laɪk ðæt ɒn ðə səʊps/
# Meaning:
- Used to say that a romantic or dramatic line sounds like clichéd dialogue from a soap opera, not something people say in real life.
- "Cheesy lines" are corny, overly sentimental, or predictable remarks; "soaps" are soap operas.
# Usage:
- Informal and conversational, often mildly mocking or amused. Said when someone uses an overly romantic, dramatic, or clichéd phrase.
- "Soaps" is short for "soap operas" — TV serials known for melodramatic dialogue.
# Example:
- _He told her she was the missing piece of his soul. I thought they only said cheesy lines like that on the soaps._
- _When he said "You complete me," I thought they only said cheesy lines like that on the soaps._
# Russian:
- Я думал(а), такие слащавые/банальные фразы говорят только в сериалах.
- _Cheesy lines_ — слащавые, пошлые, банальные фразы; _soaps_ — мыльные оперы, сериалы. Смысл: это звучит как клише из телесериала, а не как реальная речь.
