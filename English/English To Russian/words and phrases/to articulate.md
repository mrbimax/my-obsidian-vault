# to articulate
?
# Transcription:
- /tə ɑːrˈtɪkjuleɪt/ (AmE), /tə ɑːˈtɪkjuleɪt/ (BrE)
# Meaning:
- To express thoughts, ideas, or feelings clearly in words.
- To pronounce something clearly and distinctly.
- (Technical) To form a joint or connect by joints.
# Usage:
- Common patterns: articulate an idea, articulate a vision, articulate one's thoughts, articulate clearly.
- Neutral to formal. Used for speaking, writing, and formal communication. Not to confuse with adjective "articulate" (умеющий ясно выражаться), which has stress on the second syllable: /ɑːrˈtɪkjələt/.
# Example:
- _She articulated her concerns calmly and clearly._
- _He struggled to articulate what he was feeling._
# Russian:

- (Техн.) Сочленять, соединять суставом.
