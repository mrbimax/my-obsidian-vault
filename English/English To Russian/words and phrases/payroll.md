# payroll
?
# Transcription:
- /ˈpeɪroʊl/
# Meaning:
- The list of employees of a company and the total amount of money paid to them.
- The total sum of money a company pays regularly to its employees.
- The department or process responsible for paying employees.
# Usage:
- Common in business, finance, and HR contexts. Often used in phrases like "on the payroll", "payroll department", "payroll tax", "process payroll".
# Example:
- _The company has a payroll of over 500 employees._
- _She works in payroll and handles salaries every month._
- _We need to cut payroll costs to stay profitable._
# Russian:
- Платёжная ведомость; фонд заработной платы; расчёт зарплаты.
- Может также означать «отдел расчёта зарплаты» или «общая сумма выплат сотрудникам».
