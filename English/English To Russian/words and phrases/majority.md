# majority
?
# Transcription:
- BrE /məˈdʒɒrəti/; AmE /məˈdʒɔːrəti/
# Meaning:
- The greater number or part of a group; more than half.
- In voting, the number by which the winning side exceeds the losing side, or the winning candidate's excess over the runner-up.
- The age at which a person is legally considered an adult.
# Usage:
- Common in politics, voting, law, and general statements: the majority of people, a majority vote, win by a majority, reach the age of majority.
- Can be followed by "of" + plural noun: the majority of students. Verb can be singular or plural depending on emphasis.
# Example:
- _The majority of students passed the exam._
- _She won the election by a large majority._
- _He will inherit the money when he reaches the age of majority._
