# wazoo
?
# Transcription:
- /wəˈzuː/
# Meaning:
- (Slang) Buttocks or anus; often used in a humorous or vulgar way.
- Chiefly in "up the wazoo" or "out the wazoo": in large amounts; to an excessive degree.
# Usage:
- Informal, slang, often humorous or slightly vulgar. Most common in phrases: up the wazoo, out the wazoo. Not for formal writing.
# Example:
- _I've got deadlines up the wazoo this week._
- _He has money out the wazoo but still complains._
# Russian:
- (Сленг) задница, попа.
- В выражениях _up/out the wazoo_ — завались, полно, хоть отбавляй; в огромном количестве, сверх меры. Неформально, часто с юмором или грубовато.
