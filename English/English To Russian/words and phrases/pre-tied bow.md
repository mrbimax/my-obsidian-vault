# pre-tied bow
?
# Transcription:
- /ˌpriː ˈtaɪd boʊ/
# Meaning:
- A bow that is already tied and ready to use.
- Often used for hair, gifts, clothing, or bow ties.
# Usage:
- Common in fashion, hair accessories, gift wrapping, and formalwear.
- Usually singular; plural is pre-tied bows.
# Example:
- _She fixed a pre-tied bow to the gift._
- _He wore a pre-tied bow tie to the wedding._
# Russian:
- Готовый, уже завязанный бант.
- _Pre-tied bow tie_ — готовый галстук-бабочка.
