# puncture
?
# Transcription:
- BrE /ˈpʌŋktʃə(r)/; AmE /ˈpʌŋktʃər/
# Meaning:
- (Noun) A small hole made by a sharp object, especially in a tire, skin, or container.
- (Noun, BrE) A flat tire caused by such a hole.
- (Verb) To make a hole in something with a sharp object; to pierce or deflate.
# Usage:
- Common for tires, skin, and containers. In British English, "a puncture" often means a flat tire; American English usually says "a flat".
- Medical contexts: lumbar puncture, puncture wound.
# Example:
- _I got a puncture on the way to work and had to change the tire._
- _The nurse cleaned the puncture wound on his arm._
- _A sharp nail punctured the tire._
