# Nice of you.
?
# Transcription:
- /naɪs əv juː/
# Meaning:
- A polite expression used to thank someone or acknowledge that they have been kind.
- Often part of a longer phrase: That's nice of you, It's nice of you to..., Nice of you to say.
# Usage:
- Informal but polite and common in everyday speech. Used when someone does or says something kind.
- Can be sincere or, depending on tone, slightly sarcastic, but usually sincere.
# Example:
- _That's nice of you — thank you for helping._
- _It was nice of you to check on me._
- _Nice of you to say, but I don't think I deserve it._
# Russian:
- Мило с вашей стороны; очень любезно с вашей стороны.
- В зависимости от контекста: «как мило с твоей стороны», «спасибо, это так мило». _It's nice of you to..._ — мило с вашей стороны, что...
