# persuasive
?
# Transcription:
- /pərˈsweɪsɪv/
# Meaning:
- Good at persuading someone to do or believe something.
- Able to convince people through reasoning or argument.
# Usage:
- Used to describe a person, speech, argument, or manner that convinces others.
- Common patterns: a persuasive argument, a persuasive speaker, persuasive evidence.
# Example:
- _She made a persuasive case for changing the policy._
- _His persuasive manner helped him close the deal._
# Russian:
- Убедительный, умеющий убеждать.
- Описывает человека, речь или аргумент, которые способны убедить. Не путать с _persuaded_ — убеждённый.
