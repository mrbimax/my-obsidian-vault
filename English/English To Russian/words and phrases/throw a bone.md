# throw a bone
?
# Transcription:
- /θroʊ ə boʊn/
# Meaning:
- To give someone a small concession, favor, or piece of information to appease them or keep them satisfied.
- Literal: to throw a bone to a dog.
# Usage:
- Informal. Common pattern: throw someone a bone. Often implies the giver does not want to give much, just enough to stop complaints or keep someone interested.
# Example:
- _The company threw the workers a bone with a small bonus._
- _After months of protests, the government threw them a bone by promising a review._
# Russian:
- Бросить кость; сделать мелкую уступку, дать подачку.
- Буквально: бросить кость собаке. В переносном смысле — уступить чуть-чуть, чтобы успокоить или отвлечь.
