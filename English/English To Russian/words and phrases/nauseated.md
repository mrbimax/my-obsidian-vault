# nauseated
?
# Transcription:
- /ˈnɔːzieɪtɪd/ (BrE), /ˈnɔːzieɪtɪd/ (AmE)
# Meaning:
- Feeling nausea; wanting to vomit.
- Feeling disgust or strong revulsion.
- Past tense and past participle of "nauseate": to make someone feel sick or disgusted.
# Usage:
- Traditionally, "nauseated" means feeling sick, while "nauseous" means causing sickness. In modern informal use, "nauseous" is also used for feeling sick.
- Common patterns: feel nauseated, become nauseated, nauseated by something.
# Example:
- _She felt nauseated after the rough boat ride._
- _I was nauseated by the smell._
- _He was nauseated by the cruelty of the scene._
# Russian:
- Испытывающий тошноту; чувствующий дурноту.
- Чувствующий отвращение, пресыщение. Причастие от _nauseate_ — вызывать тошноту или отвращение.
