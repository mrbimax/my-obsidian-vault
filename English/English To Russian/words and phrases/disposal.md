# disposal
?
# Transcription:
- /dɪˈspoʊzl/
# Meaning:
- The act or process of getting rid of something, especially waste.
- The power or opportunity to use something; availability.
# Usage:
- Common in environmental, business, and formal contexts. Often in phrases like waste disposal, safe disposal, at someone's disposal.
# Example:
- _The safe disposal of hazardous waste is strictly regulated._
- _I have a car at my disposal for the weekend._
# Russian:
- Утилизация, удаление, избавление; ликвидация.
- _At someone's disposal_ — в чьём-либо распоряжении.
