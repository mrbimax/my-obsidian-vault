# stockholder
?
# Transcription:
- /ˈstɒkhoʊldər/ (AmE), /ˈstɒkhəʊldə(r)/ (BrE)
# Meaning:
- A person or organization that owns shares in a company.
- Another word for shareholder, especially in American English.
# Usage:
- Common in business, finance, and corporate law. Often used in phrases like major stockholder, majority stockholder, stockholders' meeting.
- In British English, "shareholder" is more common; "stockholder" is more typical of American English.
# Example:
- _The company's largest stockholder opposed the merger._
- _Stockholders will vote on the proposal next month._
# Russian:
- Акционер; держатель акций; пайщик.
- В британском английском чаще _shareholder_; в американском — _stockholder_.
