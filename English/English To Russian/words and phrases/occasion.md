# occasion
?
# Transcription:
- /əˈkeɪʒn/
# Meaning:
- A particular time or instance when something happens.
- A special or important event, ceremony, or celebration.
# Usage:
- Common in phrases: on occasion (sometimes), on this/that occasion, a special occasion, mark the occasion.
- Neutral and fairly formal. Can mean a single event or a suitable opportunity.
# Example:
- _I've met him on several occasions._
- _Her birthday was a happy occasion for the whole family._
- _We don't dress up often, but this is a special occasion._
# Russian:
- Случай, раз; событие, мероприятие; торжество.
- _On occasion_ — иногда, время от времени. _A special occasion_ — особый случай, торжество.
