# slags
?
# Transcription:
- /slæɡz/
# Meaning:
- Plural of "slag": (UK, slang, derogatory, offensive) women who are considered promiscuous or sexually immoral.
- Literal: pieces or masses of waste material from smelting (usually uncountable "slag").
# Usage:
- The derogatory sense is very offensive and should be avoided. It is mainly British English and is used as an insult.
# Example:
- _He was suspended for calling his colleagues slags._
- _The word "slags" is a derogatory insult and should not be used._
