# to bump
?
# Transcription:
- /tə bʌmp/
# Meaning:
- To hit or knock against someone or something, often accidentally.
- To move with a jolt or a series of knocks.
# Usage:
- Used when someone or something hits another thing, often not seriously. Common patterns: bump into/against something, bump one's head, bump along.
# Example:
- _I bumped my knee on the table._
- _The car bumped along the rough road._
# Russian:
- Удариться, стукнуться, натолкнуться; трястись, подпрыгивать (при движении).
- Не путать с фразовым глаголом _bump into_ — случайно встретить кого-либо.
