# to stir
?
# Transcription:
- /tə stɜːr/
# Meaning:
- To move a liquid or other substance around with a spoon, stick, etc., in order to mix it.
- To move slightly or cause something to move slightly.
- To affect someone's emotions, especially making them feel upset, excited, or motivated.
# Usage:
- Common patterns: stir something into something, stir something up, stir someone's emotions, stir interest.
- Can be literal (cooking) or figurative (emotions, reactions).
# Example:
- _She stirred the soup with a wooden spoon._
- _The news stirred up a lot of anger among the workers._
- _A gentle breeze stirred the leaves._
# Russian:
- Размешивать, помешивать; шевелить, двигать; волновать, возбуждать.
- _Stir up_ — разжигать, провоцировать (чувства, конфликт).
