# to plate
?
# Transcription:
- /tə pleɪt/
# Meaning:
- To cover a metal object with a thin layer of another metal, usually by electroplating.
- To arrange and serve food on a plate, especially in a restaurant or professional kitchen.
# Usage:
- For metal: often passive, as in gold-plated, silver-plated, nickel-plated.
- For food: transitive and common in culinary contexts: plate a dish, plate the fish, plate up (BrE).
- Neutral to technical. The food sense is neutral to professional.
# Example:
- _The earrings are plated with gold._
- _The chef plated the dish with great care._
- _He plated the fish and added a lemon wedge._
# Russian:
- Покрывать металлом; наносить гальваническое покрытие.
- Раскладывать еду по тарелкам; сервировать блюдо.
- В кулинарном контексте: _plating_ — выкладывание и оформление блюда на тарелке.
