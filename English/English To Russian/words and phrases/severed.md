# severed
?
# Transcription:
- BrE /ˈsevəd/; AmE /ˈsevərd/
# Meaning:
- Cut off or separated, especially by cutting.
- Broken off or completely ended, as in a relationship, connection, or tie.
- Past tense and past participle of "sever": to cut through or separate.
# Usage:
- Literal: a severed finger, a severed limb, a severed cable.
- Figurative: severed ties, severed relations, severed connections, severed links.
- Formal/neutral. Common in medical, legal, news, and relationship contexts.
# Example:
- _The accident left him with a severely severed artery._
- _The two countries severed diplomatic ties._
- _She severed all contact with her former colleagues._
# Russian:
- Отрезанный, отсечённый; разорванный, прерванный.
- В прямом смысле: отрезанная часть тела или предмет. В переносном: разорванные связи, отношения, контакты. Причастие от _sever_ — отрезать, разрывать.
