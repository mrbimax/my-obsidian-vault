# sedated
?
# Transcription:
- /sɪˈdeɪtɪd/
# Meaning:
- Calmed or made sleepy by a drug, especially a sedative.
- Under the effect of sedation; in a relaxed or unconscious state.
- Past tense and past participle of "sedate".
# Usage:
- Medical and formal. Common patterns: be sedated, heavily sedated, mildly sedated, sedated before surgery.
- Can describe a patient, animal, or person who has been given drugs to reduce anxiety or induce sleep.
# Example:
- _The patient was sedated before the operation._
- _She was heavily sedated and barely able to speak._
# Russian:
- Находящийся под седацией; под действием успокоительных или наркоза.
- Введённый в сонное или спокойное состояние с помощью лекарств. _Heavily sedated_ — под сильным действием седативных средств.
