# proposition
?
# Transcription:
- /ˌprɒpəˈzɪʃn/ (BrE), /ˌprɑːpəˈzɪʃn/ (AmE)
# Meaning:
- A suggested plan, offer, or idea, especially in business, politics, or negotiation.
- A statement or assertion that can be true or false; a matter to be considered.
- (Informal) An offer of sexual activity.
# Usage:
- Neutral and formal in most senses: put forward a proposition, a business proposition, a value proposition.
- In logic, a proposition is a statement that has a truth value.
- The sexual sense is informal and can be direct or euphemistic: make someone a proposition.
# Example:
- _The mayor put forward a proposition to build a new park._
- _The proposition that all men are created equal is central to the Declaration._
- _She rejected his proposition and left the bar._
