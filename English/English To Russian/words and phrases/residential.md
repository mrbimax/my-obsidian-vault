# residential
?
# Transcription:
- BrE /ˌrezɪˈdenʃl/; AmE /ˌrezɪˈdenʃl/
# Meaning:
- Relating to or designed for homes and the people who live in them, rather than for business or industry.
- (Of an area, building, or institution) consisting of or used as private homes.
# Usage:
- Common before nouns: residential area, residential building, residential street, residential care, residential property.
- Neutral and common in housing, real estate, and urban planning contexts. Contrasts with commercial or industrial.
# Example:
- _They live in a quiet residential area._
- _The city is building new residential complexes._
# Russian:
- Жилой; жилой район, жилая зона.
- Относится к жилью и проживанию людей, а не к коммерческой или промышленной застройке. _Residential area_ — жилой район; _residential building_ — жилой дом.
