# prosecutor
?
# Transcription:
- BrE /ˈprɒsɪkjuːtər/; AmE /ˈprɑːsɪkjuːtər/
# Meaning:
- A lawyer who represents the government or state in a criminal case and tries to prove the accused is guilty.
- The party who brings a criminal charge against someone in court.
# Usage:
- Formal legal term. Common in criminal law, news, and court contexts. In the US, often called a district attorney or state's attorney; in the UK, a Crown Prosecutor.
# Example:
- _The prosecutor presented the evidence to the jury._
- _The prosecutor charged him with fraud._
# Russian:
- Прокурор; обвинитель.
- В США также _district attorney_, _state's attorney_; в Великобритании _Crown Prosecutor_. Представитель обвинения в суде.
