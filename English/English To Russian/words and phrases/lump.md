# lump
?
# Transcription:
- /lʌmp/
# Meaning:
- A compact, solid mass of something, often without a definite shape.
- A swelling or bump on the body.
- A small cube of sugar or a chunk of food.
# Usage:
- Common for pieces of sugar, coal, clay, or food. Also used for a swelling on the body, and in the phrase "a lump in one's throat" (tightness from emotion).
# Example:
- _He put a lump of sugar in his coffee._
- _She found a small lump on her neck and went to the doctor._
# Russian:
- Кусок, комок; глыба; шишка, опухоль.
- В зависимости от контекста: кусок сахара, комок глины, шишка на теле.
