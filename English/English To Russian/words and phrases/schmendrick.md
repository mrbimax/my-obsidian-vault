# schmendrick
?
# Transcription:
- /ˈʃmɛndrɪk/
# Meaning:
- (Yiddish slang) A foolish, bumbling, or incompetent person; a hapless nobody.
- Often implies weakness, immaturity, or being out of one's depth rather than outright evil.
# Usage:
- Informal and mildly derogatory, often used with a tone of affectionate contempt or exasperation. Common in Jewish-American English. Variants: shmendrick, shmendrik.
# Example:
- _Some schmendrick from downstairs asked if you could turn down your stereo._
- _Jeff is managing the project? That schmendrick couldn't manage his own sock drawer._
# Russian:
- Шмендрик (идишск. сленг): никчёмный, бестолковый человек; недоумок; «горе-человек».
- Буквально: от имени персонажа-растяпы в пьесе А. Гольдфадена «Шмендрик, или Комическая свадьба» (1877). Мягче, чем «идиот»; ближе к «недотёпа» или «маменькин сынок».
