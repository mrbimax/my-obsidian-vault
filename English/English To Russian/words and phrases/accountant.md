# accountant
?
# Transcription:
- /əˈkaʊntənt/
# Meaning:
- A person whose job is to keep, check, or prepare financial records.
- A professional trained to handle accounts, taxes, and financial reports.
# Usage:
- Used for someone who works professionally with financial records, taxes, and accounts, often for a company or individual.
# Example:
- _She hired an accountant to prepare her tax return._
- _The company's accountant noticed several unusual transactions._
# Russian:
- Бухгалтер; специалист по бухгалтерскому учёту.
- В некоторых контекстах может переводиться как аудитор, если речь именно о проверке финансовой отчётности.
