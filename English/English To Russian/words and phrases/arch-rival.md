# arch-rival
?
# Transcription:
- /ˌɑːrtʃ ˈraɪvl/ (AmE), /ˌɑːtʃ ˈraɪvl/ (BrE)
# Meaning:
- A chief or main rival; the most important and long-standing opponent.
- Someone or something that competes with another person, team, company, or country more than any other.
# Usage:
- Common before a noun or as a noun: arch-rival team, political arch-rival, his arch-rival.
- Neutral to formal. Often used for sports teams, companies, political figures, and historical enemies. Also written without a hyphen: archrival.
# Example:
- _The two companies have been arch-rivals for decades._
- _She beat her arch-rival in the final._
# Russian:
- Главный соперник; заклятый враг; давний конкурент.
- _Arch-_ здесь усиливает значение: не просто соперник, а самый главный и постоянный. В спорте и бизнесе часто переводится как «принципиальный соперник».
