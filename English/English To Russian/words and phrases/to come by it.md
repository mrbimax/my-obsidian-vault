# to come by it
?
# Transcription:
- /tə kʌm baɪ ɪt/
# Meaning:
- To obtain or acquire something, often by chance, effort, or honest means.
- "Come by" here means to get or come into possession of; "it" refers to the thing already mentioned.
# Usage:
- Common in questions about how something was obtained: How did you come by it?
- Also used in statements: I came by it honestly. Not the same as "come by" meaning to visit.
# Example:
- _How did you come by it? I came by it at a flea market._
- _She came by the information by accident._
# Russian:
- Достать это, раздобыть это, приобрести это, заполучить это.
- В контексте честного приобретения: получить это честным путём. Не путать с _come by_ — зайти, заглянуть в гости.
