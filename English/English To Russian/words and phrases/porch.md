# porch
?
# Transcription:
- BrE /pɔːtʃ/; AmE /pɔːrtʃ/
# Meaning:
- A covered shelter or platform at the entrance of a building, often with a roof and sometimes steps.
- (Especially AmE) A covered area attached to the front or back of a house, used for sitting outside.
# Usage:
- Common for houses and buildings: front porch, back porch, sit on the porch, porch swing.
- In American English, often a comfortable outdoor sitting area; in British English, usually a small covered entrance.
# Example:
- _We sat on the porch and watched the sunset._
- _She left the package on the front porch._
# Russian:
- Крыльцо; веранда; терраса (с навесом).
- В американском английском часто означает крытую площадку у дома для отдыха; в британском — обычно небольшой навес над входом.
