# bespoke
?
# Transcription:
- BrE /bɪˈspəʊk/; AmE /bɪˈspoʊk/
# Meaning:
- Made to order or custom-made, especially of clothing; designed and produced for a particular person or purpose.
- (Of software, services, or solutions) Custom-designed to meet specific needs.
# Usage:
- Chiefly British. Common for tailored suits, shoes, furniture, and custom software or services. In American English, "custom-made" or "custom" is more common.
# Example:
- _He wore a bespoke suit that fitted him perfectly._
- _The company builds bespoke software for its clients._
- _They offer bespoke furniture designed to your specifications._
# Russian:
- Сшитый на заказ; сделанный по индивидуальному заказу; кастомный, индивидуальный.
- О вещах, одежде, программном обеспечении или услугах, созданных под конкретного клиента или задачу. В американском английском чаще _custom-made_ или _custom_.
