# proximity
?
# Transcription:
- BrE /prɒkˈsɪməti/; AmE /prɑːkˈsɪməti/
# Meaning:
- Nearness in space, time, or relationship.
- The state of being close to someone or something.
# Usage:
- Formal and common in phrases like in close proximity to, proximity to something, proximity of something.
- Neutral to formal. Used in descriptions of location, distance, time, and relationships.
# Example:
- _The hotel is in close proximity to the airport._
- _The proximity of the two events made people suspicious._
# Russian:
- Близость, близкое расположение; соседство.
- О расстоянии, времени или отношениях. _In close proximity to_ — в непосредственной близости от.
