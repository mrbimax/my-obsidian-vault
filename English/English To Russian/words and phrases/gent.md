# gent
?
# Transcription:
- /dʒent/
# Meaning:
- (Informal) A gentleman; a man, especially a polite, decent, or well-dressed one.
- Short form of "gentleman"; plural "gents" can also mean a men's public toilet in British English.
# Usage:
- Informal, sometimes dated or humorous. Common in British English: a nice old gent, the gents.
- On signs, "Gents" often means "Men" or "Men's room".
# Example:
- _A nice old gent helped me carry my bags._
- _He went to the gents before the film started._
# Russian:
- Разг. «мужик», «господин», «джентльмен».
- В британском английском _the gents_ также означает мужской туалет.
