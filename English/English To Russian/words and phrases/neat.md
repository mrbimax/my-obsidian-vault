# neat
?
# Transcription:
- /niːt/
# Meaning:
- Arranged in a tidy, orderly way; clean and organized.
- (Informal) Good, pleasant, clever, or impressive.
- (Of alcohol) Not diluted; straight.
# Usage:
- Common for rooms, clothes, handwriting, and solutions: a neat room, neat handwriting, a neat solution.
- Informal for something cool or clever: a neat idea, that's neat.
- For drinks, usually in the phrase "neat" meaning without ice or mixer: a whisky, neat.
# Example:
- _Her desk is always neat and tidy._
- _That's a neat trick — how did you do it?_
- _He drinks his whisky neat._
# Russian:
- Аккуратный, опрятный, чистый; организованный.
- Разг.: классный, отличный, здорово; остроумный.
- О спиртном: неразбавленный, чистый. _Neat_ может означать как порядок, так и одобрение.
