# an accountant feller
?
# Transcription:
- /əˈkaʊntənt ˈfelər/
# Meaning:
- An informal, dialectal way of saying "accountant fellow" or "accountant guy": a man who works as an accountant.
- "Feller" is a variant of "fellow" meaning man, often associated with rustic, old-fashioned, or regional speech.
# Usage:
- Very informal and non-standard. Common in fictional dialogue or rural/older speech. Prefer "accountant" or "accountant guy" in neutral English.
# Example:
- _I asked the accountant feller down the road to sort out my taxes._
- _The accountant feller said I owed more than I thought._
# Russian:
- Бухгалтер-мужик; бухгалтер, парень (разг., простореч.).
- "Feller" — просторечный/диалектный вариант "fellow" (парень, мужик); в нейтральной речи просто "бухгалтер".
