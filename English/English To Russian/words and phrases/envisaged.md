# envisaged
?
# Transcription:
- /ɪnˈvɪzɪdʒd/
# Meaning:
- Imagined or conceived as a possibility or future event; foreseen or planned.
- Past tense and past participle of "envisage": to contemplate or visualize something in the future.
# Usage:
- Formal, common in British English. Often used in passive or as adjective: the envisaged changes, as envisaged, originally envisaged.
- American English prefers "envisioned".
# Example:
- _The project was completed as originally envisaged._
- _The envisaged benefits of the policy have not yet materialized._
# Russian:
- Предусмотренный, запланированный, предполагаемый; воображаемый, представляемый.
- Причастие от _envisage_ — представлять себе, предусматривать. В американском английском чаще _envisioned_.
