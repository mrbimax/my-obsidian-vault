# inwardly
?
# Transcription:
- /ˈɪnwərdli/ (AmE), /ˈɪnwədli/ (BrE)
# Meaning:
- In one's mind or feelings; privately, without expressing it outwardly.
- Toward the inside; internally.
# Usage:
- Common with verbs of feeling or reaction: inwardly sigh, inwardly groan, inwardly smile, inwardly seethe, inwardly rejoice.
- Often contrasts with outward behavior: calm on the outside, furious inwardly.
- Neutral and common in writing and speech.
# Example:
- _She smiled politely, but inwardly she was furious._
- _He inwardly groaned when he saw the pile of paperwork._
# Russian:
- Внутренне, про себя, в душе; мысленно.
- О чувствах или мыслях, которые не выражаются внешне. Часто противопоставляется внешнему поведению.
