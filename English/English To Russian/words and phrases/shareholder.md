# shareholder
?
# Transcription:
- BrE /ˈʃeəhəʊldə(r)/; AmE /ˈʃerhoʊldər/
# Meaning:
- A person or organization that owns shares in a company.
- Also called a stockholder, especially in American English.
# Usage:
- Common in business, finance, and corporate contexts. Often in phrases like major shareholder, minority shareholder, shareholders' meeting, shareholder value.
- Neutral to formal.
# Example:
- _The company's largest shareholder opposed the merger._
- _Shareholders will vote on the proposal next month._
# Russian:
- Акционер; держатель акций; пайщик.
- В американском английском чаще _stockholder_, в британском — _shareholder_.
