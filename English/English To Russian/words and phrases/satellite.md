# satellite
?
# Transcription:
- /ˈsætəlaɪt/
# Meaning:
- An object that orbits a planet or star; it can be natural, like a moon, or artificial, like a machine sent into space.
- A man-made device placed in orbit for communication, navigation, weather monitoring, or scientific research.
- A country or organization that is controlled by or dependent on a more powerful one.
# Usage:
- Common in space, technology, and political contexts. Often used in phrases like communications satellite, weather satellite, satellite dish, satellite state.
- Neutral and common. Can refer to natural moons, man-made devices, or dependent political entities.
# Example:
- _The moon is a natural satellite of Earth._
- _The company launched a new communications satellite._
- _During the Cold War, it was considered a satellite state._
# Russian:
- Спутник (космический; естественный или искусственный).
- Сателлит; зависимое государство. _Satellite dish_ — спутниковая тарелка; _satellite state_ — государство-сателлит.
