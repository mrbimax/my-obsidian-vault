# undifferentiated
?
# Transcription:
- /ˌʌndɪfəˈrenʃieɪtɪd/
# Meaning:
- Not divided or distinguished into different types, parts, or categories.
- Having no special structure or function; not yet developed into a specialized form.
- In biology and medicine: describing cells or tissue that have not yet become specialized.
# Usage:
- Formal and academic. Common in biology, medicine, and science: undifferentiated cells, undifferentiated tissue, undifferentiated mass.
- Can also be used more generally for things that are not clearly separated or distinguished.
# Example:
- _The tumor consisted of undifferentiated cells._
- _Stem cells are initially undifferentiated and can become many cell types._
# Russian:
- Недифференцированный; неразделённый, однородный; неспециализированный.
- В биологии и медицине: о клетках или тканях, которые ещё не приобрели специализированную функцию.
