# janitorial
?
# Transcription:
- /ˌdʒænɪˈtɔːriəl/
# Meaning:
- Relating to a janitor or to the work of cleaning and maintaining a building.
- Connected with cleaning, caretaking, and minor maintenance services.
# Usage:
- Common before nouns: janitorial staff, janitorial services, janitorial duties, janitorial work.
- Neutral to formal. Used mainly in business, facilities management, and building maintenance contexts.
# Example:
- _The company hired a janitorial service to clean the offices._
- _Janitorial staff are responsible for keeping the building clean and safe._
# Russian:
- Относящийся к уборке и техническому обслуживанию зданий; клининговый; уборочный.
- _Janitorial services_ — клининговые услуги; _janitorial staff_ — персонал по уборке и обслуживанию здания.
