# phrasing
?
# Transcription:
- /ˈfreɪzɪŋ/
# Meaning:
- The way in which something is expressed in words; wording.
- The act of putting something into words or dividing speech or music into phrases.
- In music: the way a melody or passage is divided and performed in phrases.
# Usage:
- Common in writing, speech, criticism, and music. Often used to comment on how something is worded: careful phrasing, awkward phrasing.
- Neutral. Can refer to spoken or written language, and to musical performance.
# Example:
- _The phrasing of the question was confusing._
- _Her phrasing of the melody was delicate and expressive._
# Russian:
- Формулировка, словесное выражение; фразировка.
- В музыке: фразировка, членение музыкальной фразы. Может означать как выбор слов, так и манеру исполнения.
