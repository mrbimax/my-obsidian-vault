# redundancy
?
# Transcription:
- /rɪˈdʌndənsi/
# Meaning:
- The state of being redundant; unnecessary repetition or duplication.
- (British English) Dismissal from a job because the employee is no longer needed.
- In engineering and technology, an intentional duplication of critical components to provide backup in case of failure.
# Usage:
- Common in language, business, employment, and technical contexts. In British English, "redundancy" often means layoff or job loss due to restructuring.
# Example:
- _The report was full of redundancy and could have been much shorter._
- _She received a redundancy payment after twenty years with the company._
- _The system uses redundancy to keep working if one part fails._
# Russian:
- Избыточность, дублирование; многословность.
- (Брит.) Сокращение, увольнение по сокращению штата.
- В технике: резервирование, дублирование для надёжности.
