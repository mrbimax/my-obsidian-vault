# booger
?
# Transcription:
- /ˈbʊɡər/ (AmE)
# Meaning:
- (Informal, chiefly US) A piece of dried mucus from inside the nose.
- A bogey; often used by or about children.
# Usage:
- Informal and slightly childish. Common in American English. Not polite in formal situations. Usually plural in everyday speech: pick your boogers.
# Example:
- _Don't pick your boogers at the table._
- _He wiped a booger on his sleeve._
# Russian:
- Козявка; засохшая сопля (в носу).
- Разговорно-детское, американский вариант. Не используется в вежливой или официальной речи.
