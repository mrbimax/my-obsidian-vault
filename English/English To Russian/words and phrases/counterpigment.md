# counterpigment
?
# Transcription:
- /ˈkaʊntərˌpɪɡmənt/
# Meaning:
- Not a standard English word in general dictionaries.
- If used, it likely means a pigment or color applied to counteract or neutralize another pigment or color, especially in art, cosmetics, or color correction.
# Usage:
- Rare or nonstandard; understood only as a technical or coined compound. In standard English, use "color corrector" or "neutralizing pigment" instead.
# Example:
- _In color correction, a green counterpigment can neutralize redness._
- _The artist mixed a counterpigment to tone down the orange._
